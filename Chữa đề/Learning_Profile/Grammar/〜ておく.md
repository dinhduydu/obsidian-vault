 `〜ておく` (Hành động chuẩn bị trước) ↔ `〜てある` (Trạng thái kết quả do ngoại động từ)

Grammar | 〜ておく

- **Meaning:** Do in advance, leave as is for later / Làm trước, chuẩn bị trước.
- **Trigger:** Taking an preparatory action in anticipation of a future need or event.
- **Contrast:** `〜ておく` indicates advance action by an agent, whereas `〜てある` describes the resulting state already prepared and visible.
- **One-line Summary:** Performing an action in advance for future convenience.
- **Formation:** V-te + おく (casual: 〜とく).
- **Semantic Field:** Advance Preparation / Chuẩn bị trước.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜てある`, `〜てしまう`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `〜てある` (`予約してある`) describes an existing state ("it is booked"), whereas making a new booking reservation in advance requires the active volitional preparation form `〜ておく`.
- **Why Correct:** `予約しておきます` actively expresses the intention to make a reservation in advance.
- **Example:** 予約しておきます。 (Tôi sẽ đặt trước.)
