Grammar | 〜ようにする

- **Meaning:** Try to, make an effort to / Cố gắng, nỗ lực để làm (hoặc không làm).
- **Trigger:** Describing a continuous personal effort or habit to achieve a target behavior.
- **Contrast:** `〜ようにする` indicates conscious personal effort/habit, while `〜ようになる` indicates an automatic or gradual change in state/ability.
- **One-line Summary:** Indicates a conscious habit or continuous effort to do something.
- **Formation:** V-ru / V-nai + ようにする (or ようにしている for ongoing habit).
- **Semantic Field:** Effort & Habit / Nỗ lực & Thói quen.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜ようになる`, `〜ことにする`.
- **Confusion Index:** High (frequently confused with `〜ようになる`).
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `ようになる` is incorrect because daily exercise requires conscious personal willpower (`ようにしている`), whereas `ようになる` denotes a natural change of state.
- **Why Correct:** `ようにしている` accurately describes the speaker's conscious daily commitment to exercise.
- **Example:** 毎日運動するようにしています。 (Tôi cố gắng để vận động mỗi ngày.)

Grammar | 〜ようにする

- Ý nghĩa: Cố gắng làm gì đó (thành thói quen).
- Cách dùng: V-ru/V-nai + ようにする.
- Ví dụ: 子供と一緒に考えるようにすれば... (Nếu cố gắng cùng suy nghĩ với trẻ...).
