Grammar | 〜までになる

- **Meaning:** Progress to the point of, reach the level where / Tiến triển đến mức, đạt đến trình độ.
- **Trigger:** Sequential career or skill growth markers like `パート` → `正社員` → `部門を任される`.
- **Contrast:** `〜までになる` (gradual progress culminating in a major capability) vs `〜たばかりだ` (just recently finished doing an action).
- **Semantic Field:** Progression & Extent / Tiến trình & Mức độ đạt được.
- **Confusion Index:** `〜までになる ↔ 〜たばかりだ`.
- **Why Wrong:** In Question 2, the user chose `任されたばかりだ` (just recently entrusted), overlooking the 4-year career progression from part-timer to full-time manager (`任されるまでになった`).
- **One-line Summary:** Indicates reaching an advanced state or capability through step-by-step progress over time.
