Grammar | 〜しかあるまい

- **Meaning:** Have no choice but to, must / Chắc chỉ còn cách, không còn lựa chọn nào khác ngoài.
- **Trigger:** Unfavorable constraints or dead-ends like `仕事の量を減らすのは難しく`, `完全否定されたら`.
- **Contrast:** `〜しかあるまい` (conjectural necessity: must have no choice but to...) vs `〜わけがない` (it's impossible that...).
- **Semantic Field:** Unavoidability & Conjecture / Lựa chọn duy nhất & Phán đoán.
- **Confusion Index:** `〜しかあるまい ↔ 〜わけがない`.
- **Why Wrong:** In Question 5, the user answered correctly (`工夫を重ねていくしかあるまい`). In Question 13, the user chose `わけがない` (impossible that...), failing to realize that if one's work is completely rejected by the boss, one has no choice but to quit (`辞めるしかあるまい`).
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses a conjectural conclusion that no other alternative or choice exists.
