Grammar | 〜ようになる

- **Meaning**: Trở nên, trở thành (chỉ sự thay đổi trạng thái).

Grammar | 〜ようになる

- **Meaning:** Come to be able to, reach a point where / Trở nên có thể, dần dần làm được.
- **Trigger:** Indicating a gradual change in ability, state, or habit over time.
- **Contrast:** `〜ようになる` denotes an acquired ability or state transition, while `〜ようにする` denotes intentional effort.
- **One-line Summary:** Describes a gradual transition into a new state or ability.
- **Formation:** V-potential / V-ru / V-nai + ようになる.
- **Semantic Field:** Change of State / Thay đổi trạng thái & Khả năng.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜ようにする`, `〜ことになる`.
- **Confusion Index:** High (frequently confused with `〜ようにする`).
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `ようにする` is incorrect when expressing acquired language capability, as acquiring fluency is a state change rather than a mere temporary action.
- **Why Correct:** `できるようになりました` properly uses `ようになる` with the potential verb form to indicate gained Japanese speaking ability.
- **Example:** 日本語が話せるようになりました。 (Tôi đã trở nên có thể nói tiếng Nhật.)
