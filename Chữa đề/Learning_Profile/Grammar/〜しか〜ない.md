Grammar | 〜しか〜ない

- **Meaning:** Have no choice but to, can only / Không còn cách nào khác, chỉ có thể...
- **Trigger:** Expressing that no other option or recourse exists except the named action.
- **Contrast:** `〜しか〜ない` highlights an unavoidable single remaining option, whereas `〜だけだ` expresses a neutral limitation ("only").
- **One-line Summary:** Indicates that no other alternative exists except the specified action.
- **Formation:** V-ru + しかない / N + しかない.
- **Semantic Field:** Single Choice & Unavoidability / Lựa chọn duy nhất & Bất khả kháng.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜よりほかない`, `〜ほかない`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using neutral `だけだ` lacks the strong feeling of resignation and lack of other alternatives conveyed by `しか〜ない`.
- **Why Correct:** `待つしかない` accurately expresses that waiting is the sole remaining course of action available.
- **Example:** 待つしかない。 (Chỉ còn cách chờ thôi.)
