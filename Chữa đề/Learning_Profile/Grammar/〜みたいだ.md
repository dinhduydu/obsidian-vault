Grammar | 〜みたいだ

- **Meaning:** Looks like, seems like / Có vẻ, trông như.
- **Trigger:** Subjective observation or resemblance based on visual appearance or similarity to a noun/verb.
- **Contrast:** `〜みたいだ` is casual/colloquial for resemblance and subjective judgment, whereas `〜らしい` relies on objective hearsay/evidence, and `〜そうだ` indicates immediate visual impression.
- **One-line Summary:** Colloquial expression for subjective resemblance or appearance.
- **Formation:** V-plain / Adj / N + みたいだ.
- **Semantic Field:** Conjecture & Resemblance / Phán đoán & Phỏng đoán dáng vẻ.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜らしい`, `〜そうだ（様態）`, `〜ようだ`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `〜そうだ（様態）` with a direct noun (`学生そうだ`) is ungrammatical; `〜そうだ` attaches to adjective stems or verb stems, whereas `〜みたいだ` directly attaches to nouns (`学生みたいだ`).
- **Why Correct:** `〜みたいだ` directly connects with the noun `学生` to express that he looks or acts like a student.
- **Example:** 彼は学生みたいだ。 (Anh ấy trông như là học sinh/sinh viên.)
