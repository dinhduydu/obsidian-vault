Grammar | 〜ために

- **Meaning:** Because of / In order to / Vì, do; Để.
- **Trigger:** Expressing an objective cause/reason for an event, or stating a goal/purpose.
- **Contrast:** `〜ために` indicates objective cause or goal, unlike `〜おかげで`/`〜せいで` which carry strong emotional credit or blame.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses an objective cause/reason or an intended purpose.
- **Formation:** V-ru + ために (purpose) / V-plain + ために; N + の + ために (cause/purpose).
- **Semantic Field:** Cause & Reason / Purpose / Nguyên nhân & Mục đích.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜おかげで`, `〜せいで`, `〜ように`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `ように` with a controllable volitional verb for purpose is mismatched compared to `ために` which connects directly with volitional goals or causes.
- **Why Correct:** `ために` neutrally states the objective reason (an accident) that caused the delay without emotional bias.
- **Example:** 事故のために遅れました。 (Vì tai nạn nên tôi đã đến muộn.)
