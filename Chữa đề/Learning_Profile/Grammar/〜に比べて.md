Grammar | 〜に比べて

- **Meaning:** Compared to / So với, so sánh với.
- **Trigger:** Contrasting two subjects or time periods to highlight a difference in degree, quantity, or quality.
- **Contrast:** `〜に比べて` explicitly compares two entities to show contrast, whereas `〜に対して` shows direct opposition or direction.
- **One-line Summary:** Compares two subjects to highlight a difference.
- **Formation:** N + に比べて; V-plain-nonpast + のに比べて.
- **Semantic Field:** Comparison & Contrast / So sánh & Đối chiếu.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜より`, `〜に対して`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `に対して` expresses contrasting opposites or direction, whereas comparing temperatures between this year and last year requires `に比べて`.
- **Why Correct:** `に比べて` properly sets last year as the baseline for comparing the temperature.
- **Example:** 去年に比べて暑い。 (So với năm ngoái thì nóng hơn.)
