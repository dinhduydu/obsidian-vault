Grammar | 〜わけではない

- **Meaning:** It does not mean that, not necessarily / Không hẳn là, không nhất thiết là.
- **Trigger:** Softening a statement or clarifying that a total assumption is not entirely true.
- **Contrast:** `〜わけではない` negates an absolute inference/assumption, whereas `〜ない` is a simple direct negation.
- **One-line Summary:** Softly negates a full assumption or absolute conclusion.
- **Formation:** V-plain / Adj-i / Adj-na + な / N + の・である + わけではない.
- **Semantic Field:** Partial Negation / Phủ định một phần & Giải thích làm mềm.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜わけだ`, `〜とは限らない`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using simple negation `嫌いではない` lacks the nuanced clarification that "it's not that I dislike it (though it might seem so)".
- **Why Correct:** `嫌いなわけではない` accurately clarifies that the speaker does not completely dislike it, avoiding a harsh or misunderstanding tone.
- **Example:** 嫌いなわけではない。 (Không phải là tôi không thích.)
