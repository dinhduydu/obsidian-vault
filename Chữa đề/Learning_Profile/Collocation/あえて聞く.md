Collocation | あえて聞く

- **Meaning:** To dare to ask, to venture to inquire (Chủ động hỏi dù biết có thể gây khó xử).
- **Trigger:** Asking sensitive questions or difficult topics deliberately (`触れにくい話題をあえて聞く`).
- **Contrast:** `あえて` (daringly/deliberately despite awkwardness) vs `遠慮する` (refrain out of politeness).
- **One-line Summary:** Collocation for courageously bringing up a sensitive or awkward question.
- **Usage:** 相手が言いづらいことや気まずい内容を、承知の上で意図的に質問する様子を表す。(Expresses intentionally asking awkward or delicate matters knowing the context.)
- **Example:** 気まずい雰囲気だったが、本心をあえて聞いてみた。(Though the atmosphere was awkward, I dared to ask their true feelings. / Bù lại bầu không khí khó xử, tôi đã chủ động hỏi về cảm xúc thật của họ.)

Collocation | あえて聞く

- **Meaning:** Dare to ask, intentionally ask despite awkwardness (Chủ động hỏi dù có thể khó xử).
- **Trigger:** Asking sensitive questions, bringing up awkward topics for clarity or resolution.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `わざと聞く` (asking maliciously/pretending not to know) or `遠慮して聞かない` (refraining from asking out of hesitation).
- **One-line Summary:** Intentionally raise a question despite knowing it might cause discomfort.
- **Usage:** `あえて` diễn tả hành động cố ý thực hiện một việc dù biết có rủi ro, khó xử hoặc không cần thiết, nhằm đạt mục đích tốt hơn.
- **Example:** 雰囲気が悪くなるのを承知で、気になっている問題をあえて聞いた。(Knowing it might spoil the mood, I dared to ask about the troubling issue.)
