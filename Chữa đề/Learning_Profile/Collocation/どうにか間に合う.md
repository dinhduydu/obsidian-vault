Collocation | どうにか間に合う

- **Meaning:** Barely make it in time, manage to be on time somehow (Xoay xở để kịp giờ).
- **Trigger:** Tight deadlines, rushing to catch trains/flights, last-minute arrival.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `なんとか乗り越える` (overcoming an obstacle/hardship) or `余裕で間に合う` (arriving with plenty of time).
- **One-line Summary:** Manage to arrive or finish just before the deadline.
- **Usage:** `どうにか` nhấn mạnh kết quả vừa sát nút, kịp thời hạn trong tình thế suýt chút nữa là trễ.
- **Example:** 電車が遅れたが、走ってどうにか会議に間に合った。(The train was delayed, but I ran and barely managed to make it to the meeting in time.)
