Vocabulary | 浅い

- **Meaning:** Shallow, superficial, slight (Nông, hời hợt, cạn).
- **Trigger:** Shallow river, superficial knowledge, slight experience (`浅い川`, `知識が浅い`, `経験が浅い`).
- **Contrast:** Antonym of `深い` (deep). Differs from `薄い` (thin/light).
- **One-line Summary:** Shallow in physical depth, knowledge, or experience.
- **Correct Synonym:** 浅薄な
- **Distractor1:** 深い
- **Distractor2:** 濃い
- **Distractor3:** 高い
- **Explanation:** `浅薄な` is the Sino-Japanese synonym for shallow/superficial. `深い` (deep) is the direct antonym, while `濃い` (concentrated) and `高い` (high) carry unrelated concepts.
