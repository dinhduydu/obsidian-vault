Vocabulary | 直接

- **Meaning:** Direct, immediate, face-to-face (Trực tiếp).
- **Trigger:** Used when contacting, delivering, or acting without an intermediary: `直接話す`.
- **Contrast:** Opposite of `間接` (indirect).
- **One-line Summary:** Acting or communicating firsthand without intermediaries.
- **Correct Synonym:** じかに
- **Distractor1:** 間接的に
- **Distractor2:** 代理で
- **Distractor3:** 遠回りに
- **Explanation:** `直接` means directly, synonymous with `じかに` (firsthand/directly). `間接的に` means indirectly. `代理で` means by proxy. `遠回りに` means in a roundabout way.
