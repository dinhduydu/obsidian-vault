Vocabulary | 誤認

- **Meaning:** Misidentification, mistaken recognition, misapprehension (Nhận nhầm, nhận thức sai).
- **Trigger:** Misidentifying suspects, misapprehending facts or identity (`事実の〜`, `犯人と〜する`).
- **Contrast:** Differs from `錯覚` (optical/sensory illusion) and `誤解` (misunderstanding of intent).
- **One-line Summary:** Misidentify or mistakenly recognize a fact or identity.
- **Correct Synonym:** 勘違いする
- **Distractor1:** 錯覚する
- **Distractor2:** 承認する
- **Distractor3:** 把握する
- **Explanation:** `勘違いする` is a common synonym for misidentifying or misapprehending facts. `錯覚する` refers to sensory/mind illusion. `承認する` (approve) and `把握する` (grasp) are positive/correct cognition.
