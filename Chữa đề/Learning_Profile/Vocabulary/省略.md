Vocabulary | 省略する

- **Meaning:** Abbreviate, shorten, omit (Rút gọn, viết tắt, bỏ qua).
- **Trigger:** Omitting words, sentences, formal procedures, or honorifics (`敬称を〜`, `文を〜`).
- **Contrast:** Differs from `省く` (native Japanese verb for skipping steps/effort) and `短縮する` (shortening duration/distance).
- **One-line Summary:** Abbreviate or shorten text, speech, or formal steps.
- **Correct Synonym:** 短縮する
- **Distractor1:** 延長する
- **Distractor2:** 費やす
- **Distractor3:** 詳述する
- **Explanation:** `短縮する` means to shorten or abbreviate. `延長する` (extend) and `詳述する` (explain in detail) are antonyms, while `費やす` means to spend.
