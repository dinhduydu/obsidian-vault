Vocabulary | 先端

- **Meaning:** Leading edge, tip, state-of-the-art (Mũi nhọn, đi đầu, tiên tiến).
- **Trigger:** Advanced technology or physical tips (`先端技術`, `時代の先端`).
- **Contrast:** `末端` (bottom end / lowest rank), `後発` (latecomer).
- **One-line Summary:** Noun referring to the foremost position or advanced technology edge.
- **Correct Synonym:** 進んだ (Advanced / Forward-moving).
- **Distractor1:** 末端 (Lowest end / Tail end)
- **Distractor2:** 古風 (Old-fashioned)
- **Distractor3:** 平凡 (Ordinary / Average)
- **Explanation:** 先端 indicates the front position or most advanced stage in technological development.
