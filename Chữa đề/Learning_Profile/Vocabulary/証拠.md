Vocabulary | 証拠

- **Meaning:** Evidence, proof (Bằng chứng, chứng cứ).
- **Trigger:** Used in context of proof or verifying statements (`証拠がない`, `証拠を見せる`).
- **Contrast:** `形跡` (traces / physical marks), `根拠` (logical grounds / foundation).
- **One-line Summary:** Noun referring to physical or factual proof supporting a claim or belief.
- **Correct Synonym:** 裏付け (Backing / Corroboration / Proof).
- **Distractor1:** 推測 (Guess / Conjecture)
- **Distractor2:** 噂 (Rumor / Gossip)
- **Distractor3:** 仮説 (Hypothesis)
- **Explanation:** 証拠 provides concrete proof to verify a claim, synonymous with 裏付け.
