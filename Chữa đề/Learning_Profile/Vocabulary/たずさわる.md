Vocabulary | たずさわる

- **Meaning:** Be engaged in, be involved in, participate in (an industry or profession) / Tham gia vào, làm việc trong (ngành nghề/lĩnh vực).
- **Trigger:** Modifying nouns of profession or institution: `食にたずさわる学校`, `医療にたずさわる仕事`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `従事する` (formal/written Sino-Japanese term) and `関わる` (be related to / concerned with).
- **One-line Summary:** Engage or work in a specific professional field or industry.
- **Correct Synonym:** 従事する
- **Distractor1:** 妨げる
- **Distractor2:** 避ける
- **Distractor3:** 怠る
- **Explanation:** `従事する` is a Sino-Japanese synonym for being engaged in a profession. `妨げる` (hinder), `避ける` (avoid), and `怠る` (neglect) carry unrelated or negative meanings.
