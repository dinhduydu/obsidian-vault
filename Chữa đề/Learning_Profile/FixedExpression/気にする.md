FixedExpression | 気にする

- **Meaning:** To worry about, care about, be bothered by (Lo lắng, bận tâm, chú ý đến).
- **Trigger:** Expressing anxiety or concern over small details (`細かいことを気にする`).
- **Contrast:** `気にする` (to worry / be concerned) vs `気になる` (to be curious / stay on one's mind unconsciously).
- **One-line Summary:** Expression indicating conscious worry or concern over something.
- **Usage:** 物事について心配したり心に留めたりする様子を表す。(Expresses worrying about or keeping something on one's mind.)
- **Example:** 他人の目を気にしないで、自分の道を進みなさい。(Don't worry about what others think, follow your own path. / Đừng bận tâm đến ánh mắt của người khác, hãy đi theo con đường của chính mình.)
- 気になさらないでください。
