FixedExpression | 必ずしも〜ない

- **Meaning:** Not necessarily, not always (Không hẳn là, không nhất thiết là).
- **Trigger:** Partial negation combined with negative endings (`必ずしも〜とは限らない`).
- **Contrast:** `必ずしも〜ない` expresses partial negation; `絶対〜ない` expresses total negation.
- **One-line Summary:** Fixed partial-negation expression stating that a premise is not universally true.
- **Usage:** 全てがそうであるとは言えないという部分否定を表す。(Expresses partial negation that one cannot say all are so.)
- **Example:** 高価なものが必ずしも良いものだとは限らない。(Expensive things are not necessarily good things. / Đồ đắt tiền không hẳn lúc nào cũng là đồ tốt.)
