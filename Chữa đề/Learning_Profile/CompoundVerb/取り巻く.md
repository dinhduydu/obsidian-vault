CompoundVerb | 取り巻く

- **Meaning:** Surround, encircle, encompass (Bao quanh, vây quanh).
- **Trigger:** Environment surrounding children, companies, or changing situations (`環境が〜`, `〜を取り巻く状況`).
- **Contrast:** Differs from `囲む` (physically surrounding an object or table) and `取り上げる` (taking up a topic).
- **One-line Summary:** Encompass or form a surrounding environment around a subject.
- **Usage:** Thường dùng ở dạng `〜を取り巻く` để chỉ môi trường, bối cảnh hay tình hình xung quanh một đối tượng.
- **Example:** 子供を取り巻く環境は時代とともに変化している。(The environment surrounding children changes with the times.)
