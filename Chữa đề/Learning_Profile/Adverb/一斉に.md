Adverb | 一斉に

- **Meaning:** Simultaneously, all at once, in unison (Đồng thời, đồng loạt, cùng một lúc).
- **Trigger:** Multiple people or objects taking action together at the exact same moment (`一斉に走り出す`).
- **Contrast:** `順番に` (one by one / in turn - opposite), `個別に` (individually).
- **One-line Summary:** Adverb describing actions carried out simultaneously by a group.
- **Usage:** 多くの人や物が、同じ瞬間に同時に行動を起こす様子を表す。(Expresses many people or things starting an action at the exact same moment.)
- **Example:** チャイムが鳴ると、生徒たちは一斉に教室を飛び出した。(When the chime rang, the students burst out of the classroom all at once. / Khi chuông reo, các học sinh đã đồng loạt lao ra khỏi lớp học.)
