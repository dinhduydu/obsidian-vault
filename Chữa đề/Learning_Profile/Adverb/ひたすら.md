Adverb | ひたすら

- **Meaning:** Earnestly, single-mindedly, solely (Chỉ một mực, tha thiết).
- **Trigger:** Continuing an action continuously without doing anything else (`ひたすら祈る`, `ひたすら走る`).
- **Contrast:** `ひたすら` emphasizes single-minded focus on one action; `ただ` is simpler "only".
- **One-line Summary:** Adverb for single-mindedly devoting oneself to one single action.
- **Usage:** 他のことは考えず、ひとつの行動に専念する様子を表す。(Expresses concentrating solely on one action without thinking of other things.)
- **Example:** 合格を信じて、ひたすら勉強に励んだ。(Believing in passing, I single-mindedly devoted myself to studying. / Tin tưởng vào việc đỗ, tôi đã chỉ một mực dốc sức học tập.)
