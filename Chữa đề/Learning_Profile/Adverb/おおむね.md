Adverb | おおむね

- **Meaning:** Generally, mostly, on the whole (Nhìn chung, đại thể).
- **Trigger:** Describing general agreement or overall status (`おおむね賛成だ`, `おおむね順調だ`).
- **Contrast:** `おおむね` refers to overall general condition (80-90%); `だいたい` is more conversational.
- **One-line Summary:** Adverb indicating that the general state or overall majority matches a premise.
- **Usage:** 大部分、あるいは全体としてその状態にある様子を表す。(Expresses being in that state for the major part or as a whole.)
- **Example:** 計画はおおむね順調に進んでいる。(The plan is progressing generally smoothly on the whole. / Kế hoạch nhìn chung đang tiến triển thuận lợi.)
