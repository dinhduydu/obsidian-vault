ぐんぐん ＝とても速い Nhanh chóng

Adverb | ぐんぐん

- **Meaning:** Rapidly, steadily, vigorously (Nhanh chóng, tiến tới vù vù).
- **Trigger:** Rapid growth, improvement, or forward movement (`成績がぐんぐん伸びる`).
- **Contrast:** `じわりじわり` (gradually), `だらだら` (sluggishly).
- **One-line Summary:** Onomatopoeic adverb describing fast, powerful progress or growth.
- **Usage:** 物事が勢いよく速く進んだり成長したりする様子を表す。(Expresses things progressing or growing rapidly with momentum.)
- **Example:** 彼の日本語の能力はぐんぐん伸びている。(His Japanese ability is improving rapidly. / Năng lực tiếng Nhật của anh ấy đang tăng tiến nhanh chóng.)
