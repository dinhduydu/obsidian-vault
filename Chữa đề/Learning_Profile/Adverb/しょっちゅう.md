Adverb | しょっちゅう

- **Meaning:** Frequently, constantly, always (Thường xuyên, luôn luôn, lặp đi lặp lại).
- **Trigger:** Repeated daily habits or frequent mistakes (`しょっちゅう忘れる`).
- **Contrast:** `たまに` (occasionally), `めったに` (rarely).
- **One-line Summary:** Casual adverb indicating high frequency or repeated occurrence of actions.
- **Usage:** 行為や出来事が繰り返し頻繁に起こる様子を表す（日常会話でよく使われる）。(Expresses actions or events happening repeatedly and frequently; common in daily speech.)
- **Example:** 彼はしょっちゅう宿題を忘れる。(He frequently forgets his homework. / Anh ấy thường xuyên quên bài tập về nhà.)
