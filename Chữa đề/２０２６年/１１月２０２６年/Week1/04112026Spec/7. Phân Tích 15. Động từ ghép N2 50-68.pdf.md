Error Analysis

Dưới đây là phân tích chi tiết các bẫy động từ ghép và nguyên nhân gây sai sót phổ biến khi làm bài thi JLPT N2 (Mondai 1 - Cách đọc Kanji, Mondai 4 - Cách dùng từ, Mondai 5 - Từ đồng nghĩa):

1. Nhầm lẫn giữa các động từ ghép tiền tố `押し〜` (`押し付ける`, `押し込む`, `押しかける`)

- **Bẫy thi:** Đề thi N2 thường đưa các từ có chung tiền tố `押し〜` vào phương án lựa chọn:
    - `押し付ける` (おしつける): Nhấn mạnh xuống / Ép buộc công việc cho cấp dưới (`仕事を部下に押し付ける`).
    - `押し込む` (おしこむ): Nhồi nhét hành lý vào túi / Cảnh sát ập vào phòng (`荷物を無理やり押し込む`).
    - `押しかける` (おしかける): Khách hàng ùn ùn kéo đến cửa hàng (`店に客が押しかけた`).
- **Nguyên nhân & Phân biệt:** Học viên dễ chọn nhầm do đều bắt đầu bằng `押し` (áp/ấn). Cần phân biệt hậu tố: `付ける` (gắn vào/áp đặt ➔ ép công việc); `込む` (vào trong ➔ nhồi nhét/đột nhập); `かける` (hướng tới ➔ ùn ùn kéo đến).

2. Nhầm lẫn giữa các động từ ghép tiền tố `言い〜` (`言いつける`, `言い渡す`, `言い放つ`)

- **Bẫy thi:**
    - `言いつける` (いいつける): Sai bảo đi mua đồ / Mách lẻo với cô giáo (`先生に言いつける`).
    - `言い渡す` (いいわたす): Bác sĩ thông báo tên bệnh / Tòa án tuyên án (`医者に病名を言い渡された`).
    - `言い放つ` (いいはなつ): Phát ngôn trắng trợn, nói thẳng không kiêng nể (`失礼なことを言い放った`).
- **Nguyên nhân sai:** Không phân biệt được sắc thái chính thức (tuyên án: `言い渡す`), sai bảo/mách (`言いつける`), và nói thẳng thắn/thiếu lịch sự (`言い放つ`).

3. Nhầm lẫn giữa nhóm động từ `思い〜` (`思いつく`, `思い切る`, `思いがけない`)

- **Bẫy thi:**
    - `思いつく` (おもいつく): Chợt nghĩ ra ý tưởng mới (`新しいアイデアを思いつく`).
    - `思い切る` (おもいきる): Quyết tâm từ bỏ/nghỉ việc (`思い切って会社を辞める`).
    - `思いがけない` (おもいがけない): Ngoài dự tính/bất ngờ (`思いがけないプレゼント`).
- **Nguyên nhân & Phân biệt:** Phân biệt rõ: `思いつく` là bừng sáng ý tưởng; `思い切る` là dứt khoát đưa ra quyết định lớn; `思いがけない` là tính từ chỉ sự việc bất ngờ ngoài mong đợi.

Knowledge Extracted

CompoundVerb | くっつく

- **Meaning:** To stick to, to adhere to, to cling to (Dính vào, bám chặt lấy).
- **Trigger:** Papers sticking together, or a child clinging closely to a parent (`紙と紙がくっつく`, `子どもが母親にくっつく`).
- **Contrast:** Intransitive `くっつく` (to stick/cling onto) vs Transitive `くっつける` (to attach/glue together).
- **One-line Summary:** Intransitive compound verb for sticking or clinging closely to something or someone.
- **Usage:** 物や人が離れずに密着して離れない状態を表す。(Expresses objects or people sticking closely together without separating.)
- **Example:** 子どもが母親にくっついていた。(The child was clinging closely to their mother. / Đứa trẻ bám chặt lấy mẹ.)

CompoundVerb | 繰り返す

- **Meaning:** To repeat, to do over and over (Lặp đi lặp lại).
- **Trigger:** Repeating mistakes, actions, or words (`同じミスを繰り返す`).
- **Contrast:** `繰り返す` refers to repeating the same process/action; `やり直す` refers to restarting from scratch after failure.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for doing the same action or process repeatedly.
- **Usage:** 同じことや行動を何度も重ねて行う動作を表す。(Expresses doing the same action or thing repeatedly multiple times.)
- **Example:** 同じミスを繰り返してはいけない。(You must not repeat the same mistake. / Không được lặp đi lặp lại cùng một lỗi.)

CompoundVerb | 落ち込む

- **Meaning:** To decline/decrease, to feel down/depressed (Sút/giảm xuống, buồn bà/suy sụp).
- **Trigger:** Sales dropping, or feeling depressed after failing an exam (`売上が落ち込む`, `試験に落ちて落ち込む`).
- **Contrast:** `落ち込む` refers to numerical slump or emotional depression; `落ちる` simply means falling physically or failing.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for numerical slumps or feeling emotionally down/depressed.
- **Usage:** 数値や業績が低下する、または気分が凹んで落ち込む様子を表す。(Expresses numerical/business decline or feeling emotionally down.)
- **Example:** 試験に落ちて落ち込んでいる。(I am feeling depressed after failing the exam. / Tôi đang suy sụp vì trượt kỳ thi.)

CompoundVerb | 思いつく

- **Meaning:** To think of, to hit upon, to come up with (Nghĩ ra ý tưởng/kế hoạch).
- **Trigger:** Coming up with a new idea or solution suddenly (`新しいアイデアを思いつく`).
- **Contrast:** `思いつく` means suddenly coming up with an idea; `思い出す` means remembering something past.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for suddenly coming up with a new idea or thought.
- **Usage:** 新しい考えや名案が頭に浮かぶことを表す。(Expresses a new idea or clever thought coming to mind.)
- **Example:** 新しいアイデアを思いついた。(I came up with a new idea. / Tôi đã nghĩ ra ý tưởng mới.)

CompoundVerb | 思い切る

- **Meaning:** To make up one's mind, to resolve (Quyết tâm làm điều gì đó).
- **Trigger:** Making a bold decision to quit a job or take a risk (`思い切って会社を辞める`).
- **Contrast:** `思い切る` means making a resolute decision or cutting off hesitation; `思いとどまる` means holding back from acting.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for making a bold, resolute decision or cutting off hesitation.
- **Usage:** 決心をつけて思いきって行動する様子を表す。(Expresses making up one's mind and acting resolutely.)
- **Example:** 思い切って会社を辞めた。(I made up my mind and quit the company. / Tôi đã quyết tâm nghỉ việc công ty.)

CompoundVerb | 思いがけない

- **Meaning:** Unexpected, unforeseen, surprising (Ngoài dự tính, không ngờ tới).
- **Trigger:** Receiving unexpected gifts or facing unexpected news (`思いがけないプレゼント`, `思いがけないニュース`).
- **Contrast:** `思いがけない` means completely unforeseen/unexpected; `意外な` is an adjectival noun while `思いがけない` modifies nouns directly.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound word describing an unexpected or unforeseen event or gift.
- **Usage:** 事前に全く予想もしていなかった状態や出来事を表す。(Expresses a state or event that was completely unforeseen in advance.)
- **Example:** 思いがけないプレゼントをもらった。(I received an unexpected gift. / Tôi đã nhận được món quà ngoài mong đợi.)

CompoundVerb | 受け持つ

- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for exceeding or surpassing a target, expectation, or estimate.

CompoundVerb | 釣り合う

- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for two things being well-balanced, matching, or in proportion.

- **Example:** 収入と支出が釣り合っていない。(Income and expenses are not balanced. / Thu nhập và chi tiêu không cân bằng.)

CompoundVerb | 使いこなす

- **Trigger:** Operating computers, tools, or foreign languages skillfully (`パソコンを使いこなす`, `外国語を使いこなす`).
- **Contrast:** `使いこなす` means mastering tools/languages skillfully; `使い果たす` means using up completely.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for mastering tools, devices, or skills to use them proficiently.

CompoundVerb | 放り込む

- **Contrast:** `放り込む` means casually tossing/throwing something inside; `投げ出す` means abandoning or stretching out.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for casually throwing or tossing an object inside a space.

CompoundVerb | 押し付ける

- **Meaning:** To press against, to force/impose work on others (Nhấn xuống / Ép buộc, áp đặt).
- **Trigger:** Pressing a door hard, or forcing unwanted tasks onto subordinates (`ドアを強く押し付ける`, `仕事を部下に押し付ける`).
- **Contrast:** `押し付ける` means forcing a task or physical pressure onto someone; `押し込む` means shoving/cramming objects into a container.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for physically pressing down or forcing unwanted duties onto others.
- **Usage:** 物を強く押しあてる、または嫌な仕事などを他人に押しつけることを表す。(Expresses pressing an object hard, or forcing unwanted tasks onto others.)
- **Example:** 仕事を部下に押し付けた。(He forced the work onto his subordinate. / Anh ta đẩy công việc cho cấp dưới.)

CompoundVerb | 押し込む

- **Meaning:** To cram in, to shove into / to barge into (Ấn vào, nhồi nhét / Ập vào, xống vào).
- **Trigger:** Cramming luggage into a bag, or police barging into a room (`荷物を無理やり押し込む`, `警察が部屋に押し込む`).
- **Contrast:** `押し込む` means shoving/cramming things inside or breaking in; `押しかける` means a crowd flocking to a venue.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for cramming items forcefully inside or barging into a room.
- **Usage:** 物を無理に中に詰め込む、または無理やり侵入することを表す。(Expresses forcibly cramming items inside, or breaking/barging in.)
- **Example:** 荷物を無理やり押し込んだ。(I forcefully crammed the luggage inside. / Tôi nhồi nhét hành lý vào một cách miễn cưỡng.)

CompoundVerb | 押しかける

- **Meaning:** To flock to, to rush to, to crowd around (Xúm lại, ùa đến).
- **Trigger:** Customers flocking to a shop or fans crowding a venue (`店に客が押しかける`).
- **Contrast:** `押しかける` refers to a crowd flocking to a place; `押し寄せる` refers to waves/crowds surging forward.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for a large group of people flocking or crowding around a location.
- **Usage:** 大勢の人が押しよせるように押しかけて来る様子を表す。(Expresses a large group of people flocking together to a place.)
- **Example:** 店に客が押しかけた。(Customers flocked to the shop. / Khách hàng ùn ùn kéo đến cửa hàng.)

CompoundVerb | 打ち解ける

- **Meaning:** To open up, to warm up to, to become friendly (Thân thiết, cởi mở).
- **Trigger:** Warming up quickly to a new class or colleagues (`新しいクラスにすぐ打ち解ける`).
- **Contrast:** `打ち解ける` means opening up emotionally to become friendly; `打ち切る` means bringing to an abrupt end.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for opening up emotionally and quickly bonding with a group or person.
- **Usage:** 警戒心がなくなって親しい気持ちでなじむ様子を表す。(Expresses dropping guard and becoming friendly and comfortable with others.)
- **Example:** 新しいクラスにもすぐ打ち解けた。(I quickly warmed up to the new class. / Tôi nhanh chóng hòa đồng với lớp mới.)

CompoundVerb | 打ち切る

- **Meaning:** To call off, to abort, to terminate abruptly (Ngừng, bỏ ngang giữa chừng).
- **Trigger:** Abruptly terminating a meeting or stopping a program halfway (`会議を途中で打ち切る`).
- **Contrast:** `打ち切る` means calling off/terminating mid-way; `締め切る` means closing at a scheduled deadline.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for bringing an event, meeting, or plan to an abrupt early halt.
- **Usage:** 途中で進行中の物事を中止して終わらせることを表す。(Expresses bringing an ongoing event or matter to an abrupt halt halfway.)
- **Example:** 会議を途中で打ち切った。(The meeting was called off mid-way. / Cuộc họp đã bị dừng giữa chừng.)

CompoundVerb | 言いつける

- **Meaning:** To order/command, to tell on/snitch (Ra lệnh / Mách lẻo).
- **Trigger:** Ordering someone to go shopping, or telling on someone to the teacher (`買い物を言いつける`, `先生に言いつける`).
- **Contrast:** `言いつける` means commanding or snitching; `言い渡す` means officially pronouncing a verdict/sentence.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for ordering someone to do something or telling on someone to authority.
- **Usage:** 命令して行わせる、または告げ口して他人の過失を知らせることを表す。(Expresses ordering an action, or snitching to report another's fault.)
- **Example:** 先生に言いつけるよ。(I will tell on you to the teacher! / Tôi sẽ mách cô giáo đấy.)

CompoundVerb | 言い渡す

- **Meaning:** To pronounce, to declare, to announce officially (Tuyên án, thông báo tên bệnh/kết quả).
- **Trigger:** A judge sentencing a verdict or doctor announcing a diagnosis (`病名を言い渡された`, `判決を言い渡す`).
- **Contrast:** `言い渡す` is official/formal announcement of a sentence or diagnosis; `言いつける` is ordering or snitching.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for officially declaring or pronouncing a verdict, sentence, or medical diagnosis.
- **Usage:** 判決や診断結果などを公式に宣告して告げることを表す。(Expresses officially pronouncing or declaring a judgment or diagnosis.)
- **Example:** 医者に病名を言い渡された。(I was officially informed of my diagnosis by the doctor. / Tôi được bác sĩ thông báo tên bệnh.)

CompoundVerb | 言い放つ

- **Meaning:** To blurt out, to state bluntly/arrogantly (Nói thẳng, phát ngôn không kiêng nể).
- **Trigger:** Blurting out rude or sharp statements bluntly without care (`失礼なことを言い放った`).
- **Contrast:** `言い放つ` means blurting out sharp/rude statements openly; `言い張る` means stubbornly insisting on one's claim.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for blurting out statements openly or bluntly without regard for feelings.
- **Usage:** 配慮せずにきっぱりと言い捨てるように発言することを表す。(Expresses blurting out something bluntly without consideration.)
- **Example:** 彼は失礼なことを言い放った。(He bluntly made a rude remark. / Anh ta nói thẳng những điều thiếu lịch sự.)
