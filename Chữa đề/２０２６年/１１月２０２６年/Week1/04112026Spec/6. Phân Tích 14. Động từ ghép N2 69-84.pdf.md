Error Analysis

Dưới đây là phân tích chi tiết các bẫy động từ ghép và nguyên nhân gây sai sót phổ biến khi làm bài thi JLPT N2 (Mondai 1 - Cách đọc Kanji, Mondai 4 - Cách dùng từ, Mondai 5 - Từ đồng nghĩa):

1. Nhầm lẫn giữa các động từ ghép tiền tố `言い〜` (`言い張る`, `言い残す`, `言い返す`)

- **Bẫy thi:** Đề thi N2 thường đưa các từ có chung tiền tố `言い〜` vào phương án lựa chọn:
    - `言い張る` (いいはる): Khăng khăng giữ ý mình dù bị phản đối (`自分は悪くないと言い張る`).
    - `言い残す` (いいのこす): Dặn dò, để lại lời trăn trối trước khi đi/mất (`感謝を言い残す`).
    - `言い返す` (いいかえす): Đáp trả, cãi lại khi bị phàn nàn (`文句を言われてすぐ言い返した`).
- **Nguyên nhân & Phân biệt:** Học viên dễ chọn nhầm do đều bắt đầu bằng `言い` (nói). Cần phân biệt hậu tố: `張る` (căng ra ➔ khăng khăng); `残す` (để lại ➔ trăn trối/dặn lại); `返す` (trả lại ➔ cãi lại/đáp trả).

2. Nhầm lẫn động từ di chuyển/lịch trình (`乗り継ぐ`, `ずれ込む`, `たどり着く`)

- **Bẫy thi:**
    - `乗り継ぐ` (のりつぐ): Đổi chuyến phương tiện nối tiếp (`新幹線を乗り継ぐ`). Phân biệt với `乗り換える` (đổi tàu nói chung).
    - `ずれ込む` (ずれこむ): Lịch trình bị dời/kéo dài sang giai đoạn sau (`予定が来月までずれ込む`). Phân biệt với `ずれる` (lệch vị trí/mục tiêu).
    - `たどり着く` (たどりつく): Gian nan mãi mới tới đích (`目的地にたどり着いた`). Phân biệt với `到着する` (đến nơi trung tính).
- **Nguyên nhân sai:** Không chú ý đến sắc thái gian khổ (`たどり着く`) hoặc sự kéo dài thời gian sang kỳ sau (`ずれ込む`).

3. Nhầm lẫn nghĩa đen và nghĩa ẩn dụ của `引っかかる`, `投げ出す`, `吸い上げる`

- **Bẫy thi:**
    - `引っかかる`: Nghĩa đen là mắc vào cửa (`ドアに引っかかる`), nghĩa ẩn dụ là mắc lừa bẫy/lừa đảo (`詐欺に引っかかる`).
    - `投げ出す`: Nghĩa đen là duỗi chân (`足を投げ出す`), nghĩa ẩn dụ là bỏ dở công việc (`仕事を投げ出す`).
    - `吸い上げる`: Nghĩa đen là bơm nước (`水を吸い上げる`), nghĩa ẩn dụ là bòn rút lợi nhuận / tiếp thu ý kiến (`利益を吸い上げる`).
- **Nguyên nhân & Phân biệt:** Thí sinh thường chỉ nhớ 1 nghĩa đen mà bỏ sót nghĩa chuyển hóa trong các bài đọc hiểu N2.

Knowledge Extracted

CompoundVerb | 言い張る

- **Meaning:** To insist stubbornly, to assert one's stance (Khăng khăng, quả quyết giữ ý mình).
- **Trigger:** Used when someone stubbornly maintains their opinion despite opposition or blame (`自分は悪くないと言い張る`).
- **Contrast:** `言い張る` means stubbornly insisting on one's opinion; `言い返す` means talking back to criticism.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb meaning stubbornly insisting on one's own stance or opinion.
- **Usage:** 自分の主張を頑固に変えずに言い続ける様子を表す。(Expresses stubbornly continuing to state one's opinion.)
- **Example:** 彼は自分は悪くないと言い張った。(He insisted stubbornly that he was not at fault. / Anh ta khăng khăng rằng mình không sai.)

CompoundVerb | 言い残す

- **Meaning:** To leave a message, to say one's last words (Chuyện chưa nói xong, để lại lời trăn trối/dặn dò).
- **Trigger:** Expressing unsaid thoughts before leaving or final words before passing away (`何か言い残したこと`, `感謝を言い残す`).
- **Contrast:** `言い残す` refers to leaving words unsaid or convey final wishes; `言い張る` refers to stubborn insistence.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for leaving words unsaid or conveying final wishes before departing.
- **Usage:** 去る前や亡くなる前に言い忘れたことや最後の言葉を告げておくことを表す。(Expresses saying unsaid words or final wishes before leaving or passing away.)
- **Example:** 祖父は最後に家族への感謝を言い残した。(Grandfather left words of gratitude to the family before he passed away. / Ông tôi đã để lại lời cảm ơn gia đình trước lúc mất.)

CompoundVerb | 言い返す

- **Meaning:** To talk back, to retort, to answer back (Nói lại, cãi lại, đáp trả).
- **Trigger:** Retorting or talking back immediately when criticized or complained to (`文句を言われて言い返す`).
- **Contrast:** `言い返す` focuses on replying back to criticism; `言い張る` focuses on insisting on one's own claim.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for retorting or talking back when provoked or criticized.
- **Usage:** 批判や文句に対して負けずに言い返す動作を表す。(Expresses replying back forcefully to criticism or complaints.)
- **Example:** 文句を言われて、すぐ言い返した。(When complained to, I immediately talked back. / Bị phàn nàn nên tôi đã cãi lại ngay.)

CompoundVerb | 当てはめる

- **Meaning:** To apply, to adapt, to fit into (Áp dụng, đặt vào quy tắc/mô hình).
- **Trigger:** Applying rules, theories, or conditions to specific cases or people (`ルールを全員に当てはめる`).
- **Contrast:** Transitive `当てはめる` (apply something to) vs Intransitive `当てはまる` (be applicable / fit).
- **One-line Summary:** Transitive compound verb for applying a rule, theory, or condition to a case.
- **Usage:** 条件やルールを特定の事例や人物に適用することを表す。(Expresses applying conditions or rules to specific cases or people.)
- **Example:** このルールを全員に当てはめるのは難しい。(It is difficult to apply this rule to everyone. / Khó áp dụng quy tắc này cho tất cả mọi người.)

CompoundVerb | 乗り継ぐ

- **Meaning:** To transfer, to change trains/flights (Đổi chuyến tàu/máy bay nối chuyến).
- **Trigger:** Changing from one vehicle to another at a junction or station (`新幹線を乗り継ぐ`).
- **Contrast:** `乗り継ぐ` implies connecting vehicles for a longer journey; `乗り換える` means switching vehicles neutrally.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for transferring between transport vehicles to continue a journey.
- **Usage:** 交通機関を次々に乗り変えて目的地へ向かう様子を表す。(Expresses switching transportation successively to reach a destination.)
- **Example:** 東京駅で新幹線を乗り継いだ。(I transferred to the Shinkansen at Tokyo Station. / Tôi đã đổi tàu Shinkansen ở ga Tokyo.)

CompoundVerb | 割り込む

- **Meaning:** To cut in, to intrude, to interrupt (Chen ngang hàng, xen vào cuộc trò chuyện).
- **Trigger:** Intruding into a line of people or cutting into an ongoing conversation (`列に割り込む`, `話に割り込む`).
- **Contrast:** `割り込む` means intruding into a line or talk; `割り当てる` means allocating tasks.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for intruding into a line or cutting into a conversation.
- **Usage:** 人の列や会話の中に強引に入ることを表す。(Expresses forcing one's way into a line or conversation.)
- **Example:** 電車に乗るとき、列に割り込む人がいた。(There was someone who cut in line when boarding the train. / Có người chen ngang hàng khi lên tàu điện.)

CompoundVerb | 投げ出す

- **Meaning:** To stretch out (legs), to abandon mid-way, to give up (Duỗi chân, bỏ dở công việc giữa chừng).
- **Trigger:** Stretching legs onto a couch or quitting work halfway (`足を投げ出す`, `仕事を投げ出す`).
- **Contrast:** `投げ出す` means stretching out limbs or abandoning halfway; `投げ捨てる` means throwing away.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for stretching out limbs casually or abandoning a task halfway.
- **Usage:** 足を前に伸ばす、または途中で責任を放棄してやめることを表す。(Expresses stretching out legs or abandoning responsibility halfway.)
- **Example:** 途中で仕事を投げ出してはいけない。(You must not abandon your work halfway. / Không được bỏ dở công việc giữa chừng.)

Vocabulary | むかつく

- **Meaning:** To feel nauseous, to feel irritated/angry (Buồn nôn, nôn nao / Cảm thấy tức tối, khó chịu).
- **Trigger:** Motion sickness in cars or feeling annoyed by someone's attitude (`気分がむかつく`, `話し方がむかつく`).
- **Contrast:** `むかつく` expresses stomach nausea or sharp irritation; `いらいらする` expresses feeling restless/impatient.
- **One-line Summary:** Verb describing stomach nausea or emotional annoyance and irritation.
- **Correct Synonym:** 腹が立つ (To get angry / feel offended).
- **Distractor1:** すっきりする (Feel refreshed / Relieved)
- **Distractor2:** 落ち着く (Calm down / Settle)
- **Distractor3:** 和らぐ (Soften / Ease)
- **Explanation:** むかつく describes stomach nausea or sharp emotional irritation, functioning synonymously with 腹が立つ.

CompoundVerb | 見渡す

- **Meaning:** To look over, to survey, to take a wide view (Nhìn tổng thể, nhìn bao quát toàn cảnh).
- **Trigger:** Looking out over a city or landscape from a high vantage point (`町全体を見渡す`).
- **Contrast:** `見渡す` means surveying a wide panoramic view; `見下ろす` means looking down from above.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for surveying or taking a wide panoramic view from an elevated spot.
- **Usage:** 広範囲を遠くまで見広げる動作を表す。(Expresses looking far across a wide range.)
- **Example:** 山の上から町全体を見渡した。(I surveyed the whole city from the top of the mountain. / Tôi nhìn bao quát toàn bộ thành phố từ trên núi.)

CompoundVerb | 切り替える

- **Meaning:** To switch over, to change, to update (Sửa đổi, thay thế, chuyển đổi hệ thống/tâm trạng).
- **Trigger:** Replacing an old system with a new one or switching mindsets (`新しいものに切り替える`).
- **Contrast:** `切り替える` means updating/switching to a new mode; `切り詰める` means economizing/cutting back.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for switching or updating from an old system/mindset to a new one.
- **Usage:** 従来のものから新しい方式や気分に変えることを表す。(Expresses changing from a conventional method/mindset to a new one.)
- **Example:** 古いシステムを新しいものに切り替えた。(I switched the old system to a new one. / Tôi đã chuyển từ hệ thống cũ sang hệ thống mới.)

CompoundVerb | 引き止める

- **Meaning:** To hold back, to detain, to restrain (Níu kéo, ngăn chặn, kéo lại không cho đi).
- **Trigger:** Restraining someone from leaving or stopping a guest (`私を引き止めた`).
- **Contrast:** `引き止める` means holding someone else back from leaving; `引き返す` means turning back oneself.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for restraining or holding someone back from departing.
- **Usage:** 去ろうとする人を呼び止めたり押しとどめたりすることを表す。(Expresses calling back or detaining someone trying to leave.)
- **Example:** 母が私を引き止めた。(My mother held me back. / Mẹ đã giữ tôi lại.)

CompoundVerb | 引っかかる

- **Meaning:** To get caught/stuck, to be tricked/cheated (Bị mắc vào, mắc kẹt / Mắc lừa bẫy).
- **Trigger:** Bag getting caught on a door, or falling victim to a fraud scheme (`ドアに引っかかる`, `詐欺に引っかかる`).
- **Contrast:** Intransitive `引っかかる` (get caught/tricked) vs Transitive `引っかける` (hook/hang something).
- **One-line Summary:** Intransitive compound verb for getting caught on something or being tricked by a scam.
- **Usage:** 物が引っかかって止まる、または騙されて罠にはまることを表す。(Expresses getting caught on an object or falling into a trap/scam.)
- **Example:** 詐欺に引っかかった。(I got tricked by a scam. / Tôi đã mắc lừa vụ lừa đảo.)

CompoundVerb | 立ち直る

- **Meaning:** To recover, to get back on one's feet, to bounce back (Trở lại như cũ, khôi phục, hồi phục).
- **Trigger:** Recovering from failure, grief, or economic slump (`失敗から立ち直る`, `景気が立ち直る`).
- **Contrast:** `立ち直る` means recovering emotionally or economically; `立ち上がる` means standing up physically.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for recovering and bouncing back from failure or economic decline.
- **Usage:** 悪い状態から元の正常な状態に回復することを表す。(Expresses recovering from a bad state back to normal.)
- **Example:** 景気が少しずつ立ち直っている。(The economy is gradually recovering. / Kinh tế đang dần hồi phục.)

CompoundVerb | たどり着く

- **Meaning:** To arrive at after struggle, to reach after difficulty (Khổ sở, gian gian mãi mới đến nơi/đích).
- **Trigger:** Finally reaching a destination after getting lost or struggling (`目的地にたどり着く`).
- **Contrast:** `たどり着く` emphasizes struggling through hardship to arrive; `到着する` is neutral arriving.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for reaching a destination after a difficult or exhausting journey.
- **Usage:** 苦労や迷いの末にやっと目的地に到達することを表す。(Expresses reaching a destination after struggle or wandering.)
- **Example:** やっと目的地にたどり着いた。(I finally reached the destination after much effort. / Cuối cùng tôi cũng đến được đích.)

CompoundVerb | ずれ込む

- **Meaning:** To be delayed, to slide over, to be shifted into (Bị dời lịch, trễ kế hoạch sang thời gian khác).
- **Trigger:** Schedules or construction deadlines shifting into the next month/period (`予定が来月までずれ込む`).
- **Contrast:** `ずれ込む` means schedule shifting into a later timeframe; `ずれる` means being misaligned/off-target.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for a schedule or project deadline being shifted or delayed into a later period.
- **Usage:** 予定されていた時期や期限が後へと遅れて入り込む様子を表す。(Expresses a scheduled time or deadline shifting into a later period.)
- **Example:** 工事の予定が来月までずれ込んだ。(The construction schedule was shifted into next month. / Lịch thi công bị dời sang tháng sau.)

CompoundVerb | 吸い上げる

- **Meaning:** To pump up, to siphon/extort, to absorb/gather feedback (Bơm/hút lên, bòn rút lợi nhuận, tiếp thu ý kiến).
- **Trigger:** Pumping water, siphoning company profits, or gathering opinions from below (`水をもポンプで吸い上げる`, `利益を吸い上げる`).
- **Contrast:** `吸い上げる` means pumping up or gathering upward; `吸い込む` means inhaling/absorbing inward.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for pumping liquid upward, siphoning profits, or gathering feedback.
- **Usage:** 液体を吸って上にあげる、または利益や意見を吸い取って集めることを表す。(Expresses pumping liquid upward or siphoning profits/opinions.)
- **Example:** 利益を全部吸い上げられた。(All the profits were siphoned off. / Toàn bộ lợi nhuận đã bị rút sạch.)
