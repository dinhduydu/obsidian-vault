Error Analysis

Dưới đây là phân tích chi tiết các bẫy động từ ghép và nguyên nhân gây sai sót phổ biến khi làm bài thi JLPT N2 (Mondai 1 - Cách đọc Kanji, Mondai 4 - Cách dùng từ, Mondai 5 - Từ đồng nghĩa):

1. Nhầm lẫn giữa các động từ ghép tiền tố `立ち〜` (`立ち寄る` vs `立ち去る`)

- **Bẫy thi:**
    - `立ち寄る` (たちよる): Tạt qua, ghé qua quán xá/nhà bạn trên đường về (`帰りにコンビニに立ち寄る`).
    - `立ち去る` (たちさる): Rời đi, bỏ đi khỏi hiện trường (`現場から立ち去る`).
- **Nguyên nhân & Phân biệt:** Học viên dễ nhầm do cùng bắt đầu bằng `立ち`. Cần phân biệt hậu tố: `寄る` (tới gần ➔ ghé qua); `去る` (rời xa ➔ bỏ đi).

2. Nhầm lẫn giữa các động từ ghép chỉ sự vượt qua (`乗り切る` vs `乗り越える`)

- **Bẫy thi:**
    - `乗り切る` (のりきる): Vượt qua khủng hoảng, giai đoạn khó khăn an toàn (`危機を乗り切る`, `暑さを乗り切る`).
    - `乗り越える` (のりこえる): Trèo qua rào cản / vượt qua nghịch cảnh (`障害を乗り越える`).
- **Nguyên nhân sai:** `乗り切る` nhấn mạnh việc bình an vượt qua một _giai đoạn/thời kỳ_ đầy biến động; còn `乗り越える` nhấn mạnh việc chinh phục một _chướng ngại/rào cản_ cụ thể.

3. Nhầm lẫn giữa nhóm động từ tác động đối phương (`呼び止める`, `引き離す`, `追い返す`)

- **Bẫy thi:**
    - `呼び止める` (よびとめる): Gọi giật lại khi đối phương đang đi xa (`先生に呼び止められた`). Phân biệt với `引き止める` (níu giữ lại bằng tay/hành động).
    - `引き離す` (ひきはなす): Tách đứa trẻ khỏi cha mẹ / Bỏ xa đối thủ xếp thứ 2 (`2位の選手をぐんぐん引き離す`).
    - `追い返す` (おいかえす): Đuổi người đến quay về (`来た者を追い返す`). Phân biệt với `追いかける` (chạy đuổi theo).

Knowledge Extracted

CompoundVerb | 取りかかる

- **Meaning:** To set to work, to begin, to start on (Bắt đầu, bắt tay vào việc).
- **Trigger:** Used with particle `に` when starting a project, construction, or task (`工事に取り掛かる`).
- **Contrast:** `取りかかる` means actively beginning work on a task; `始まる` is an intransitive verb for neutral starting.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for setting to work or starting a task or project actively.
- **Usage:** 仕事や工事などの作業を始める動作を表す。(Expresses beginning work on a task or construction.)
- **Example:** 工事に取り掛かる。(To set to work on construction. / Bắt tay vào công trình thi công.)

CompoundVerb | 振り向く

- **Meaning:** To turn around, to look back (Ngoảnh mặt, ngoảnh lại nhìn).
- **Trigger:** Turning around upon feeling someone's gaze or hearing one's name (`視線を感じて振り向く`).
- **Contrast:** `振り向く` means physically turning one's head/body around; `振り返る` can also mean reflecting on the past.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for physically turning around to look behind.
- **Usage:** 後ろを振り返って視線を向ける動作を表す。(Expresses turning around to face backwards.)
- **Example:** 視線を感じて振り向くと知らん人が私を見ていた。(When I turned around feeling a gaze, a stranger was looking at me. / Cảm thấy có ánh nhìn nên tôi quay lại thì thấy người lạ đang nhìn mình.)

CompoundVerb | 取り払う

- **Meaning:** To dismantle, to clear away, to remove barriers (Tháo dỡ công trình / Xóa bỏ rào cản, định kiến).
- **Trigger:** Demolishing old huts physically, or removing prejudice/barriers abstractly (`古い小屋を取り払う`, `偏見を取り払う`).
- **Contrast:** `取り払う` means clearing away structures or prejudice; `取り壊す` specifically means demolishing buildings completely.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for dismantling physical structures or clearing away abstract barriers.
- **Usage:** 建物や障害物、または偏見などの障壁を取り除くことを表す。(Expresses removing buildings, obstacles, or abstract barriers like prejudice.)
- **Example:** 古い小屋を取り払って、駐車場にした。(We dismantled the old shed and made it a parking lot. / Phá bỏ cái nhà kho cũ đi để làm bãi đỗ xe.)

CompoundVerb | 持ち寄る

- **Meaning:** To bring together, to pool resources/food (Góp lại, mang đồ đến góp chung).
- **Trigger:** Each person bringing food, ideas, or funds to share (`食べ物を持ち寄る`, `アイデアを持ち寄る`).
- **Contrast:** `持ち寄る` implies bringing items from individual places to pool them; `集める` means collecting items neutrally.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for individuals bringing food or ideas together to pool or share.
- **Usage:** 各自が持ち寄って一箇所に集める様子を表す。(Expresses individuals bringing items together to one place to pool.)
- **Example:** 互いに食べ物を持ち寄った。(Everyone brought food to share. / Mọi người mang đồ ăn đến góp chung.)

CompoundVerb | 問い合わせる

- **Meaning:** To inquire, to make an inquiry, to contact for information (Liên hệ để giải đáp thắc mắc, hỏi thông tin).
- **Trigger:** Inquiring at government offices, customer service, or helpdesks (`区役所に問い合わせる`).
- **Contrast:** `問い合わせる` means officially making inquiries for information; `質問する` means asking a question neutrally.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for contacting an institution or service to make formal inquiries.
- **Usage:** 役所や窓口に連絡して情報を確認・質問することを表す。(Expresses contacting an office or counter to verify information or ask questions.)
- **Example:** 住民登録について、区役所に問い合わせた。(I inquired at the ward office about resident registration. / Tôi đã hỏi/liên hệ ủy ban quận về việc đăng ký cư trú.)

CompoundVerb | 見渡す

- **Meaning:** To look over, to survey, to scan a whole process/landscape (Nhìn bao quát, quan sát toàn bộ).
- **Trigger:** Surveying a landscape from above or looking over an entire production process (`工程全体を見渡す`).
- **Contrast:** `見渡す` means taking a broad panoramic view across an area or process; `見下ろす` means looking down from a high point.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for surveying or scanning an entire wide area or complete process.
- **Usage:** 広い範囲や全体をくまなく視界に収めて観察することを表す。(Expresses taking in a wide range or whole process into view to observe.)
- **Example:** 工程全体を見渡して、不具合のある個所を修正した。(I looked over the entire process and fixed defective parts. / Quan sát toàn bộ quy trình và đã sửa các chỗ có lỗi.)

CompoundVerb | 飛び上がる

- **Meaning:** To jump up, to leap into the air (Bay vút lên / Nhảy cẫn lên vì ngạc nhiên/vui mừng).
- **Trigger:** Birds taking flight or jumping up in excitement (`鳥が飛び上がる`, `喜んで飛び上がる`).
- **Contrast:** `飛び上がる` means jumping straight up in excitement or birds taking off; `飛び降りる` means jumping down.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for leaping upward in excitement or birds launching into flight.
- **Usage:** 空中へ跳びはねる、または喜んで跳び上がる動きを表す。(Expresses leaping into the air or jumping up with joy.)
- **Example:** 喜んで飛び上がった。(I leaped up with joy. / Tôi nhảy cẫng lên vì vui mừng.)

CompoundVerb | 立ち寄る

- **Meaning:** To stop by, to drop in, to make a short detour (Tạt qua, ghé qua).
- **Trigger:** Dropping by a convenience store or friend's house on the way home (`帰りにコンビニに立ち寄る`).
- **Contrast:** `立ち寄る` means making a brief stop on a journey; `立ち去る` means walking away or leaving a spot.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for making a brief visit or stopover on the way somewhere.
- **Usage:** 目的地への途中でついでに短時間訪れることを表す。(Expresses making a short visit on the way to a destination.)
- **Example:** 帰りにコンビニに立ち寄った。(I dropped by the convenience store on the way home. / Trên đường về tôi ghé qua cửa hàng tiện lợi.)

CompoundVerb | 受け入れる

- **Meaning:** To accept, to receive, to embrace rules/refugees (Tiếp nhận, chấp nhận luật lệ/người mới).
- **Trigger:** Accepting country rules, foreign students, or proposals (`日本のルールを受け入れる`).
- **Contrast:** `受け入れる` means accepting/embracing rules or people into a system; `受け取る` means physically receiving a package.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for accepting or embracing rules, people, or proposals into a group.
- **Usage:** 条件やルール、人などを承諾して迎え入れることを表す。(Expresses consenting to and welcoming conditions, rules, or people.)
- **Example:** 日本に行ったら、日本のルールを受け入れなさい。(When you go to Japan, accept Japan's rules. / Khi sang Nhật hãy chấp nhận luật lệ của Nhật.)

CompoundVerb | 乗り切る

- **Meaning:** To weather, to get through, to overcome a crisis/hardship (Vượt qua khó khăn/khủng hoảng).
- **Trigger:** Overcoming economic crisis, heat waves, or difficulties as a group (`危機を乗り切る`, `暑さを乗り切る`).
- **Contrast:** `乗り切る` emphasizes getting through a tough period/crisis safely; `乗り越える` emphasizes scaling over an obstacle.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for safely weathering or surviving a crisis or difficult phase.
- **Usage:** 困難や危機を最後まで無事に経過させることを表す。(Expresses passing through difficulty or crisis safely to the end.)
- **Example:** みんなでこの危機を乗り切ろう。(Let's get through this crisis together. / Hãy cùng nhau vượt qua khủng hoảng này.)

CompoundVerb | 呼び止める

- **Meaning:** To call back, to flag down, to stop someone by calling out (Gọi quay lại, gọi giật lại).
- **Trigger:** A teacher calling a student back before they leave (`先生に呼び止められた`).
- **Contrast:** `呼び止める` means stopping someone walking away by calling out; `引き止める` means physically holding someone back.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for stopping someone walking away by shouting or calling out.
- **Usage:** 立ち去ろうとする声をかけて呼び戻して足を止めさせることを表す。(Expresses calling out to someone leaving to make them stop.)
- **Example:** 学校からの帰り際、先生から話があると呼び止められた。(As I was leaving school, I was called back by the teacher. / Lúc đang rời trường, thầy/cô gọi tôi lại nói chuyện.)

CompoundVerb | 引き離す

- **Meaning:** To pull apart, to separate, to pull ahead of competitors (Tách ra / Bỏ xa đối thủ).
- **Trigger:** Separating a toddler from parents, or pulling far ahead of runner #2 (`2位の選手をぐんぐん引き離す`).
- **Contrast:** `引き離す` means pulling two entities apart or widening a gap; `引き止める` means detaining someone.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for separating entities or widening the distance ahead of a competitor.
- **Usage:** 二つのものを引き離す、または競走で差を広げることを表す。(Expresses separating two things or widening a lead in a race.)
- **Example:** 1位の選手が2位の選手をぐんぐん引き離した。(The first-place runner pulled far ahead of the second-place runner. / Vận động viên hạng nhất bỏ xa người hạng nhì.)

CompoundVerb | 追い返す

- **Meaning:** To turn away, to drive back, to send someone back (Đuổi về, xua đuổi).
- **Trigger:** Turning away unwelcome visitors or intruders at the door (`来た者を追い返す`).
- **Contrast:** `追い返す` means forcing a visitor to turn back and leave; `追いかける` means chasing after someone.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for driving away or refusing entry to visitors, sending them back.
- **Usage:** 来た者を拒否して帰らせる動作を表す。(Expresses refusing someone who came and sending them back.)
- **Example:** 来た者を、追い立てて帰らせた。(They turned away those who came and sent them back. / Đuổi người đã đến về.)

CompoundVerb | かかわり合う

- **Meaning:** To get involved with, to be tied up with (Có liên quan, dính líu tới).
- **Trigger:** Refusing to get involved in troublesome matters (`面倒なことにはかかわり合いたくない`).
- **Contrast:** `かかわり合う` implies mutual connection or getting entangled in affairs; `関わる` is neutral involvement.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for getting entangled or involved in relationships or troublesome affairs.
- **Usage:** 他人の問題や複雑な事情に関係を持つことを表す。(Expresses having a connection or involvement with others' issues or matters.)
- **Example:** 面倒なことにはかかわり合いたくない。(I don't want to get involved in troublesome matters. / Tôi không muốn dính líu tới chuyện rắc rối.)

CompoundVerb | 打ち明ける

- **Meaning:** To confide, to reveal a secret, to open one's heart (Bộc bạch, thổ lộ bí mật).
- **Trigger:** Confiding a personal secret or true feelings to a trusted friend (`秘密を打ち明ける`).
- **Contrast:** `打ち明ける` means revealing a secret/confiding; `打ち合わせる` means holding an advance planning meeting.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for opening one's heart to confide a secret or true feeling.
- **Usage:** 心の中に隠していた秘密や本心を打ち明けて話すことを表す。(Expresses opening up and telling hidden secrets or true feelings.)
- **Example:** 私の秘密を打ち明けることにします。(I decided to confide my secret. / Tôi quyết định thổ lộ bí mật của mình.)

Weak Points

1. **Phân biệt cặp động từ ghép di chuyển** **立ち寄る** **vs** **立ち去る****:**
    - Cần phân biệt rõ: `立ち寄る` (ghé qua cửa hàng/nhà bạn trên đường) vs `立ち去る` (bỏ đi khỏi hiện trường).
2. **Phân biệt sự khác nhau giữa** **乗り切る** **và** **乗り越える****:**
    - `乗り切る` mang nghĩa an toàn vượt qua một _giai đoạn/khủng hoảng_ (thời gian), còn `乗り越える` mang nghĩa chinh phục _chướng ngại vật_ (đối tượng/hoàn cảnh cụ thể).
3. **Âm đọc Furigana và Hán Việt chuẩn:**
    - Phản xạ chính xác âm đọc Kana: `とりかかる` (取りかかる), `ふりむく` (振り向く), `とりはらう` (取り払う), `もちよる` (持ち寄る), `といあわせる` (問い合わせる), `よびとめる` (呼び止める), `ひきはなす` (引き離す), `うちあける` (打ち明ける).
