Error Analysis

Dưới đây là phân tích chi tiết các bẫy động từ ghép và nguyên nhân gây sai sót phổ biến khi làm bài thi JLPT N2 (Mondai 1 - Cách đọc Kanji, Mondai 4 - Cách dùng từ, Mondai 5 - Từ đồng nghĩa):

1. Nhầm lẫn giữa các động từ ghép chứa tiền tố `見〜` (`見極める`, `見抜く`, `見逃す`, `見計らう`)

- **Bẫy thi:** Đề thi N2 thường đưa các động từ có chung tiền tố `見〜` vào phương án lựa chọn:
    - `見極める` (みきわめる): Nhìn rõ bản chất, xác định thật giả (`本物かどうかを見極める`).
    - `見抜く` (みぬく): Nhìn thấu sự thật ẩn giấu / lời nói dối (`嘘を見抜く`).
    - `見逃す` (みのがす): Bỏ lỡ cơ hội / cố tình làm ngơ bỏ qua lỗi (`チャンスを見逃す`).
    - `見計らう` (みはからう): Canh, chọn thời điểm thích hợp (`相手の様子を見計らう`).
- **Nguyên nhân & Phân biệt:** Học viên dễ nhầm lẫn do đều mang nét nghĩa "nhìn". Cần phân biệt hậu tố: `極める` (đến cùng ➔ xác định bản chất); `抜く` (xuyên qua ➔ nhìn thấu sự thật); `逃す` (nới tay ➔ bỏ qua/bỏ lỡ); `計らう` (suy tính ➔ canh thời điểm).

2. Nhầm lẫn sắc thái nghĩa đen và nghĩa ẩn dụ của `踏み込む` và `寄りかかる`

- **Bẫy thi:**
    - `寄りかかる`: Nghĩa đen là dựa vào tường (`壁に寄りかかる`), nghĩa ẩn dụ là dựa dẫm phụ thuộc cha mẹ (`親に寄りかかる`).
    - `踏み込む`: Nghĩa đen là đạp mạnh phanh (`ブレーキを踏み込む`), nghĩa ẩn dụ là đi sâu thảo luận bản chất vấn đề (`問題に深く踏み込む`).
- **Nguyên nhân sai:** Chỉ nhớ nghĩa thể chất ban đầu mà bỏ qua nét nghĩa mở rộng về tâm lý và lập luận trong bài đọc hiểu N2.

Knowledge Extracted

CompoundVerb | 取り締まる

- **Meaning:** To crack down on, to control, to supervise, to police (Giám sát, kiểm soát, xử lý vi phạm).
- **Trigger:** Used in legal, public order, or organizational contexts regarding violations (`違反行為を取り締まる`).
- **Contrast:** `取り締まる` refers to actively supervising/cracking down on illegal acts; `取り扱う` refers to handling goods or transactions.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for policing, supervising, or cracking down on illegal actions and violations.
- **Usage:** 法律や規則に違反する行為を監視・ can thiệp để ngăn chặn và xử lý.(Used for monitoring and cracking down on illegal or rule-breaking behaviors.)
- **Example:** 警察が違反行為を取り締まっている。(The police are cracking down on traffic violations. / Cảnh sát đang kiểm soát hành vi vi phạm.)

CompoundVerb | 見極める

- **Meaning:** To ascertain, to see through clearly, to judge correctly (Nhìn rõ bản chất, xác định rõ thật giả).
- **Trigger:** Used when evaluating the true nature, authenticity, or truth of something (`本物かどうかを見極める`).
- **Contrast:** `見極める` ascertains authenticity/truth; `見抜く` sees through deception/lies; `見分ける` distinguishes between two items.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for carefully judging and ascertaining the true nature or authenticity of something.
- **Usage:** 事物の本質や真偽、状況を見つめて確実に見定めることを表す。(Expresses scrutinizing and ascertaining the true nature or validity of something.)
- **Example:** 本物かどうかを見極める。(To ascertain whether it is genuine or fake. / Xác định xem đó có phải hàng thật hay không.)

CompoundVerb | 寄りかかる

- **Meaning:** To lean against, to depend on, to rely on (Dựa vào tường / Dựa dẫm vào người khác).
- **Trigger:** Physical leaning on a wall or psychological reliance on parents/others (`壁に寄りかかる`, `親に寄りかかる`).
- **Contrast:** `寄りかかる` involves physically leaning or relying heavily on others; `頼る` means seeking support neutrally.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for leaning physically against a structure or depending heavily on someone.
- **Usage:** 身体を物にをもたれかけさせる、または他人を当てにして依存することを表す。(Expresses physically leaning against an object or emotionally depending on someone.)
- **Example:** 壁に寄りかかって休んだ。(I rested leaning against the wall. / Tôi dựa vào tường để nghỉ.)

CompoundVerb | 見逃す

- **Meaning:** To overlook, to miss an opportunity, to turn a blind eye (Bỏ lỡ cơ hội, không để ý thấy, nhắm mắt bỏ qua).
- **Trigger:** Missing a chance, overlooking small mistakes, or letting an offender off (`チャンスを見逃す`, `小さなミスを見逃す`).
- **Contrast:** `見逃す` can be intentional (turning a blind eye) or missing an opportunity; `見落とす` is strictly unintentional oversight.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for missing a chance, failing to notice, or deliberately overlooking a minor fault.
- **Usage:** 機会を逸する、過失に気づかない、または寛大に見て許すことを表す。(Expresses missing a chance, failing to notice a flaw, or intentionally overlooking a fault.)
- **Example:** チャンスを見逃した。(I missed the opportunity. / Tôi đã bỏ lỡ cơ hội.)

CompoundVerb | 見せびらかす

- **Meaning:** To show off, to flaunt, to boast (Khoe khoang đồ mới/đắt tiền).
- **Trigger:** Flaunting expensive items, wealth, or achievements to others (`高級時計を見せびらかす`).
- **Contrast:** `見せびらかす` carries a negative/boastful connotation; `見せる` is simply showing neutrally.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for boastfully displaying expensive possessions or achievements to elicit envy.
- **Usage:** 自慢したい物を人に見せて誇る動作を表す。(Expresses proudly displaying possessions to others to show off.)
- **Example:** 高級時計を見せびらかしていた。(He was showing off his expensive watch. / Anh ta khoe chiếc đồng hồ đắt tiền.)

CompoundVerb | 呼び込む

- **Meaning:** To invite in, to attract, to bring in (Mời gọi khách hàng, mang lại may mắn).
- **Trigger:** Attracting customers into a shop or bringing good fortune/luck (`客を呼び込む`, `幸運を呼び込む`).
- **Contrast:** `呼び込む` focuses on drawing people/luck inside a space; `招く` means inviting guests formally.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for inviting or drawing customers inside or bringing in good fortune.
- **Usage:** 人を誘い入れる、または幸運などの良い結果を引き寄せることを表す。(Expresses drawing people inside or attracting positive outcomes like luck.)
- **Example:** 店員が客を呼び込んでいる。(The clerk is inviting customers inside the shop. / Nhân viên đang mời khách vào quán.)

CompoundVerb | 見抜く

- **Meaning:** To see through, to perceive hidden truth, to unmask (Nhìn thấu dối trá/sự thật ẩn giấu).
- **Trigger:** Uncovering lies, secrets, or true intentions behind expressions (`嘘を見抜く`, `本心を見抜く`).
- **Contrast:** `見抜く` specifically targets seeing through hidden lies/secrets; `見極める` ascertains true identity/quality.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for seeing through lies, deceptions, or secret motives to uncover the truth.
- **Usage:** 隠されている真実や本心、嘘を見やぶって知ることを表す。(Expresses seeing through hidden truth, secrets, or lies to perceive them clearly.)
- **Example:** 彼は私の嘘を見抜いていた。(He saw right through my lie. / Anh ấy nhìn thấu lời nói dối của tôi.)

CompoundVerb | 見計らう

- **Meaning:** To choose a good time, to judge timing, to select appropriately (Canh/chọn thời điểm thích hợp).
- **Trigger:** Timing a moment to speak or selecting suitable items/time (`相手の様子を見計らう`, `時間を見計らう`).
- **Contrast:** `見計らう` is timing an action or selecting wisely based on situation; `計らう` is managing/arranging affairs.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for picking the optimal moment or making an appropriate selection based on context.
- **Usage:** 状況に合わせて適当な時期や物事を選び定めることを表す。(Expresses picking a suitable time or choice matching the situation.)
- **Example:** 相手の様子を見計らって話しかけた。(I timed the right moment watching their reaction before speaking. / Tôi canh thời điểm thích hợp rồi bắt chuyện.)

CompoundVerb | やっつける

- **Meaning:** To defeat, to beat down, to finish off (Hạ gục kẻ địch, kết liễu).
- **Trigger:** Defeating enemies, opponents in games, or finishing a tough task (`敵をやっつける`).
- **Contrast:** `やっつける` means beating an enemy or rushing to finish work; `倒す` means knocking down neutrally.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for decisively beating down an opponent or vigorously finishing off a task.
- **Usage:** 相手を攻撃して打ち負かす、または仕事を一気に片付けることを表す。(Expresses attacking and defeating an opponent, or rushing through work.)
- **Example:** 敵をやっつけた。(We defeated the enemy. / Đã hạ gục kẻ địch.)

CompoundVerb | 結びつく

- **Meaning:** To be connected with, to lead to, to be tied to (Kết nối, dẫn đến thành công/liên quan).
- **Trigger:** Effort leading to success, or events tied to political issues (`努力が成功に結びつく`, `政治問題に結びつく`).
- **Contrast:** `結びつく` is intransitive (be linked/lead to); `結びつける` is transitive (to connect two things).
- **One-line Summary:** Intransitive compound verb for being directly connected to or resulting in an outcome.
- **Usage:** 物事が関連を持つ、または結果としてある状態につながる様子を表す。(Expresses things having a connection or resulting in a certain state.)
- **Example:** 努力が成功に結びついた。(Hard work led to success. / Nỗ lực đã dẫn đến thành công.)

CompoundVerb | 踏み込む

- **Meaning:** To step hard into, to delve deeply into, to intrude (Đạp mạnh phanh xe, đi sâu vào vấn đề).
- **Trigger:** Pressing down hard on pedals or delving deeply into complex discussions (`ブレーキを強く踏み込む`, `問題に深く踏み込む`).
- **Contrast:** `踏み込む` means stepping into/delving deep into issues; `入り込む` means simply entering a space.
- **One-line Summary:** Compound verb for stepping down hard on a pedal or probing deeply into sensitive topics.
- **Usage:** 足を強く踏み下ろす、または問題の核心に深入りして議論することを表す。(Expresses stepping down hard with foot, or probing deeply into the core of a problem.)
- **Example:** その問題に深く踏み込んで議論した。(We stepped deeply into that issue and discussed it. / Chúng tôi thảo luận sâu về vấn đề đó.)
