Reading | 「寝る子は育つ」の理由

- **Meaning:** Explaining why "sleeping children grow" based on growth hormone secretion during deep sleep / Giải thích lý do vì sao "trẻ ngủ nhiều sẽ mau lớn" dựa trên sự tiết hormone tăng trưởng khi ngủ sâu.
- **Trigger:** Question asking "なぜか" about a proverb in a short passage: `筆者によれば、「寝る子は育つ」のはなぜか。`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from Option 4 (confusing non-REM with REM sleep based on text definition) and Option 1 (focusing solely on fatigue recovery).
- **One-line Summary:** Children grow when sleeping because growth hormone is heavily secreted during deep sleep.

Vocabulary | 格言

- **Meaning:** Proverb, maxim, saying / Khẩu hiệu, châm ngôn, câu nói truyền miệng có ý nghĩa.
- **Trigger:** Referring to traditional wisdom or sayings: `この格言には、科学的な根拠があります`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `名言` (famous quote by a famous person) and `方言` (regional dialect).
- **One-line Summary:** Traditional proverb or wise saying passed down through generations.
- **Correct Synonym:** 諺
- **Distractor1:** 迷言
- **Distractor2:** 方言
- **Distractor3:** 遺言
- **Explanation:** `諺` (ことわざ) is a direct synonym for proverb/maxim. `迷言` means bizarre quote, `方言` means dialect, and `遺言` means last testament/will.

Vocabulary | 分泌

- **Meaning:** Secretion (of hormones/fluids) / Sự tiết ra (hormone, dịch cơ thể).
- **Trigger:** Biological or medical context regarding hormones: `成長ホルモンが多く分泌される`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `排出` (excretion/discharge of waste) and `吸収` (absorption).
- **One-line Summary:** Biological process of releasing hormones or fluids in the body.
- **Correct Synonym:** 放出
- **Distractor1:** 吸収
- **Distractor2:** 消化
- **Distractor3:** 感染
- **Explanation:** `放出` or `内分泌` refers to bodily secretion/release. `吸収` (absorption), `消化` (digestion), and `感染` (infection) represent completely different biological processes.

Vocabulary | 熟睡

- **Meaning:** Deep sleep, sound sleep / Giấc ngủ sâu, ngủ say.
- **Trigger:** Describing deep, uninterrupted sleep condition: `熟睡しているときに`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `浅い睡眠` (light sleep) and `仮眠` (nap/catnap).
- **One-line Summary:** Sound and deep sleep state essential for physical recovery.
- **Correct Synonym:** 深睡眠
- **Distractor1:** 浅眠
- **Distractor2:** 徹夜
- **Distractor3:** 居眠り
- **Explanation:** `深睡眠` means deep sleep. `浅眠` (light sleep) is the antonym, `徹夜` means staying up all night, and `居眠り` means dozing off.

Adverb | スクスク

- **Meaning:** Rapidly, healthily, briskly growing (of children/plants) / (Lớn/phát triển) nhanh chóng, vù vù, khỏe mạnh.
- **Trigger:** Combined with growth verbs like `育つ` or `成長する`: `子供は、スクスクと成長していく`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `すらすら` (smoothly solving/speaking) and `すっきり` (refreshed).
- **Usage:** Phó từ tượng hình `スクスク` (thường đi với `と`) dùng để diễn tả sự lớn lên, phát triển vọt lên nhanh chóng và khỏe mạnh của trẻ em hoặc cây trồng.
- **Example:** 赤ちゃんは病気もせずスクスクと育っている。(The baby is growing rapidly and healthily without getting sick. / Em bé lớn nhanh vù vù khỏe mạnh mà không bị ốm.)
- **One-line Summary:** Growing up rapidly and healthily (applied to children or young plants).

FixedExpression | 寝る子は育つ

- **Meaning:** Children who sleep grow well / Trẻ ngủ nhiều thì mau lớn (Thành ngữ/châm ngôn dân gian).
- **Trigger:** Quote at the start of wellness or child development articles: `昔から「寝る子は育つ」とよく言います`.
- **Usage:** Thành ngữ `寝る子は育つ` dùng để động viên trẻ em ngủ đủ giấc và giải thích cơ chế phát triển thể chất lành mạnh.
- **Example:** 「寝る子は育つ」と言うから、早く寝なさい。(They say "sleeping children grow well", so go to bed early! / Dân gian có câu "trẻ ngủ nhiều thì mau lớn", nên hãy ngủ sớm đi!)
- **Contrast:** Differs from general health idioms like `早寝早起` (early to bed, early to rise).
- **One-line Summary:** Traditional Japanese proverb stating that adequate sleep promotes healthy child growth.

FixedExpression | 〜はもちろん

- **Meaning:** Not to mention..., let alone..., of course... / Không chỉ... mà còn..., đương nhiên là...
- **Trigger:** Pattern `Nは/Vのละ/Sentenceはもちろん、〜も`: `疲労回復に効果があるのはもちろん、成長期にある子供は...`.
- **Usage:** Cụm cố định `〜はもちろん` dùng để khẳng định vế trước là đương nhiên, từ đó nhấn mạnh thêm vế sau còn có tác dụng/ý nghĩa hơn thế nữa.
- **Example:** この野菜は栄養が豊富なのはもちろん、味も美味しい。(This vegetable is not only rich in nutrition, but also tastes delicious. / Loại rau này không chỉ giàu dinh dưỡng mà vị cũng rất ngon.)
- **Contrast:** Differs from `〜ばかりか` (not only X but also Y) and `〜をはじめ` (starting with X).
- **One-line Summary:** States that the first point is obvious while emphasizing the second point.

Grammar | 〜によって

- **Meaning:** By means of, due to, through / Bằng, nhờ vào, do (nguyên nhân/phương tiện).
- **Trigger:** Noun indicating a cause, condition, or means followed by `によって`: `深い睡眠によって、成長ホルモンがよく分泌されれば`.
- **Contrast:** `〜を通じて` (through an intermediary over time) vs `〜によって` (direct cause/means/condition).
- **Formation:** N ＋ によって / により
- **Semantic Field:** Means / Cause / Basis (Phương tiện, Nguyên nhân, Căn cứ).
- **Related Grammar:** `〜を通じて`, `〜によって`
- **Confusion Index:** `〜によって ↔ 〜を通じて`
- **Why Wrong:** Người làm chọn sai câu hỏi do đọc lướt và không nắm bắt được điều kiện trực tiếp `深い睡眠によって` dẫn đến sự tiết hormone.
- **Why Correct:** `深い睡眠によって` diễn tả nguyên nhân/điều kiện trực tiếp để tiết hormone tăng trưởng tốt nhất.
- **Example:** 深い睡眠によって、成長ホルモンがよく分泌される。(Through deep sleep, growth hormones are well secreted.)
- **One-line Summary:** Indicates the cause, condition, or direct means bringing about a result.
