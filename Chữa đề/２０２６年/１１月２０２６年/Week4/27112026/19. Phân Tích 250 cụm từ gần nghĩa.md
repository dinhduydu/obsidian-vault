Error Analysis

- **Lỗi nhầm lẫn nhóm từ ghép bắt đầu bằng chữ 「手-」 (Hand/Task Prefixes):**
    - Học viên rất dễ bị rối giữa các danh từ/động từ ghép có tiền tố `手-`:
        - `手薄` (thiếu hụt nhân lực/không đủ - tương đương `不十分`).
        - `手はず` (sắp xếp/chuẩn bị - tương đương `準備`).
        - `手引き` (hướng dẫn/sách hướng dẫn - tương đương `マニュアル`).
        - `手分け` (phân công/chia nhau làm - tương đương `分担`).
    - Dấu hiệu nhận biết: Các câu hỏi điền từ vào khoảng trống đòi hỏi phân biệt rõ giữa "hành động chuẩn bị trước" (`手はず`), "tài liệu chỉ dẫn" (`手引き`), "sự phân chia lực lượng" (`手分け`) và "trạng thái thiếu hụt" (`手薄`).
- **Lỗi nhầm lẫn sắc thái từ Hán tự / Từ cổ mang tính văn học:**
    - `告げる` (thông báo/cho hay) là dạng văn phong viết trang trọng của `知らせる`, dễ bị dùng nhầm với các động từ phát ngôn thông thường.
    - `償い` (sự đền bù/bù đắp/chuộc lỗi) tương đương `補償`, dễ chọn nhầm sang các từ chỉ sự trừng phạt (`処罰`) hoặc gây hại (`加害`).
    - `天辺` (đỉnh/điểm cao nhất) tương đương `頂上` / `頂点`, dễ nhầm với từ chỉ chân núi hay phần đáy (`底`).
- **Lỗi phạm vi ý nghĩa của từ vựng N2/N1:**
    - `募る` có hai nét nghĩa chính: "kêu gọi/tuyển mộ" (`広く呼びかけて集める`) và "tình cảm/cảm xúc dâng trào" (ví dụ: lo lắng, cô đơn). Học viên thường chỉ nhớ nghĩa tuyển mộ mà bỏ qua ngữ cảnh cảm xúc.
    - `重宝する` (trọng dụng/hữu ích) tương đương `便利で役に立つ`, dễ bị nhầm nghĩa với việc đánh giá cao tư cách con người thay vì sự tiện lợi của đồ vật/kỹ năng.

Knowledge Extracted

Vocabulary | 重宝する

- **Meaning:** Useful, handy, or valued for convenience; in Vietnamese: hữu ích, tiện lợi, được trọng dụng vì có ích.
- **Trigger:** Expressing appreciation for tools, items, or skills that come in handy in daily life or work.
- **Contrast:** `重宝する` emphasizes practical usefulness and valuing an item/tool (`便利で役に立つ`), whereas `尊重する` means respecting a person or opinion.
- **One-line Summary:** Finding something very useful, convenient, and handy to use.
- **Correct Synonym:** 便利で役に立つ
- **Distractor1:** 邪魔になる
- **Distractor2:** 粗末にする
- **Distractor3:** 無視する
- **Explanation:** `便利で役に立つ` accurately reflects being useful and handy. `邪魔になる` means getting in the way, `粗末にする` means treating carelessly, and `無視する` means ignoring.

Vocabulary | 償い

- **Meaning:** Compensation, atonement, or reparation; in Vietnamese: sự đền bù, bù đắp, chuộc lỗi.
- **Trigger:** Making up for past mistakes, damages, or injuries caused to others.
- **Contrast:** `償い` refers specifically to atonement or compensation for harm/loss (`補償`), whereas `報い` can mean retribution or punishment.
- **One-line Summary:** Atonement or compensation to make amends for a wrong.
- **Correct Synonym:** 補償
- **Distractor1:** 処罰
- **Distractor2:** 放置
- **Distractor3:** 加害
- **Explanation:** `補償` means compensation or indemnification for loss. `処罰` means punishment, `放置` means leaving unattended, and `加害` means inflicting harm.

Vocabulary | 告げる

- **Meaning:** To inform, announce, or tell; in Vietnamese: thông báo, báo cho biết, cho hay.
- **Trigger:** Formally announcing news, telling decisions, or signalling the arrival of an event.
- **Contrast:** `告げる` is a formal/literary verb to inform or announce (`知らせる`), whereas `隠す` means to conceal.
- **One-line Summary:** Formally informing, announcing, or conveying news.
- **Correct Synonym:** 知らせる
- **Distractor1:** 隠す
- **Distractor2:** 黙る
- **Distractor3:** 偽る
- **Explanation:** `知らせる` means to inform or notify. `隠す` means to hide, `黙る` means to remain silent, and `偽る` means to lie/falsify.

Vocabulary | つつましい

- **Meaning:** Modest, reserved, or humble; in Vietnamese: khiêm nhường, giản dị, không phô trương.
- **Trigger:** Describing a modest lifestyle, humble attitude, or reserved behavior.
- **Contrast:** `つつましい` emphasizes quiet modesty and restraint (`控えめだ`), whereas `図々しい` means impudent or brazen.
- **One-line Summary:** Living or behaving modestly and humbly without ostentation.
- **Correct Synonym:** 控えめだ
- **Distractor1:** 図々しい
- **Distractor2:** 派手だ
- **Distractor3:** 傲慢だ
- **Explanation:** `控えめだ` means reserved or restrained. `図々しい` means brazen, `派手だ` means showy/flashy, and `傲慢だ` means arrogant.

Vocabulary | 募る

- **Meaning:** To recruit, invite applications, or grow stronger (feelings); in Vietnamese: kêu gọi, tuyển mộ, tập hợp (hoặc tình cảm dâng trào).
- **Trigger:** Publicly inviting applicants for jobs/contributions, or feelings of anxiety/loneliness intensifying.
- **Contrast:** `募る` means publicly calling for participants or gathering (`広く呼びかけて集める`), whereas `解散する` means disbanding.
- **One-line Summary:** Publicly recruiting or gathering applicants, or feelings swelling up.
- **Correct Synonym:** 広く呼びかけて集める
- **Distractor1:** 解散する
- **Distractor2:** 追放する
- **Distractor3:** 削減する
- **Explanation:** `広く呼びかけて集める` accurately expresses calling out broadly to collect/recruit. `解散する` means to disband, `追放する` means to expel, and `削減する` means to cut/reduce.

Vocabulary | 手薄

- **Meaning:** Short-handed, insufficient, or inadequately staffed; in Vietnamese: thiếu hụt, không đủ nhân lực hoặc nguồn lực.
- **Trigger:** Security, staffing, or defenses being undermanned or inadequately prepared.
- **Contrast:** `手薄` means insufficient personnel/resources (`不十分`), whereas `手厚い` means generous or lavish.
- **One-line Summary:** Being short-handed or inadequately resourced/staffed.
- **Correct Synonym:** 不十分
- **Distractor1:** 充分
- **Distractor2:** 手厚い
- **Distractor3:** 完璧
- **Explanation:** `不十分` means insufficient or inadequate. `充分` means sufficient, `手厚い` means lavish/generous, and `完璧` means perfect.

Vocabulary | 天辺

- **Meaning:** Top, summit, or peak; in Vietnamese: đỉnh, điểm cao nhất.
- **Trigger:** Reaching the top of a mountain, top of the head, or apex of a structure.
- **Contrast:** `天辺` is a colloquial word for top or peak (`頂上`, `頂点`), whereas `底` refers to the bottom.
- **One-line Summary:** The very top, summit, or highest point of something.
- **Correct Synonym:** 頂上
- **Distractor1:** 底
- **Distractor2:** 起点
- **Distractor3:** 内部
- **Explanation:** `頂上` means peak or summit. `底` means bottom, `起点` means starting point, and `内部` means inside.

Vocabulary | 手はず

- **Meaning:** Arrangements, plan, or preparation; in Vietnamese: sự chuẩn bị, sắp xếp trước.
- **Trigger:** Making prior arrangements for events, trips, operations, or procedures.
- **Contrast:** `手はず` refers to advance planning and arrangements (`準備`), whereas `本番` refers to the actual performance/event.
- **One-line Summary:** Prior arrangements and setup for an upcoming event or plan.
- **Correct Synonym:** 準備
- **Distractor1:** 本番
- **Distractor2:** 結果
- **Distractor3:** 中断
- **Explanation:** `準備` means preparation or setup. `本番` means the actual performance, `結果` means result, and `中断` means interruption.

Vocabulary | 手引き

- **Meaning:** Guidance, manual, or guidebook; in Vietnamese: sự hướng dẫn, tài liệu hướng dẫn.
- **Trigger:** Using a guide, handbook, or manual to learn how to operate a system or navigate a process.
- **Contrast:** `手引き` refers to instructional guidance or manual (`マニュアル`), whereas `障害` refers to an obstacle.
- **One-line Summary:** A manual or guide providing instruction and direction.
- **Correct Synonym:** マニュアル
- **Distractor1:** 障害
- **Distractor2:** 妨害
- **Distractor3:** 迷路
- **Explanation:** `マニュアル` means manual or guide. `障害` means obstacle, `妨害` means interference, and `迷路` means maze.

Vocabulary | 手分け

- **Meaning:** Division of labor, splitting up work; in Vietnamese: phân chia công việc, phân công nhau làm.
- **Trigger:** Multiple people dividing tasks to complete a search, cleanup, or project efficiently.
- **Contrast:** `手分け` means dividing up tasks among group members (`分担`), whereas `独占` means monopolizing work alone.
- **One-line Summary:** Splitting up and dividing work among group members.
- **Correct Synonym:** 分担
- **Distractor1:** 独占
- **Distractor2:** 丸投げ
- **Distractor3:** 放任
- **Explanation:** `分担` means division of labor/sharing tasks. `独占` means monopoly, `丸投げ` means dumping work entirely on someone else, and `放任` means non-interference.
