Knowledge Extracted

Vocabulary | 名高い（なだかい）

- **Meaning:** Famous, well-known, renowned / Nổi tiếng, có danh tiếng lớn.
- **Trigger:** Used when introducing individuals, places, historical sites, or artistic works that enjoy widespread fame and high reputation.
- **Contrast:** Distinguishes from 著名 (scholarly or authoritative fame) and 評判 (reputation, which can be either positive or negative).
- **One-line Summary:** High fame or renown attributed to a person, place, or thing.
- **Correct Synonym:** 有名（ゆうめい）
- **Distractor1:** 無名（むめい）
- **Distractor2:** 平凡（へいぼん）
- **Distractor3:** 未知（みち）
- **Explanation:** 名高い and 有名 both mean famous or well-known. 無名 means anonymous/unknown, 平凡 means ordinary, and 未知 means unknown.

Adverb | なにかと

- **Meaning:** In various ways, one way or another / Về nhiều mặt, việc này việc kia, bằng cách này hay cách khác.
- **Trigger:** Contexts expressing gratitude for multi-faceted support or describing ongoing care and involvement across different matters.
- **Contrast:** Differs from いろいろ (simply "various things") by carrying a nuanced tone of personal attention or multi-sided kindness/assistance.
- **One-line Summary:** Dealing with or expressing care regarding multiple varied matters.
- **Usage:** Dùng trong lời cảm ơn hoặc diễn tả sự quan tâm, giúp đỡ về nhiều mặt khác nhau trong đời sống/công việc.
- **Example:** なにかとお世話になりました。 (Thank you for taking care of me in so many ways.)

Adverb | 何卒（なにとぞ）

- **Meaning:** Kindly, please, humbly request / Kính mong, rất mong, xin vui lòng.
- **Trigger:** Formal business correspondence, official emails, or polite closing phrases requesting favor or understanding from the listener.
- **Contrast:** Significantly more formal and solemn than どうぞ or お願いします.
- **One-line Summary:** Formal and polite adverb used to express earnest requests.
- **Usage:** Dùng trong văn phong kinh doanh trang trọng (thư từ, email) khi đưa ra lời yêu cầu khẩn thiết và lịch sự.
- **Example:** 今後とも何卒よろしくお願いいたします。 (Please continue to favor us in the future.)

Vocabulary | 粘り（ねばり）

- **Meaning:** Persistence, perseverance, tenacity / Sự kiên trì, bền bỉ, nhẫn nại.
- **Trigger:** Describing mental stamina and steadfast effort when facing difficult challenges, long exams, or tough competitions.
- **Contrast:** Differs from 根性 (grit/willpower) by emphasizing sustained, flexible endurance over time until completion.
- **One-line Summary:** Sustained persistence and resistance against giving up under difficulty.
- **Correct Synonym:** 根気（こんき）
- **Distractor1:** 妥協（だきょう）
- **Distractor2:** 断念（だんねん）
- **Distractor3:** 怠惰（たいだ）
- **Explanation:** 粘り and 根気 both refer to patience, perseverance, and persistence. 妥協 means compromise, 断念 means giving up/abandonment, and 怠惰 means laziness.

Vocabulary | 賑わう（にぎわう）

- **Meaning:** To be crowded, bustling, prosperous / Đông đúc, nhộn nhịp, sầm uất.
- **Trigger:** Scenes featuring festive streets, bustling markets, popular tourist spots, or thriving retail stores full of customers.
- **Contrast:** Differs from 混雑する (crowded in a congested or uncomfortable manner) by conveying a positive, lively atmosphere.
- **One-line Summary:** Lively bustling activity in a place with many people or prosperous trade.
- **Correct Synonym:** 繁盛する（はんじょうする）
- **Distractor1:** 衰退する（すいたいする）
- **Distractor2:** 閑散とする（かんさんとする）
- **Distractor3:** 閉鎖する（へいさする）
- **Explanation:** 賑わう and 繁盛する both convey lively activity and commercial prosperity. 閑散とする means deserted/quiet, 衰退する means decline, and 閉鎖する means close down.

CompoundVerb | 乗り越える（のりこえる）

- **Meaning:** To overcome, surmount, get over / Vượt qua, khắc phục (khó khăn, trở ngại, khủng hoảng).
- **Trigger:** Contexts involving hardship, financial crises, emotional loss, or physical barriers that demand resilience to pass.
- **Contrast:** Differs from 乗り越す (to ride past one's intended station) and 乗り換える (to transfer to another train/bus).
- **One-line Summary:** Successfully surmounting difficult challenges or mental/physical barriers.
- **Usage:** Diễn tả việc nỗ lực cùng nhau hoặc tự mình vượt qua khó khăn, nghịch cảnh hay giai đoạn thử thách.
- **Example:** 困難を全員で乗り越えた。 (Everyone worked together to overcome the difficulty.)

Vocabulary | はかどる

- **Meaning:** To make good progress, proceed smoothly / Tiến triển thuận lợi, công việc trôi chảy.
- **Trigger:** Applied when studying, writing reports, or fulfilling work tasks efficiently without unexpected interruptions.
- **Contrast:** Differs from 成功する (to achieve total success) by focusing on the active speed and smoothness of ongoing work.
- **One-line Summary:** Smooth, efficient ongoing progress in work or study tasks.
- **Correct Synonym:** 順調に進む（じゅんちょうにすすむ）
- **Distractor1:** 停滞する（ていたいする）
- **Distractor2:** 遅れる（おくれる）
- **Distractor3:** 中断する（ちゅうだんする）
- **Explanation:** はかどる and 順調に進む both indicate progress moving along smoothly. 停滞する means stagnate, 遅れる means fall behind, and 中断する means interrupt.

Vocabulary | 背景（はいけい）

- **Meaning:** Background, backdrop, underlying context / Bối cảnh, phông nền, hoàn cảnh phía sau.
- **Trigger:** Explaining the historical, social, or economic root context behind an event, or referring to the physical backdrop of an image.
- **Contrast:** Differs from 前提 (prerequisite/assumption) and 原因 (direct immediate cause).
- **One-line Summary:** The underlying background context or visual backdrop of a situation.
- **Correct Synonym:** バック
- **Distractor1:** 前提（ぜんてい）
- **Distractor2:** 結論（けつろん）
- **Distractor3:** 表面（ひょうめん）
- **Explanation:** 背景 and バック both mean background, context, or support behind an event or picture. 前提 means prerequisite, 結論 means conclusion, and 表面 means surface.

Vocabulary | 励ます（はげます）

- **Meaning:** To encourage, cheer up, motivate / Động viên, khích lệ, an ủi.
- **Trigger:** Speaking uplifting words to a friend, colleague, or athlete who has encountered failure or feels downhearted.
- **Contrast:** Differs from 慰める (to console/comfort someone in sorrow) by actively inspiring morale and action forward.
- **One-line Summary:** Actively lifting someone's spirit and urging them forward with encouraging words.
- **Correct Synonym:** 激励する（げきれいする）
- **Distractor1:** けなす
- **Distractor2:** 批判する（ひはんする）
- **Distractor3:** 責める（せめる）
- **Explanation:** 励ます and 激励する both mean to encourage and invigorate someone. けなす means speak ill of, 批判する means criticize, and 責める means blame.

Katakana | バックアップ

- **Meaning:** Backup, support, assistance / Hỗ trợ, trợ giúp, hậu thuẫn, sao lưu dữ liệu.
- **Trigger:** Business backing, organizational financial aid, or saving duplicate copies of digital files in IT contexts.
- **Contrast:** Differs from メイン (main/primary focus) and 妨害 (obstruction/interference).
- **One-line Summary:** Providing supportive backing, financial help, or data duplicate.
- **Usage:** Dùng để chỉ sự trợ giúp, hậu thuẫn đằng sau cho ai đó hoặc việc sao lưu dữ liệu hệ thống.
- **Example:** チーム全員が彼をバックアップした。 (The whole team backed him up.)

Weak Points

- **Hạn chế thống kê:** Do chưa có kết quả thi trắc nghiệm thực tế của người học, chưa thể đưa ra điểm yếu dựa trên tần suất làm sai thực tế.
- **Nhóm kiến thức dễ bẫy trong kỳ thi JLPT N2–N1:**
    1. **Động từ ghép (Compound Verb):** Phân biệt chính xác giữa `乗り越える` (vượt qua khó khăn/nghịch cảnh) với các động từ ghép có gốc `乗り` như `乗り越す` (đi quá ga) hay `乗り換える` (đổi xe).
    2. **Phó từ kính ngữ & giao tiếp:** Sử dụng `何卒` (trang trọng trong email/văn bản kinh doanh) đúng hoàn cảnh so với `どうぞ` (dùng trong giao tiếp hàng ngày).
    3. **Từ đồng nghĩa Katakana - Hán tự:** Nhận biết mối liên hệ tương đương giữa `バックアップ` và `支援/援助`, cũng như `背景` và `バック`.
