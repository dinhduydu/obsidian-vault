Knowledge Extracted

Vocabulary | 抜群だ（ばつぐんだ）

- **Meaning:** Outstanding, preeminent, extraordinary / Xuất sắc, vượt trội, nổi bật hơn hẳn.
- **Trigger:** Describing exceptional skills, performance, sense of fashion, taste, or academic results superior to others.
- **Contrast:** Differs from 平凡 (mediocre) or 劣等 (inferior); emphasizes standing out clearly above the crowd.
- **One-line Summary:** Outstanding or preeminent quality compared to others.
- **Correct Synonym:** 他と比べて特によい（ほかとくらべてとくによい）
- **Distractor1:** 平凡（へいぼん）
- **Distractor2:** 劣等（れっとう）
- **Distractor3:** 拙劣（せつれつ）
- **Explanation:** 抜群だ and 他と比べて特によい both express preeminent quality. 平凡 means mediocre, 劣等 means inferior, and 拙劣 means clumsy or poor.

CompoundVerb | 張り合う（はりあう）

- **Meaning:** To compete with, rival, vie with / Cạnh tranh, ganh đua, thi đua với nhau.
- **Trigger:** Used when two or more rivals compete fiercely for superiority in sales, games, abilities, or positions.
- **Contrast:** Differs from 協力する (cooperate) or 譲り合う (concede to each other); focuses on active rivalry.
- **One-line Summary:** Competing fiercely with rivals for superiority.
- **Usage:** Dùng khi hai bên hoặc nhiều đối thủ cạnh tranh, ganh đua quyết liệt về tài năng, doanh số hay vị trí.
- **Example:** 互いに技量を張り合っている。 (They are competing fiercely with each other in technical skill.)

Vocabulary | 比喩（ひゆ）

- **Meaning:** Metaphor, figurative expression / Ví von, phép ẩn dụ, cách nói hình tượng.
- **Trigger:** Describing rhetorical figures of speech, analogies, or non-literal expressions used in literature and speech.
- **Contrast:** Differs from 直説 (direct expression) or 事実 (literal fact); relies on symbolic comparison.
- **One-line Summary:** A figurative analogy or metaphorical comparison.
- **Correct Synonym:** 例え（たとえ）
- **Distractor1:** 直説（ちょくせつ）
- **Distractor2:** 事実（じじつ）
- **Distractor3:** 誇張（こちょう）
- **Explanation:** 比喩 and 例え both refer to figurative metaphors or analogies. 直説 is direct expression, 事実 is literal fact, and 誇張 is exaggeration.

Vocabulary | 日和（ひより）

- **Meaning:** Fine weather, ideal weather condition / Thời tiết đẹp, thuận lợi (đặc biệt cho các hoạt động ngoài trời).
- **Trigger:** Usually attached to outdoor activities like 行楽日和 (great weather for outings) or 洗濯日和 (perfect laundry weather).
- **Contrast:** Differs from 悪天候 (bad weather) or 荒天 (stormy weather); indicates sunny and clear sky.
- **One-line Summary:** Clear, sunny, and ideal weather for specific activities.
- **Correct Synonym:** 晴天（せいてん）
- **Distractor1:** 悪天候（あくてんこう）
- **Distractor2:** 荒天（こうてん）
- **Distractor3:** 陰気（いんき）
- **Explanation:** 日和 and 晴天 both mean clear and favorable sunny weather. 悪天候 and 荒天 mean bad/stormy weather, while 陰気 means gloomy.

Adverb | 不意に（ふいに）

- **Meaning:** Unexpectedly, abruptly, out of the blue / Bất ngờ, đột ngột, không báo trước.
- **Trigger:** Used when an event or action occurs suddenly without any prior warning or sign.
- **Contrast:** Differs from 計画的 (planned) or 徐々に (gradually); stresses suddenness.
- **One-line Summary:** Happening unexpectedly without warning or notice.
- **Usage:** Diễn tả hành động hoặc sự việc xảy ra đột ngột, bất ngờ khiến đối phương ngạc nhiên.
- **Example:** 不意に名前を呼ばれて驚いた。 (I was surprised when my name was unexpectedly called.)

Katakana | フォロー

- **Meaning:** Follow-up, assistance, support / Hỗ trợ, bổ trợ, theo dõi và giúp đỡ tiếp.
- **Trigger:** Business or social contexts where one aids a colleague, fixes a mistake, or offers support after an initial action.
- **Contrast:** Differs from 放置 (abandonment/neglect) or 妨害 (interference).
- **One-line Summary:** Providing supportive follow-up aid or assistance.
- **Usage:** Dùng trong công việc hoặc đời sống để chỉ sự giúp đỡ, bổ trợ, xử lý giúp khó khăn cho người khác.
- **Example:** 新人のミスを先輩がフォローした。 (The senior colleague followed up and helped fix the rookie's mistake.)

Vocabulary | 不器用（ぶきよう）

- **Meaning:** Clumsy, awkward, unskillful / Vụng về, không khéo léo.
- **Trigger:** Describing a person who lacks dexterity in manual tasks or is socially awkward/inept in expressing feelings.
- **Contrast:** Differs from 器用 (skillful/dexterous) or 上手 (proficient).
- **One-line Summary:** Lacking dexterity, skill, or tactfulness.
- **Correct Synonym:** 下手（へた）
- **Distractor1:** 器用（きよう）
- **Distractor2:** 上手（じょうず）
- **Distractor3:** 巧み（たくみ）
- **Explanation:** 不器用 and 下手 both refer to being clumsy or lacking skill. 器用, 上手, and 巧み all mean dexterous, skillful, or masterly.

Vocabulary | 復帰（ふっき）

- **Meaning:** Return, comeback, reinstatement / Quay trở lại, trở lại vị trí/công việc cũ sau thời gian vắng mặt.
- **Trigger:** Athletes returning to games after injury, workers returning after parental leave, or politicians returning to office.
- **Contrast:** Differs from 引退 (retirement) or 離脱 (withdrawal); marks a return to a former role.
- **One-line Summary:** Returning to a former position or activity after absence.
- **Correct Synonym:** カムバック
- **Distractor1:** 引退（いんたい）
- **Distractor2:** 辞任（じにん）
- **Distractor3:** 離脱（りだつ）
- **Explanation:** 復帰 and カムバック both mean returning to a former activity or role. 引退 means retirement, 辞任 means resignation, and 離脱 means withdrawal.

FixedExpression | 腑に落ちない（ふにおちない）

- **Meaning:** Unconvincing, hard to comprehend, dubious / Không thể hiểu thấu, không thấy thuyết phục, lấn cấn.
- **Trigger:** Expressing lingering doubt or dissatisfaction with an explanation, reason, or outcome that lacks logic.
- **Contrast:** Differs from 納得できる (fully convincing) or 明白 (clear and obvious).
- **One-line Summary:** Feeling unconvincing or unable to fully digest an explanation.
- **Usage:** Quen dùng khi nghe giải thích hoặc lý do nhưng vẫn cảm thấy có điểm vô lý, chưa tâm phục khẩu phục.
- **Example:** 彼の説明はどうも腑に落ちない。 (His explanation somehow remains unconvincing to me.)

Katakana | ブランク

- **Meaning:** Blank period, career gap, time outage / Khoảng trống, thời gian gián đoạn công việc/học tập.
- **Trigger:** Referring to a time period where one stopped practicing a skill, working, or playing sports.
- **Contrast:** Differs from 継続 (continuation) or 連続 (continuity).
- **One-line Summary:** A gap or blank interval in practice or employment history.
- **Usage:** Dùng để chỉ khoảng thời gian nghỉ ngơi, gián đoạn không luyện tập hay làm việc.
- **Example:** 5年のブランクを経て仕事に復帰した。 (I returned to work after a 5-year career gap.)

Weak Points

- **Hạn chế thống kê:** Do chưa có kết quả làm bài trắc nghiệm thực tế của người học, chưa thể xác định điểm yếu dựa trên tần suất câu làm sai.
- **Nhóm kiến thức dễ nhầm lẫn trong kỳ thi JLPT N2–N1:**
    1. **Phân biệt phó từ chỉ tính đột ngột:** Phân biệt `不意に` (đột ngột, bất ngờ không báo trước) với `突如` (xảy ra biến cố bất ngờ) và `とっさに` (hành động phản xạ tức thì).
    2. **Cách dùng Katakana trong văn phong công sở:** Phân biệt `フォロー` (hỗ trợ, theo dõi xử lý giúp) vs `バックアップ` (sao lưu dữ liệu hoặc hậu thuẫn tổng thể).
    3. **Cụm từ cố định chỉ trạng thái tâm lý:** Cần ghi nhớ cụm `腑に落ちない` (không thấy thuyết phục/lấn cấn) tương đương với `納得できない`.
