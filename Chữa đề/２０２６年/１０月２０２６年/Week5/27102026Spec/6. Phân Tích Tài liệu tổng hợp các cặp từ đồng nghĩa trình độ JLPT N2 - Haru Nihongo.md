Knowledge Extracted

Adverb | 少しも

- **Meaning:** Not a bit, not at all (Một chút cũng không).
- **Trigger:** Used with negative verb forms or negative predicate endings: `少しも〜ない`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `かなり` (considerably) and `少し` (a little - affirmative context).
- **One-line Summary:** Adverb emphasizing complete negation, meaning "not at all".
- **Usage:** 否定形を伴い、打ち消しの意味を強く強調する。(Accompanies negative verbs to strongly emphasize complete negation.)
- **Example:** 彼の説明を聞いても少しも分からなかった。(Even after listening to his explanation, I didn't understand at all.)

Adverb | ずらり

- **Meaning:** In a line, in a row, lining up numerously (Hàng loạt, xếp thành hàng dài).
- **Trigger:** Describes many objects or people lined up in an orderly fashion: `ずらりと並ぶ`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `ぽつりと` (isolated/sparse) and `ばらばら` (scattered).
- **One-line Summary:** Describes a large number of people or items lined up in a row.
- **Usage:** 人や物がたくさん一列に並んでいる様子を表す。(Describes many people or items lined up in a row.)
- **Example:** 店の棚に新商品がずらりと並んでいる。(New products are lined up in a long row on the store shelves.)

Adverb | しいんと

- **Meaning:** Quietly, silently, dead silent (Im lặng, yên tĩnh hẳn).
- **Trigger:** Describes a sudden or complete drop in noise in a room or place: `しいんとする`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `ざわわざわわ` (noisy/buzzing) and `にぎやか` (lively).
- **One-line Summary:** Onomatopoeic adverb describing absolute silence or stillness.
- **Usage:** 音が全くなく、静まり返っている状態を表す。(Describes a state of absolute silence where no sound is made.)
- **Example:** 先生が教室に入ると、部屋の中がしいんとなった。(When the teacher entered the classroom, the room became dead silent.)

Adverb | わざわざ

- **Meaning:** Expressly, going out of one's way, taking the trouble to (Cất công, cố tình).
- **Trigger:** Used when someone takes extra effort or trouble to do an action for a specific purpose: `わざわざ来てくれた`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `ついでに` (incidentally/while at it) and `偶然` (by chance).
- **One-line Summary:** Taking deliberate extra effort or trouble to perform an action.
- **Usage:** 特にそのためだけに労力をかけて行動する様子を表す。(Expresses taking deliberate extra effort specifically for a task.)
- **Example:** 遠くからわざわざ会いに来てくれてありがとう。(Thank you for going out of your way to come visit me from afar.)

Vocabulary | おのおの

- **Meaning:** Each, respective, everyone individually (Mỗi, mỗi người, từng người).
- **Trigger:** Used when referring to individuals taking responsibility or acting independently: `おのおのの意見`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `全員` (everyone as a collective group).
- **One-line Summary:** Formal noun/pronoun referring to each person or item individually.
- **Correct Synonym:** 各自
- **Distractor1:** 一括
- **Distractor2:** 共通
- **Distractor3:** 全体
- **Explanation:** `おのおの` means each person individually, synonymous with `各自`. `一括` means all together/lump sum. `共通` means common/shared. `全体` means the whole entity.

Vocabulary | 身近

- **Meaning:** Familiar, close to oneself, near at hand (Thân thuộc, gần gũi).
- **Trigger:** Modifies things, people, or issues closely connected to daily life: `身近な問題`.
- **Contrast:** Opposite of `疎遠` (distant/estranged) and `遥か` (far away).
- **One-line Summary:** Close at hand or intimately connected to one's daily existence.
- **Correct Synonym:** 親しい
- **Distractor1:** 遠い
- **Distractor2:** 珍しい
- **Distractor3:** 複雑な
- **Explanation:** `身近` describes something or someone close and familiar, synonymous with `親しい` (familiar/close). `遠い` means far. `珍しい` means rare. `複雑な` means complex.

Adverb | やがて

- **Meaning:** Before long, soon, eventually (Chẳng mấy chốc, rồi thì, rồi đây).
- **Trigger:** Modifies upcoming natural transitions or future events: `やがて雨がやむ`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `とっくに` (long ago) and `直ちに` (immediately/instantly).
- **One-line Summary:** Indicating that a state or event will occur before long.
- **Usage:** 時間の経過とともに、ある事態になると予想される時に用いる。(Used when anticipating that an event will happen as time passes.)
- **Example:** 空が暗くなり、やがて雨が降り始めた。(The sky darkened, and before long, rain began to fall.)

Vocabulary | 直接

- **Meaning:** Direct, immediate, face-to-face (Trực tiếp).
- **Trigger:** Used when contacting, delivering, or acting without an intermediary: `直接話す`.
- **Contrast:** Opposite of `間接` (indirect).
- **One-line Summary:** Acting or communicating firsthand without intermediaries.
- **Correct Synonym:** じかに
- **Distractor1:** 間接的に
- **Distractor2:** 代理で
- **Distractor3:** 遠回りに
- **Explanation:** `直接` means directly, synonymous with `じかに` (firsthand/directly). `間接的に` means indirectly. `代理で` means by proxy. `遠回りに` means in a roundabout way.

FixedExpression | 骨を折る

- **Meaning:** To take pains, to exert effort, to work hard (Cần cù, vất vả, dốc sức).
- **Trigger:** Used when making strenuous efforts or troubling oneself for a goal: `仕事のために骨を折る`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `手を抜く` (cut corners) and `怠ける` (be lazy).
- **One-line Summary:** Idiomatic phrase meaning to labor hard or take great pains.
- **Usage:** 苦労して力を尽くすことを表す慣用句。(Idiomatic phrase meaning to exert effort and take great pains.)
- **Example:** 彼はイベントの成功のために大変骨を折った。(He took great pains to ensure the success of the event.)

Vocabulary | 懐かしい

- **Meaning:** Nostalgic, fondly remembered, missed (Hoài niệm, nhớ nhung, da diết).
- **Trigger:** Used when recalling fond memories from the past: `昔の写真を見て懐かしい`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `新鮮` (fresh/new) and `不快` (unpleasant).
- **One-line Summary:** Feeling warm nostalgia for fond past memories.
- **Correct Synonym:** 恋しい
- **Distractor1:** 忌々しい
- **Distractor2:** 退屈な
- **Distractor3:** 羨ましい
- **Explanation:** `懐かしい` describes feeling nostalgic or missing the past, closely overlapping in sentiment with `恋しい` (yearned for/missed). `忌々しい` means annoying/detestable. `退屈な` means boring. `羨ましい` means envious.

Katakana | プリント

- **Meaning:** Print, handout, printing (In ấn, tờ rơi, tài liệu in).
- **Trigger:** Used for school/office handouts or text printing: `プリントを配る`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `手書き` (handwritten).
- **One-line Summary:** Katakana word for printed paper handouts or the process of printing.
- **Usage:** 学校やオフィスで配られる印刷物や、印刷すること自体を指す。(Refers to printed handouts distributed in schools/offices, or the act of printing.)
- **Example:** 授業で先生から新しいプリントが配られた。(A new handout was distributed by the teacher in class.)

Adverb | 比較的

- **Meaning:** Relatively, comparatively (Tương đối, khá).
- **Trigger:** Modifies adjectives or verbs when comparing against a general standard: `比較的安い`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `絶対的` (absolutely) and `極端に` (extremely).
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses a degree determined by comparison with standard baselines.
- **Usage:** 他のものと比較して、ある程度そうである様子を表す。(Describes a degree when compared against other things.)
- **Example:** この地域の家賃は比較的に安い方だ。(Rent in this area is relatively cheap.)

Vocabulary | 支度

- **Meaning:** Preparation, getting ready (Chuẩn bị, sửa soạn).
- **Trigger:** Used for daily preparations like dressing or meal prep: `出かける支度をする`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `後始末` (cleaning up afterward).
- **One-line Summary:** Getting oneself or things ready for an event, journey, or meal.
- **Correct Synonym:** 準備
- **Distractor1:** 片付け
- **Distractor2:** 延期
- **Distractor3:** 中断
- **Explanation:** `支度` means getting ready or preparing, synonymous with `準備`. `片付け` means tidying up. `延期` means postponement. `中断` means interruption.

Katakana | オーバー

- **Meaning:** Over, exceeding, exaggerated (Vượt quá, quá đà, làm quá).
- **Trigger:** Used when exceeding limits or exaggerating: `予算をオーバーする`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `不足` (shortage) and `控えめ` (modest/restrained).
- **One-line Summary:** Exceeding a limit, time, budget, or behaving with exaggeration.
- **Usage:** 基準や制限を超えたり、誇張したりすることを指す。(Refers to exceeding limits/standards or exaggerating.)
- **Example:** 話が少しオーバーだが、面白かった。(The story was a bit exaggerated, but it was interesting.)

Katakana | レアな

- **Meaning:** Rare, uncommon, hard to find (Hiếm có, hiếm gặp).
- **Trigger:** Modifies items, collectibles, or experiences that are unusual: `レアなカード`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `ありふれた` (common/ordinary) and `平凡な` (unremarkable).
- **One-line Summary:** Katakana adjective describing rare, hard-to-find items or occurrences.
- **Usage:** めったに手に入らない貴重なものや状況を表す。(Describes precious things or situations that are rarely obtained.)
- **Example:** レアな古本を古書店で見つけることができた。(I managed to find a rare old book at a second-hand bookstore.)
