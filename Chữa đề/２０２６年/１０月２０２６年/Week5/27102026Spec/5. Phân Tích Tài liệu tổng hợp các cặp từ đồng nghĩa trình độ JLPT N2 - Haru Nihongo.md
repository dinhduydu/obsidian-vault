Katakana | オーダー

- **Meaning:** Order, placing an order (Gọi món, đặt hàng).
- **Trigger:** Used in restaurant, service, or business ordering contexts: `オーダーを取る`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `キャンセル` (cancellation) and `サービス` (free service).
- **One-line Summary:** Katakana term for ordering food or goods in service settings.
- **Usage:** レストランやビジネスで注文を出す際に使用する。(Used when placing an order in restaurants or business contexts.)
- **Example:** 居酒屋で追加のドリンクをオーダーした。(I ordered additional drinks at the izakaya.)

Vocabulary | 検索

- **Meaning:** Search, retrieval (Tìm kiếm thông tin/dữ liệu).
- **Trigger:** Used in IT, database, or dictionary searching contexts: `ネットで検索する`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `探す` (searching physically for lost objects) and `調査` (investigating).
- **One-line Summary:** Searching or retrieving digital/systematic information from a database.
- **Correct Synonym:** 探す
- **Distractor1:** 調査する
- **Distractor2:** 放置する
- **Distractor3:** 整理する
- **Explanation:** `検索` means searching for information in a system, synonymous in basic meaning with `探す`. `調査する` means investigating or surveying deeply. `放置する` means leaving unattended. `整理する` means organizing or sorting.

Vocabulary | 用いる

- **Meaning:** To use, to employ, to utilize (Sử dụng, dùng).
- **Trigger:** Formal or written context for using methods, tools, or materials: `特別な方法を用いる`.
- **Contrast:** Formal literary verb compared to everyday conversational `使う`.
- **One-line Summary:** Formal written verb for utilizing a method, tool, or resource.
- **Correct Synonym:** 使う
- **Distractor1:** 捨てる
- **Distractor2:** 避ける
- **Distractor3:** 妨げる
- **Explanation:** `用いる` is a formal literary verb meaning to use, synonymous with `使う`. `捨てる` means to throw away. `避ける` means to avoid. `妨げる` means to obstruct or hinder.

Adverb | たまたま

- **Meaning:** By chance, unexpectedly, accidentally (Tình cờ, ngẫu nhiên).
- **Trigger:** Modifies coincidental or unplanned events: `駅でたまたま昔の友人に会った`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `わざと` (intentionally) and `いつも` (always).
- **One-line Summary:** Happening by coincidence without prior planning.
- **Usage:** 偶然に起こった出来事を述べる際に動詞を修飾する。(Modifies verbs to describe coincidental events.)
- **Example:** たまたま入ったカフェで美味しいケーキを見つけた。(By chance, I found a delicious cake at a cafe I randomly entered.)

Katakana | ブーム

- **Trigger:** Used when describing sudden social popularity or craze: `健康ブーム`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `不景気` (recession) and `伝統` (tradition).
- **One-line Summary:** A sudden surge in public popularity or social trend.
- **Usage:** 社会的な流行や急激な人気上昇を表す。(Expresses a sudden surge in social popularity or fashion.)
- **Example:** 最近、レトロゲームのブームが再燃している。(Recently, the retro game boom has reignited.)

Vocabulary | 卑怯

- **Meaning:** Mean, unfair, cowardly, sneaky (Xảo quyệt, gian trá, hèn nhát).
- **Trigger:** Describes unfair behavior, cheating, or cowardice: `卑怯なやり方`.
- **Contrast:** Opposite of `堂々` (fair and honorable) and `正当` (just/legitimate).
- **One-line Summary:** Acting unfairly, dishonorably, or sneakily.
- **Correct Synonym:** ずるい
- **Distractor1:** 堂々とした
- **Distractor2:** 寛大な
- **Distractor3:** 誠実な
- **Explanation:** `卑怯` describes sneaky or unfair behavior, synonymous with `ずるい`. `堂々とした` means dignified or fair. `寛大な` means generous/tolerant. `誠実な` means sincere or honest.

Adverb | 一段

- **Meaning:** Much more, further, all the more (Ngày càng, hơn hẳn).
- **Trigger:** Modifies comparative changes or degree increases: `一段と寒くなる`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `少し` (a little) and `徐々に` (gradually).
- **One-line Summary:** Indicating a noticeable jump or step up in degree.
- **Usage:** 程度や状態が前よりさらに高まることを強調する。(Emphasizes that degree or state increases notably compared to before.)
- **Example:** 秋が深まり、朝晩の冷え込みが一段と厳しくなった。(As autumn deepened, the morning and evening cold became noticeably harsher.)

Vocabulary | 慌ただしい

- **Meaning:** Busy, hurried, hectic (Bận rộn, hối hả, xô xát).
- **Trigger:** Describes a restless, hurried atmosphere or timeframe: `慌ただしい一日`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `穏やか` (calm/peaceful) and `暇` (free/idle).
- **One-line Summary:** Restlessly busy or moving at a hurried pace.
- **Correct Synonym:** 忙しい
- **Distractor1:** 穏やかな
- **Distractor2:** 静かな
- **Distractor3:** 退屈な
- **Explanation:** `慌ただしい` describes hectic or hurried busyness, synonymous with `忙しい`. `穏やかな` means calm or gentle. `静かな` means quiet. `退屈な` means boring.

Vocabulary | 躊躇う

- **Trigger:** Used when indecisive about taking action due to fear or doubt: `発言を躊躇う`.
- **Contrast:** Opposite of `決断する` (decide promptly).

Adverb | とっくに

Adverb | 大体

- **Meaning:** Generally, roughly, approximately (Khoảng, đại khái, hầu như).
- **Trigger:** Modifies numbers, estimations, or general understanding: `大体理解した`.

- **One-line Summary:** Giving a rough estimate or general overview.
- **Usage:** おおよその数量や全体的な状況を述べる際に用いる。(Used when stating approximate quantities or general situations.)

Vocabulary | 悔しい

- **Meaning:** Regretful, vexed, frustratingly disappointing (Bực bội, hối tiếc, đáng tiếc).
- **Trigger:** Expresses emotional frustration at defeat or missed opportunity: `試合に負けて悔しい`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `嬉しい` (happy) and `満足` (satisfied).
- **One-line Summary:** Feeling bitter frustration over a disappointment or loss.
- **Correct Synonym:** 残念
- **Distractor1:** 誇らしい
- **Distractor2:** 満足な
- **Distractor3:** 楽しい
- **Explanation:** `悔しい` expresses bitter frustration or regret over defeat/failure, closely overlapping in tone with `残念`. `誇らしい` means proud. `満足な` means satisfied. `楽しい` means enjoyable.

Adverb | 結局

- **Meaning:** After all, in the end, eventually (Rốt cuộc, kết cục, nói chung là).
- **Trigger:** Introduces final outcome after a process or debate: `結局雨で中止になった`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `最初に` (at first) and `途中で` (midway).
- **One-line Summary:** Stating the ultimate outcome after a period of time or effort.
- **Usage:** 多彩な経緯の末にたどり着いた最終的な結果を導く。(Leads to the final outcome reached after various developments.)
- **Example:** 色々悩んだが、結局元のデザインに決めた。(After worrying about many options, in the end I decided on the original design.)

Vocabulary | 完全

- **Meaning:** Complete, total, perfect (Hoàn toàn, toàn bộ, đầy đủ).
- **Trigger:** Describes states without defect or exception: `完全な勝利`.
- **Contrast:** Opposite of `不完全` (incomplete) and `部分的` (partial).
- **One-line Summary:** Flawless and thorough without any missing parts.
- **Correct Synonym:** すっかり
- **Distractor1:** 部分的
- **Distractor2:** あいまい
- **Distractor3:** わずか
- **Explanation:** `完全` means total or complete, functioning as a synonym to `すっかり` when describing thoroughness. `部分的` means partial. `あいまい` means vague. `わずか` means a tiny bit.

Vocabulary | 無事

- **Meaning:** Safe, peaceful, without incident (Bình an, vô sự, êm đao).
- **Trigger:** Used when completing a trip, task, or crisis safely: `無事に到着した`.
- **Contrast:** Opposite of `危険` (dangerous) and `事故` (accident).
- **One-line Summary:** Free from harm, trouble, or unexpected incident.
- **Correct Synonym:** 平穏
- **Distractor1:** 危険な
- **Distractor2:** 波乱の
- **Distractor3:** 不安な
- **Explanation:** `無事` refers to safety and peaceful completion, synonymous with `平穏`. `危険な` means dangerous. `波乱の` means stormy or turbulent. `不安な` means anxious.

    - **Mức độ ưu tiên:** High.
3. **Từ đồng nghĩa Katakana và từ văn viết (****オーダー****,** **ブーム****,** **用いる****):**
    - **Nguyên nhân:** Cần phân biệt từ dùng trong văn bản chính thức (`用いる` = `使う`) và từ Katakana mượn tiếng Anh trong giao tiếp dịch vụ (`オーダー` = `注文`).
    - **Mức độ ưu tiên:** Medium.
