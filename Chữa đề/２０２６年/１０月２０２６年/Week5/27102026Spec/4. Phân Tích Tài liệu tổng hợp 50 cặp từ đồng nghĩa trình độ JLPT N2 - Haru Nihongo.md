Vocabulary | おわび

- **Meaning:** Apology, expression of regret (Lời xin lỗi, sự tạ lỗi).
- **Trigger:** Used in formal or polite situations when apologizing for a mistake or inconvenience: `おわびを申し上げる`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `言い訳` (excuse) and `感謝` (gratitude).
- **One-line Summary:** Formal noun expressing apology or regret.
- **Correct Synonym:** 謝る
- **Distractor1:** 感謝する
- **Distractor2:** 弁解する
- **Distractor3:** 許す
- **Explanation:** `おわび` (noun) means apology, directly synonymous with `謝る` (to apologize / apology). `感謝する` means to thank. `弁解する` means to make excuses or defend oneself. `許す` means to forgive.

Vocabulary | 明け方

- **Meaning:** Dawn, daybreak, early morning (Bình minh, sáng sớm).
- **Trigger:** Used when indicating the time of day just before sunrise: `明け方に起きる`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `夕方` (dusk/evening) and `真夜中` (midnight).
- **One-line Summary:** The time of day when morning light first appears before sunrise.
- **Correct Synonym:** 朝早く
- **Distractor1:** 夕方
- **Distractor2:** 夜中
- **Distractor3:** 日中
- **Explanation:** `明け方` refers to dawn/early morning, equivalent to `朝早く`. `夕方` refers to late afternoon/dusk. `夜中` means late at night. `日中` means daytime.

Vocabulary | 鈍い

- **Meaning:** Dull, slow, blunt, insensitive (Chậm chạp, lơ đơ, cùn).
- **Trigger:** Modifies physical movement, mental responsiveness, or sharpness: `動作が鈍い`, `刃物が鈍い`.
- **Contrast:** Opposite of `鋭い` (sharp/keen/quick).
- **One-line Summary:** Lacking quickness, sharpness, or sensitivity.
- **Correct Synonym:** 遅い
- **Distractor1:** 鋭い
- **Distractor2:** 早い
- **Distractor3:** 激しい
- **Explanation:** `鈍い` means slow/dull, synonymous with `遅い`. `鋭い` is the antonym meaning sharp or keen. `早い` means fast or early. `激しい` means intense or violent.

Adverb | 年中

- **Meaning:** All year round, constantly, always (Quanh năm, lúc nào cũng).
- **Trigger:** Modifies continuous or habitually repeated actions throughout the year: `年中文句を言っている`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `たまに` (occasionally) and `一時的` (temporarily).
- **One-line Summary:** Continuously or habitually happening all year round.
- **Usage:** Adverbial use indicating that something happens continuously or without interruption throughout the entire year or habitually.
- **Example:** 彼は年中風邪を引いている。(He is always catching a cold all year round.)

Adverb | 至急

- **Meaning:** Urgently, immediately, promptly (Ngay lập tức, khẩn cấp).
- **Trigger:** Used in business or urgent requests requiring immediate action: `至急連絡してください`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `のちほど` (later) and `徐々に` (gradually).
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses extreme urgency requiring immediate action.
- **Usage:** Placed before verbs or in business communications to demand prompt execution.
- **Example:** この書類を至急確認してください。(Please check this document immediately.)

Vocabulary | 決まり

- **Meaning:** Rule, regulation, agreement, outcome (Quy định, quy tắc).
- **Trigger:** Used when referring to established guidelines or social rules: `学校の決まりを守る`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `例外` (exception) and `自由` (freedom).
- **One-line Summary:** Established rules or regulations that people must follow.
- **Correct Synonym:** 規則
- **Distractor1:** 例外
- **Distractor2:** 習慣
- **Distractor3:** 権利
- **Explanation:** `決まり` refers to a rule or regulation, equivalent to `規則`. `例外` means an exception. `習慣` means custom or habit. `権利` means right/privilege.

Adverb | つねに

- **Meaning:** Always, constantly, at all times (Luôn luôn, thường xuyên).
- **Trigger:** Used in formal speech or writing to describe consistent states or actions: `つねに努力する`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `たまに` (sometimes) and `めったに` (rarely).
- **One-line Summary:** Happening consistently at all times without exception.
- **Usage:** Modifies verbs or adjectives to show an unwavering, continuous state or habit.
- **Example:** 彼女はつねに笑顔を絶やさない。(She always keeps a smile on her face.)

Vocabulary | 平凡

- **Meaning:** Ordinary, commonplace, average (Bình thường, tầm thường).
- **Trigger:** Describes a life, event, or thing that lacks special features or drama: `平凡な日常`.
- **Contrast:** Opposite of `非凡` (extraordinary) and `特別` (special).
- **One-line Summary:** Lacking extraordinary features; completely normal or average.

- **Distractor1:** 特別

- **Explanation:** `平凡` means ordinary or average, synonymous with `普通`. `特別` means special. `優秀` means superior or excellent. `独特` means unique or peculiar.

Vocabulary | 当たり前

- **Meaning:** Natural, obvious, taken for granted (Đương nhiên, dĩ nhiên).
- **Trigger:** Used when something is regarded as self-evident or standard: `お礼を言うのは当たり前だ`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `意外` (unexpected) and `不思議` (mysterious).

Collocation | 気になる

- **Meaning:** To weigh on one's mind, to be concerned, to be curious about (Quan tâm, lo lắng, bận tâm).
- **Trigger:** N + が気になる: `試験の結果が気になる`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `気にする` (transitive active worrying).
- **One-line Summary:** Something naturally holding one's interest or causing worry.
- **Usage:** Combines noun `気` with intransitive verb `なる` to indicate a state of feeling anxious, curious, or bothered by something.
- **Example:** 明日の天気が気になって眠れない。(I am concerned about tomorrow's weather and cannot sleep.)

Adverb | ぱっと

- **Meaning:** Suddenly, rapidly, in a flash (Nhanh chóng, đột nhiên).
- **Trigger:** Describes sudden movement, sudden spread, or immediate change: `ぱっと広がる`, `ぱっと立ち上がる`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `じっくり` (thoroughly/slowly) and `だらだら` (sluggishly).
- **One-line Summary:** Happening in a sudden, energetic flash or instant.
- **Usage:** Onomatopoeic adverb modifying verbs to describe an instantaneous or sudden action or transformation.
- **Example:** ニュースを聞いて、みんなの顔がぱっと明るくなった。(Hearing the news, everyone's faces suddenly brightened up.)

Adverb | 再三

- **Meaning:** Again and again, repeatedly, time after time (Nhiều lần, dồn dập).
- **Trigger:** Used when warnings, requests, or notices are given multiple times: `再三注意する`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `初めて` (for the first time) and `一度だけ` (only once).
- **One-line Summary:** Occurring or being repeated multiple times.
- **Usage:** Formal adverb modifying verbs involving communication, warning, or action performed repeatedly.
- **Example:** 再三お願いしたにもかかわらず、返事が来ない。(Despite asking repeatedly, no reply has arrived.)

Vocabulary | 物騒

- **Meaning:** Dangerous, unsettled, unsafe (Loạn lạc, nguy hiểm, đáng lo ngại).
- **Trigger:** Describes neighborhoods, times, or rumors that feel unsafe: `最近は物騒な世の中だ`.
- **Contrast:** Opposite of `安全` (safe) and `平和` (peaceful).
- **One-line Summary:** Creating a sense of danger or insecurity.
- **Correct Synonym:** 危ない
- **Distractor1:** 安全
- **Distractor2:** 静か
- **Distractor3:** 賑やか
- **Explanation:** `物騒` describes a dangerous or unsafe situation/environment, synonymous with `危ない`. `安全` means safe. `静か` means quiet. `賑やか` means lively/bustling.

Vocabulary | 奇妙

- **Meaning:** Strange, peculiar, odd, mysterious (Kỳ lạ, lạ lùng).
- **Trigger:** Describes events, sights, or sounds that feel mysterious or unusual: `奇妙な音が聞こえる`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `普通` (normal) and `定石` (standard).
- **One-line Summary:** Unusually strange or mysterious in nature.
- **Correct Synonym:** 不思議
- **Distractor1:** 普通
- **Distractor2:** 当たり前
- **Distractor3:** 正確
- **Explanation:** `奇妙` means strange or odd, synonymous with `不思議`. `普通` means normal. `当たり前` means taken for granted. `正確` means accurate/exact.

FixedExpression | 手を焼く

- **Meaning:** To be at a loss with, to struggle with, to have a hard time handling (Bất trị, bó tay, gặp rắc rối).
- **Trigger:** N + に手を焼く: `いたずらっ子に手を焼く`.
- **Contrast:** Differs from `手を貸す` (to lend a hand) and `手を抜く` (to cut corners).
- **One-line Summary:** Struggling to deal with a troublesome person or problem.
- **Usage:** Idiomatic expression combining `手` and `焼く` meaning to be troubled or unable to handle a difficult child, situation, or task.
- **Example:** 反抗期の息子に親は手を焼いている。(The parents are struggling to handle their rebellious son.)
