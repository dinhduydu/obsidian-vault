Knowledge Extracted

Grammar | 〜までもない

- **Meaning:** No need to, unnecessary to / Không cần thiết phải, không đến mức phải.
- **Trigger:** Paired with verbs of effort or movement like `勉強する`, `行く`, `調べる` when an action is obvious or overly redundant.
- **Contrast:** `〜までもない` (unnecessary because it is obvious) vs `〜ところではない` (incapable of doing due to severe circumstances) vs `〜ないではすまない` (must do / cannot get away without doing).
- **Semantic Field:** Necessity & Unnecessity / Sự không cần thiết.
- **Confusion Index:** `〜までもない ↔ 〜ところではない ↔ 〜ないではすまない`.
- **Why Wrong:** In Question 1, the user chose `ところではない` (incapable due to situation), failing to realize that a simple textbook makes extra studying redundant (`勉強するまでもない`). In Question 16, the user chose `行かないではすまない` (must go), failing to see that calling by phone makes visiting in person unnecessary (`行くまでもない`).
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses that an action is completely unnecessary or redundant because the situation is simple or clear.

Grammar | 〜までになる

- **Meaning:** Progress to the point of, reach the level where / Tiến triển đến mức, đạt đến trình độ.
- **Trigger:** Sequential career or skill growth markers like `パート` → `正社員` → `部門を任される`.
- **Contrast:** `〜までになる` (gradual progress culminating in a major capability) vs `〜たばかりだ` (just recently finished doing an action).
- **Semantic Field:** Progression & Extent / Tiến trình & Mức độ đạt được.
- **Confusion Index:** `〜までになる ↔ 〜たばかりだ`.
- **Why Wrong:** In Question 2, the user chose `任されたばかりだ` (just recently entrusted), overlooking the 4-year career progression from part-timer to full-time manager (`任されるまでになった`).
- **One-line Summary:** Indicates reaching an advanced state or capability through step-by-step progress over time.

Adverb | ともかく

- **Meaning:** Regardless, setting aside, anyway / Bất luận thế nào, gác lại A sang một bên, dù sao đi nữa.
- **Trigger:** Clause pattern `AはともかくB` or adverbial placement before an imperative/encouragement `一生懸命努力してみなさい`.
- **Contrast:** `ともかく` (setting aside A to focus on B) vs `あくまで` (persistently / strictly to the end).
- **Usage:** Dùng để tạm thời không bàn tới vấn đề A (như thắng bại, kết quả) mà ưu tiên tập trung vào hành động B.
- **Example:** 成功するかしないかはともかく、一生懸命努力してみなさい。(Regardless of whether you succeed or not, try your best. / Thành bại tính sau, trước mắt hãy nỗ lực hết sức.)
- **One-line Summary:** Sets aside an uncertain outcome or topic to emphasize immediate effort or focus.

Grammar | 〜ようがない

- **Meaning:** There is no way to, impossible to / Hoàn toàn không có cách nào để, không thể nào.
- **Trigger:** Stem form of a verb + `ようがない` after strong definitive evidence (`確かな目撃者が存在する`).
- **Contrast:** `〜ようがない` (lack of any means/method to perform) vs `〜そうもない` (doesn't look likely to happen).
- **Semantic Field:** Impossibility & Inability / Sự bất khả thi về phương cách.
- **Confusion Index:** `〜ようがない ↔ 〜そうもない`.
- **Why Wrong:** In Question 4, the user chose `ごまかしそうもない` (looks unlikely to deceive), failing to see that ironclad eyewitness testimony leaves absolutely zero way to deceive (`ごまかしようがない`).
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses that it is physically or logically impossible to perform an action due to lack of means.

Grammar | 〜しかあるまい

- **Meaning:** Have no choice but to, must / Chắc chỉ còn cách, không còn lựa chọn nào khác ngoài.
- **Trigger:** Unfavorable constraints or dead-ends like `仕事の量を減らすのは難しく`, `完全否定されたら`.
- **Contrast:** `〜しかあるまい` (conjectural necessity: must have no choice but to...) vs `〜わけがない` (it's impossible that...).
- **Semantic Field:** Unavoidability & Conjecture / Lựa chọn duy nhất & Phán đoán.
- **Confusion Index:** `〜しかあるまい ↔ 〜わけがない`.
- **Why Wrong:** In Question 5, the user answered correctly (`工夫を重ねていくしかあるまい`). In Question 13, the user chose `わけがない` (impossible that...), failing to realize that if one's work is completely rejected by the boss, one has no choice but to quit (`辞めるしかあるまい`).
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses a conjectural conclusion that no other alternative or choice exists.

Grammar | 〜はともかく

- **Meaning:** Setting aside, regardless of / Tạm thời chưa bàn tới, kết quả ra sao chưa tính.
- **Trigger:** Noun + `はともかく` followed by a resolute statement (`悔いはない`, `頑張る`).
- **Contrast:** `〜はともかく` (setting aside A) vs `〜につけても` (whenever A happens / in every case).
- **Semantic Field:** Exclusion & Focus / Gác lại chủ đề & Tập trung vế sau.
- **Confusion Index:** `〜はともかく ↔ 〜につけても`.
- **Why Wrong:** In Question 6, the user chose `につけても` (whenever), missing the logical structure "test results aside (`試験の結果はともかく`), I have no regrets because I did my best."
- **One-line Summary:** Excludes or sets aside a topic to emphasize the main point in the following clause.

Grammar | 〜を問わず

- **Meaning:** Regardless of, irrespectively of / Bất kể, không kể, không phân biệt.
- **Trigger:** Paired contrast nouns like `昼夜`, `男女`, `年齢`, or `国籍` followed by continuous availability (`24時間受け付けている`).
- **Contrast:** `〜を問わず` (regardless of A or B) vs `〜にかけて` (spanning a spatial/temporal range).
- **Semantic Field:** Non-condition & Scope / Bất kể & Khớp phạm vi.
- **Confusion Index:** `〜を問わず ↔ 〜にかけて`.
- **Why Wrong:** In Question 7, the user chose `にかけて` (spanning from... to...), failing to recognize that a 24-hour hotline operates irrespectively of day or night (`昼夜を問わず`).
- **One-line Summary:** Indicates that an action or rule applies universally regardless of conditions like time, age, or gender.

Grammar | 〜をはじめ

- **Meaning:** Starting with, including as a primary example / Trước tiên phải kể đến, tiêu biểu là.
- **Trigger:** A representative landmark/item (`スキー`, `清水寺`) followed by a broad category (`冬のスポーツ`, `古いお寺`).
- **Contrast:** `〜をはじめ` (singling out a representative example) vs `〜からして` (judging from a single detail) vs `〜の上に` (in addition to).
- **Semantic Field:** Exemplification & Representative / Ví dụ tiêu biểu.
- **Confusion Index:** `〜をはじめ ↔ 〜からして ↔ 〜の上に`.
- **Why Wrong:** In Question 8, the user chose `からして` (judging from). In Question 12, the user chose `の上に` (in addition to). Both missed that skiing and Kiyomizu-dera are premier representative examples introducing their respective categories.
- **One-line Summary:** Singles out a prominent, leading example to represent a whole group or category.

Grammar | 〜まい

- **Meaning:** Will not, intend not to / Nhất định không, quyết không.
- **Trigger:** First-person volitional negative resolution with `二度と` + Verb-plain + `まいと思う`.
- **Contrast:** `〜まい` (negative volition / strong resolution not to do) vs `〜かねない` (might happen / risk of).
- **Semantic Field:** Negative Volition / Ý chí phủ định.
- **Confusion Index:** `〜まい ↔ 〜かねない`.
- **Why Wrong:** The user answered Question 9 correctly (`二度と遅れまいと思う` - resolving never to be late again).
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses a strong personal determination or resolution never to perform an action again.

Grammar | 〜までなら

- **Meaning:** Up until, as long as it is within the limit of / Nếu trong phạm vi/thời hạn cho phép thì.
- **Trigger:** Time limit or quantity cap (`3日前まで`) + `なら` + favorable condition (`無料です`).
- **Contrast:** `〜までなら` (up to a specific limit/deadline) vs `〜となら` (if with someone).
- **Semantic Field:** Limit & Condition / Giới hạn & Điều kiện.
- **Confusion Index:** `〜までなら ↔ 〜となら`.
- **Why Wrong:** In Question 10, the user chose `となら`, failing to see the combination of deadline `3日前まで` and conditional `なら` for free cancellation.
- **One-line Summary:** Specifies a temporal or quantitative boundary within which a condition remains valid.

Grammar | 〜しかない

- **Meaning:** Have no choice but to, can only / Không còn cách nào khác ngoài.
- **Trigger:** Stating an unavoidable sole remaining action (`タクシーで帰るしかない`) when alternatives fail (`終電に間に合わなかった`).
- **Contrast:** `〜しかない` (unavoidable sole option) vs `〜にすぎない` (merely / nothing more than).
- **Semantic Field:** Necessity & Sole Option / Lựa chọn duy nhất.
- **Confusion Index:** `〜しかない ↔ 〜にすぎない`.
- **Why Wrong:** The user answered Question 11 correctly (`タクシーで帰るしかない` - missed the last train, so must take a taxi).
- **One-line Summary:** Indicates that no other alternative exists except the stated action.

Grammar | 〜を中心に

- **Meaning:** Centered around, focused on / Lấy... làm trung tâm, tập trung vào.
- **Trigger:** Noun marking a core hub (`学校`) + `を中心に` + `たくさんの店が集まっている`.
- **Contrast:** `〜を中心に` (centered geographically or operationally around X) vs `〜をこめて` (with feelings/love) vs `〜を通して` (through the medium of).
- **Semantic Field:** Focus & Core / Trung tâm & Trọng tâm.
- **Confusion Index:** `〜を中心に ↔ 〜をこめて`.
- **Why Wrong:** The user answered Question 14 correctly (`学校を中心に` - shops gathered around the school).
- **One-line Summary:** Identifies the central hub or primary focus around which other entities or activities cluster.

Grammar | 〜ではあるまいか

- **Meaning:** Isn't it likely that...? / Chẳng phải là... hay sao?
- **Trigger:** Rhetorical conjecture or warning in severe conditions (`こんな天気では`, `中止になるのではあるまいか`).
- **Contrast:** `〜ではあるまいか` (rhetorical negative conjecture: isn't it likely?) vs `〜きり` (only / since).
- **Semantic Field:** Rhetorical Conjecture & Suspicion / Phán đoán nghi vấn nhẹ nhàng.
- **Confusion Index:** `〜ではあるまいか ↔ 〜きり`.
- **Why Wrong:** The user answered Question 15 correctly (`中止になるのではあるまいか` - won't the concert be canceled in this bad weather?).
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses a rhetorical expectation or suspicion that a certain negative or logical outcome will occur.

1. **Nhầm lẫn Cấu trúc Chỉ Giới hạn / Sự Không Cần Thiết (****〜までもない** **vs** **〜ところではない** **vs** **〜ないではすまない****):**
    - Học viên thường chọn nhầm `ところではない` hoặc `ないではすまない` khi câu chỉ đơn thuần diễn tả "không cần thiết phải cất công làm" (`わざわざ〜までもない`).
2. **Khả năng Nhận diện Cấu trúc Tiến triển Trình độ (****〜までになる****):**
    - Rất dễ nhầm lẫn giữa sự tiến triển năng lực/chức vụ qua một quá trình dài (`までになった`) và thì vừa mới hoàn thành (`たばかりだ`).
3. **Chưa Nắm Vững Nhóm Ngữ pháp Ví dụ Tiêu biểu (****〜をはじめ****):**
    - Sai cả 2 câu có sự xuất hiện của `〜をはじめ` (Q8 và Q12), nhầm lẫn với `〜からして` (xét từ điểm nhỏ) hoặc `〜の上に` (hơn nữa/ngoài ra).
4. **Phân biệt Cấu trúc Phủ định Khả năng / Phương cách (****〜ようがない** **vs** **〜そうもない****):**
    - Nhầm giữa sự tuyệt đối không có cách nào thực hiện (`ようがない`) và sự phỏng đoán trông có vẻ khó xảy ra (`そうもない`).
5. **Nhầm lẫn Ngữ pháp Phạm vi / Không Phân biệt (****〜を問わず** **vs** **〜にかけて****):**
    - Nhầm giữa việc không phân biệt/bất kể điều kiện (`昼夜を問わず`) và khoảng thời gian/không gian trải dài (`〜にかけて`).
