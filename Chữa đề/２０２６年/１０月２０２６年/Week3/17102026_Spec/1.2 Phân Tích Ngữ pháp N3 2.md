Error Analysis

Tình trạng Dữ liệu Bài làm

Tài liệu `1.2. Ngữ pháp N3 2.pdf` cung cấp cấu trúc ngữ pháp lý thuyết và câu ví dụ minh họa, không có dữ liệu câu sai thực nghiệm từ bài làm của học viên.

Bẫy Kiến thức & Nhầm lẫn Phổ biến ở 20 Cấu trúc Ngữ pháp N3 (Mẫu 21–40)

1. **Phân biệt Nguồn thông tin ↔ Sự phụ thuộc / Nguyên nhân:**
    - `〜によると` (chỉ nguồn tin, đi với `〜そうだ`/`〜らしい`) ↔ `〜によって` (tùy thuộc vào, do, bởi).
2. **Phân biệt Đối tượng Thái độ ↔ Chủ đề ↔ Góc nhìn Đánh giá:**
    - `〜に対して` (hướng thái độ/hành động về phía đối tượng) ↔ `〜について` / `〜に関して` (nói/nghiên cứu về chủ đề) ↔ `〜にとって` (đối với -> góc nhìn đánh giá cá nhân).
3. **Phân biệt Mục tiêu Thiết kế ↔ Sự Phù hợp Tự nhiên:**
    - `〜向け` (được thiết kế/chế tạo dành riêng cho đối tượng) ↔ `〜向き` (bản chất tự nhiên phù hợp với đối tượng).
4. **Phân biệt Mức độ Biểu cảm ↔ Ước lượng Thông thường:**
    - `〜ほど` (mức độ cao, dùng hình ảnh so sánh sống động) ↔ `〜くらい` (mức độ/khoảng chừng trong đời sống hàng ngày).
5. **Phân biệt Nhấn mạnh Cực đoan ↔ Nhấn mạnh Khẳng định Duy nhất:**
    - `〜さえ` (ngay cả -> đưa ra ví dụ tối thiểu để nhấn mạnh toàn bộ) ↔ `〜こそ` (chính là -> nhấn mạnh khẳng định đặc biệt).

Knowledge Extracted

Grammar | 〜ことはない

- **Meaning:** No need to, don't have to / Không cần phải, không nhất thiết phải.
- **Trigger:** Reassuring or advising someone that a specific action is unnecessary in the given situation.
- **Contrast:** `〜ことはない` indicates unnecessity, whereas `〜てはいけない` prohibits an action, and `〜ないほうがいい` advises against doing it.
- **One-line Summary:** Reassuring someone that an action is completely unnecessary.
- **Formation:** V-ru + ことはない.
- **Semantic Field:** Advice & Unnecessity / Lời khuyên & Sự không cần thiết.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜なくてもいい`, `〜必要はない`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using prohibition `〜てはいけない` incorrectly forbids the action, whereas the speaker intends to comfort that worrying is simply unnecessary.
- **Why Correct:** `ことはない` accurately expresses that there is no need or obligation to worry.
- **Example:** 心配することはない。 (Không cần phải lo lắng.)

Grammar | 〜に対して

- **Meaning:** Towards, regarding / Đối với, hướng tới (thái độ, hành vi).
- **Trigger:** Expressing an attitude, feeling, or action directed specifically toward a person, group, or object.
- **Contrast:** `〜に対して` marks a target of emotional/action orientation, while `〜について` marks a topic of discussion/thought.
- **One-line Summary:** Marks the target toward which an attitude or action is directed.
- **Formation:** N + に対して; N + に対する + N.
- **Semantic Field:** Target & Attitude / Đối tượng & Thái độ.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜について`, `〜に関して`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `について` merely indicates a topic being discussed or researched, lacking the directional emotional attitude of gratitude toward parents.
- **Why Correct:** `に対して` correctly specifies parents as the target of the gratitude.
- **Example:** 親に対して感謝している。 (Tôi biết ơn đối với bố mẹ.)

Grammar | 〜について

- **Meaning:** About, regarding / Về, liên quan đến.
- **Trigger:** Introducing the topic or content of speech, writing, thought, or investigation.
- **Contrast:** `〜について` introduces a general topic of speech/thought, whereas `〜に関して` is a more formal equivalent covering broader relations.
- **One-line Summary:** Marks the main topic of discussion or thought.
- **Formation:** N + について; N + についての + N.
- **Semantic Field:** Topic & Content / Chủ đề & Nội dung.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜に関して`, `〜に対して`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `に対して` implies directing a personal action or attitude toward a target, which doesn't fit discussing an environmental topic.
- **Why Correct:** `について` properly introduces environmental issues as the subject of the discussion.
- **Example:** 環境問題について話す。 (Nói về vấn đề môi trường.)

Grammar | 〜によると

- **Meaning:** According to / Theo như (nguồn tin).
- **Trigger:** Citing a source of information, almost always paired with hearsay sentence endings like `〜そうだ`, `〜らしい`, or `〜とのことだ`.
- **Contrast:** `〜によると` cites an information source, whereas `〜によって` indicates cause, means, passive agent, or variation.
- **One-line Summary:** Cites an external source of information.
- **Formation:** N + によると / N + によれば.
- **Semantic Field:** Information Source & Hearsay / Nguồn thông tin & Truyền đạt.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜によれば`, `〜そうだ`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `によって` indicates cause, means, or variation, whereas citing a news report requires `によると`.
- **Why Correct:** `によると` properly identifies the news broadcast as the source reporting the typhoon's approach.
- **Example:** ニュースによると台風が来る。 (Theo tin tức thì bão sắp đến.)

Grammar | 〜によって

- **Meaning:** Depending on / Due to / By / Tùy thuộc vào; Do, bởi.
- **Trigger:** Expressing diversity/variation based on different cases, or marking a cause, means, or passive agent.
- **Contrast:** `〜によって` denotes variation or cause/means, whereas `〜によると` cites an information source.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses variation depending on something, or specifies a cause or means.
- **Formation:** N + によって; N + による + N.
- **Semantic Field:** Diversity & Cause / Sự khác biệt & Nguyên nhân.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜によると`, `〜を通じて`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `によると` incorrectly frames individual perspectives as a news/information source rather than a factor causing variation.
- **Why Correct:** `によって` correctly expresses that ways of thinking vary depending on each individual person.
- **Example:** 人によって考え方が違う。 (Tùy vào mỗi người mà cách suy nghĩ khác nhau.)

Grammar | 〜において

- **Meaning:** In, at, in the field of / Tại, ở, trong (lĩnh vực, địa điểm, thời đại).
- **Trigger:** Formal or written context specifying a location, historical era, or field of activity.
- **Contrast:** `〜において` is a formal/written counterpart of the locative particle `で`.
- **One-line Summary:** Formal particle marking a location, era, or domain of activity.
- **Formation:** N + において; N + における + N.
- **Semantic Field:** Formal Location & Domain / Địa điểm & Lĩnh vực trang trọng.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜で`, `〜においての`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using casual `で` is grammatically acceptable in speech but lacks the formal written register appropriate for a public issue analysis.
- **Why Correct:** `において` provides the formal written framing for highlighting Japan as the setting for this issue.
- **Example:** 日本において重要な問題だ。 (Đây là vấn đề quan trọng ở Nhật Bản.)

Grammar | 〜に関して

- **Meaning:** Regarding, concerning / Liên quan đến, về.
- **Trigger:** Formal discussions, inquiries, reports, or research regarding a specific matter or field.
- **Contrast:** `〜に関して` is a formal version of `〜について`, often covering a broader scope or systemic connection.
- **One-line Summary:** Formal pattern used to introduce a topic or domain of inquiry.
- **Formation:** N + に関して; N + に関する + N.
- **Semantic Field:** Topic & Scope / Chủ đề & Phạm vi liên quan.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜について`, `〜に対して`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `に対して` incorrectly frames the inquiry as a personal attitude directed against the matter rather than an inquiry about its details.
- **Why Correct:** `に関して` properly connects the question to the matter being inquired about.
- **Example:** その件に関して質問があります。 (Tôi có câu hỏi liên quan đến việc đó.)

Grammar | 〜に比べて

- **Meaning:** Compared to / So với, so sánh với.
- **Trigger:** Contrasting two subjects or time periods to highlight a difference in degree, quantity, or quality.
- **Contrast:** `〜に比べて` explicitly compares two entities to show contrast, whereas `〜に対して` shows direct opposition or direction.
- **One-line Summary:** Compares two subjects to highlight a difference.
- **Formation:** N + に比べて; V-plain-nonpast + のに比べて.
- **Semantic Field:** Comparison & Contrast / So sánh & Đối chiếu.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜より`, `〜に対して`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `に対して` expresses contrasting opposites or direction, whereas comparing temperatures between this year and last year requires `に比べて`.
- **Why Correct:** `に比べて` properly sets last year as the baseline for comparing the temperature.
- **Example:** 去年に比べて暑い。 (So với năm ngoái thì nóng hơn.)

Grammar | 〜に加えて

- **Meaning:** In addition to / Ngoài ra, thêm vào đó.
- **Trigger:** Adding another item, qualification, or situation on top of an existing one.
- **Contrast:** `〜に加えて` adds additional elements of similar valence, whereas `〜にかわって` substitutes one item for another.
- **One-line Summary:** Adds an extra element on top of what is already present.
- **Formation:** N + に加えて.
- **Semantic Field:** Addition & Accumulation / Bổ sung & Thêm vào.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜だけでなく`, `〜とともに`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `にかわって` implies substituting Chinese in place of English, contradicting the fact that the speaker studies both languages.
- **Why Correct:** `に加えて` correctly expresses that Chinese is being studied in addition to English.
- **Example:** 英語に加えて中国語も勉強する。 (Ngoài tiếng Anh, tôi còn học cả tiếng Trung.)

Grammar | 〜にかわって

- **Meaning:** Instead of, on behalf of / Thay cho, thay thế cho.
- **Trigger:** Performing an action in place of someone else, or replacing one item/person with another.
- **Contrast:** `〜にかわって` indicates substitution or representation, whereas `〜に加えて` indicates addition.
- **One-line Summary:** Indicates acting on behalf of or replacing another person or thing.
- **Formation:** N + にかわって / N + に代わり.
- **Semantic Field:** Substitution & Representation / Thay thế & Đại diện.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜のかわりに`, `〜に代わって`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `に加えて` implies attending alongside father, whereas the sentence specifies representing father in his absence.
- **Why Correct:** `にかわって` properly denotes attending the ceremony on behalf of the father.
- **Example:** 父にかわって出席した。 (Tôi đã tham dự thay cho bố.)

Grammar | 〜にとって

- **Meaning:** To, for, from the perspective of / Đối với, dành cho (góc nhìn đánh giá).
- **Trigger:** Stating a value judgment, evaluation, or emotional importance from the subjective standpoint of a person or group.
- **Contrast:** `〜にとって` marks a subjective standpoint of evaluation, whereas `〜に対して` marks a target of action/attitude, and `〜向け` marks an intended target demographic.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses a personal perspective, value, or evaluation.
- **Formation:** N + にとって.
- **Semantic Field:** Perspective & Evaluation / Góc nhìn & Đánh giá.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜として`, `〜に対して`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `に対して` implies directing an attitude toward the person, whereas describing how important someone is to oneself requires `にとって`.
- **Why Correct:** `にとって` correctly establishes the speaker's personal standpoint in evaluating the person's importance.
- **Example:** 私にとって大切な人です。 (Đối với tôi, đó là người quan trọng.)

Grammar | 〜向け

- **Meaning:** Intended for, aimed at / Dành cho, hướng tới (mục tiêu thiết kế/chế tạo).
- **Trigger:** Indicating the target audience or user group for whom a product, book, service, or facility was specially created.
- **Contrast:** `〜向け` indicates intentional design/target audience, whereas `〜向き` indicates natural suitability or fitting character.
- **One-line Summary:** Indicates that something was specially made or designed for a target group.
- **Formation:** N + 向け (or N + 向けの + N / N + 向けに).
- **Semantic Field:** Target Audience & Design Purpose / Đối tượng mục tiêu & Thiết kế.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜向き`, `〜ために`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `向き` implies natural suitability rather than intentionally publishing/designing the book specifically for beginners.
- **Why Correct:** `向け` correctly denotes that the book was specifically targeted and written for beginners.
- **Example:** 初心者向けの本です。 (Đây là cuốn sách dành cho người mới bắt đầu.)

Grammar | 〜向き

- **Meaning:** Suitable for, fitting for / Phù hợp với, thích hợp cho (bản chất tự nhiên).
- **Trigger:** Assessing whether something naturally suits a person's character, inclinations, skills, or nature.
- **Contrast:** `〜向き` indicates inherent natural compatibility, whereas `〜向け` indicates deliberate design for a demographic.
- **One-line Summary:** Indicates inherent suitability or natural compatibility.
- **Formation:** N + 向き (or N + 向きだ / N + 向きの + N).
- **Semantic Field:** Suitability & Compatibility / Sự phù hợp & Tương thích.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜向け`, `〜に合っている`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `向け` implies the job was manufactured/designed as a product for him, whereas assessing job-personality fit requires `向き`.
- **Why Correct:** `向いている` accurately describes that the job's demands naturally suit his personal skills and character.
- **Example:** この仕事は彼に向いている。 (Công việc này phù hợp với anh ấy.)

Grammar | 〜ほど

- **Meaning:** To the extent that, the more... the more / Đến mức; Càng... càng...
- **Trigger:** Illustrating extreme degree by comparing it to a vivid action/state, or expressing proportional change.
- **Contrast:** `〜ほど` expresses extreme degree via vivid illustration or proportion, whereas `〜くらい` expresses everyday extent or approximation.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses an extreme degree by vivid illustration or proportional change.
- **Formation:** V-plain / Adj-i / Adj-na + な / N + ほど.
- **Semantic Field:** Degree & Proportion / Mức độ & Tỷ lệ thuận.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜くらい`, `〜くらい...ない`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using simple `ように` lacks the vivid expression of emotional intensity reached (sad to the point of crying).
- **Why Correct:** `ほど` vividly emphasizes the extreme depth of sadness by referencing the threshold of tears.
- **Example:** 泣きたいほど悲しい。 (Tôi buồn đến mức muốn khóc.)

Grammar | 〜くらい

- **Meaning:** To the extent that, about / Đến mức; Khoảng, chừng.
- **Trigger:** Expressing degree, extent, or approximate quantity in daily situations.
- **Contrast:** `〜くらい` expresses general extent or approximation in everyday speech, whereas `〜ほど` carries a more dramatic or formal degree.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses approximate quantity or daily extent of a state.
- **Formation:** V-plain / Adj / N + くらい (or ぐらい).
- **Semantic Field:** Degree & Approximation / Mức độ & Ước lượng.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜ほど`, `〜ごろ`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using temporal `ごろ` is only used for specific points in time on a clock/calendar, not for degree of physical exhaustion.
- **Why Correct:** `くらい` correctly illustrates the degree of fatigue (tired to the extent of being unable to walk).
- **Example:** 歩けないくらい疲れた。 (Tôi mệt đến mức không thể đi được.)

Grammar | 〜さえ

- **Meaning:** Even, not even / Ngay cả, thậm chí... cũng.
- **Trigger:** Citing an extreme or basic example to emphasize that other obvious things are naturally included.
- **Contrast:** `〜さえ` presents an extreme baseline example to emphasize total extent, whereas `〜も` is simple addition/emphasis.
- **One-line Summary:** Emphasizes an extreme baseline example to highlight total extent.
- **Formation:** N (+ Particle) + さえ; V-te + さえ.
- **Semantic Field:** Extreme Example & Emphasis / Ví dụ cực đoan & Nhấn mạnh.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜すら`, `〜でさえ`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using simple particle `も` lacks the strong dramatic nuance of failing to know even the most fundamental detail (one's name).
- **Why Correct:** `さえ` strongly highlights that the ignorance is so complete that even the basic name is unknown.
- **Example:** 名前さえ知らない。 (Ngay cả tên tôi cũng không biết.)

Grammar | 〜こそ

- **Meaning:** Precisely, specially, for sure / Chính là, đúng là, nhất định (nhấn mạnh).
- **Trigger:** Strongly emphasizing a specific noun, time, or reason to single it out from all others.
- **Contrast:** `〜こそ` provides positive, determined exclusive emphasis, whereas `〜さえ` provides negative/extreme baseline emphasis.
- **One-line Summary:** Provides strong, exclusive emphasis to highlight a specific target or determination.
- **Formation:** N + こそ; Particle + こそ.
- **Semantic Field:** Exclusive Emphasis & Determination / Nhấn mạnh đặc quyền & Quyết tâm.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜こそすれ`, `〜まさに`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `さえ` implies an extreme negative baseline ("even this year"), which contradicts the positive strong determination to pass.
- **Why Correct:** `こそ` powerfully single-outs "this year" as the definitive year for passing the exam.
- **Example:** 今年こそ合格したい。 (Năm nay tôi nhất định muốn đỗ.)

Grammar | 〜しか〜ない

- **Meaning:** Have no choice but to, can only / Không còn cách nào khác, chỉ có thể...
- **Trigger:** Expressing that no other option or recourse exists except the named action.
- **Contrast:** `〜しか〜ない` highlights an unavoidable single remaining option, whereas `〜だけだ` expresses a neutral limitation ("only").
- **One-line Summary:** Indicates that no other alternative exists except the specified action.
- **Formation:** V-ru + しかない / N + しかない.
- **Semantic Field:** Single Choice & Unavoidability / Lựa chọn duy nhất & Bất khả kháng.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜よりほかない`, `〜ほかない`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using neutral `だけだ` lacks the strong feeling of resignation and lack of other alternatives conveyed by `しか〜ない`.
- **Why Correct:** `待つしかない` accurately expresses that waiting is the sole remaining course of action available.
- **Example:** 待つしかない。 (Chỉ còn cách chờ thôi.)

Grammar | 〜ものだ

- **Meaning:** Used to; Naturally is / Thường (thói quen quá khứ); Vốn dĩ là (bản chất/chân lý).
- **Trigger:** Expressing general truths, inherent properties of things, or nostalgic recall of past habits.
- **Contrast:** `〜ものだ` states universal truths, inherent nature, or nostalgic habits, whereas `〜ことだ` gives direct personal advice/instructions.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses inherent nature, general truths, or nostalgic past habits.
- **Formation:** V-ru / V-ta / Adj-i / Adj-na + な + ものだ.
- **Semantic Field:** Truth & General Nature / Chân lý & Bản chất tự nhiên.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜ことだ`, `〜ものではない`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `ことだ` turns the statement into an instruction/advice, whereas passing time is an objective natural truth (`ものだ`).
- **Why Correct:** `ものだ` properly reflects the universal emotional truth about how fast time naturally passes.
- **Example:** 時間がたつのは早いものだ。 (Thời gian trôi qua vốn là nhanh thật.)

Grammar | 〜ようだ

- **Meaning:** It seems like, looks as if / Có vẻ (như), dường như.
- **Trigger:** Stating a subjective conjecture or impression based on direct visual/sensory observation.
- **Contrast:** `〜ようだ` expresses conjecture based on overall sensory impression or resemblance, `〜そうだ` expresses conjecture from immediate visual signs, and `〜らしい` expresses conjecture from objective external evidence/rumors.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses conjecture or impression based on direct sensory observation.
- **Formation:** V-plain / Adj-i / Adj-na + な / N + の + ようだ.
- **Semantic Field:** Conjecture & Appearance / Phán đoán & Dáng vẻ.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜そうだ`, `〜らしい`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `そうだ` (`雨が降りそうだ`) indicates "it looks like it's about to rain right this second" based on sky appearance, whereas observing overall damp weather/clouds leads to the general conjecture `雨が降るようだ`.
- **Why Correct:** `ようだ` properly conveys the speaker's subjective impression/conjecture that rain is likely falling or going to fall.
- **Example:** 雨が降るようだ。 (Có vẻ như sắp mưa.)

Weak Points

1. **Nhầm lẫn Cặp Cấu trúc Nguồn thông tin vs Sự phụ thuộc (****〜によると** **vs** **〜によって****):**
    - Dễ dùng nhầm `によって` khi dẫn nguồn tin tức hoặc nhầm `によると` khi nói về sự thay đổi tùy theo đối tượng.
2. **Nhầm lẫn Nhóm Cấu trúc Chỉ Đối tượng & Góc nhìn (****〜に対して** **vs** **〜について** **vs** **〜に関して** **vs** **〜にとって****):**
    - Rất dễ lẫn lộn giữa thái độ hướng về đối tượng (`に対して`), chủ đề trao đổi (`について`/`に関して`), và đánh giá từ góc nhìn cá nhân (`にとって`).
3. **Phân biệt Cặp Ngữ pháp Thiết kế Mục tiêu vs Tính chất Tự nhiên (****〜向け** **vs** **〜向き****):**
    - Chưa phân biệt rõ giữa sản phẩm/sách được thiết kế cho người dùng (`向け`) và tính cách/kỹ năng tự nhiên phù hợp với công việc (`向き`).
4. **Nhầm lẫn Nhóm Cấu trúc Chỉ Mức độ & Nhấn mạnh (****〜ほど** **vs** **〜くらい** **vs** **〜さえ** **vs** **〜こそ****):**
    - Dễ nhầm giữa hình ảnh biểu đạt mức độ sống động (`ほど`), ước lượng thông thường (`くらい`), ví dụ cực đoan (`さえ`), và nhấn mạnh duy nhất (`こそ`).
