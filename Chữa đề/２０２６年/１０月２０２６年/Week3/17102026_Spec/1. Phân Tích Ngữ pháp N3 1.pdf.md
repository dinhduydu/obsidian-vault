Error Analysis

Tình trạng Dữ liệu Bài làm

Tài liệu `1.1. Ngữ pháp N3 1.pdf` cung cấp cấu trúc ngữ pháp lý thuyết và câu ví dụ minh họa, không có dữ liệu câu sai thực nghiệm từ bài làm của học viên.

Bẫy Kiến thức & Nhầm lẫn Phổ biến ở 20 Cấu trúc Ngữ pháp N3

1. **Phân biệt Cặp Nguyên nhân - Kết quả Tích cực ↔ Tiêu cực:**
    - `〜おかげで` (nhờ có -> dùng cho kết quả tốt) ↔ `〜せいで` (tại vì -> dùng cho kết quả xấu, mang tính đổ lỗi).
2. **Phân biệt Hành vi Nỗ lực ↔ Sự Thay đổi Trạng thái/Khả năng:**
    - `〜ようにする` (cố gắng duy trì thói quen/hành vi) ↔ `〜ようになる` (chuyển biến trạng thái/khả năng theo thời gian).
3. **Phân biệt Quyết định Cá nhân ↔ Quyết định Tập thể/Quy định:**
    - `〜ことにする` (cá nhân tự quyết định) ↔ `〜ことになる` (được quyết định bởi tổ chức, hoàn cảnh hoặc quy định).
4. **Phân biệt Thì Hoàn thành Ngay lập tức ↔ Cảm giác Tâm lý Vừa mới:**
    - `〜たところ` (vừa mới làm xong ngay tại thời điểm nói) ↔ `〜ばかり` (cảm giác vừa mới làm xong theo đánh giá chủ quan dù thời gian đã trôi qua).
5. **Phân biệt Mức độ Khẳng định / Phán đoán Logic ↔ Chủ quan:**
    - `〜に違いない` (khẳng định chắc chắn dựa trên cảm nhận/chứng cứ trực tiếp) ↔ `〜はずだ` (chắc chắn/đáng lẽ phải xảy ra dựa trên logic, kế hoạch).

Knowledge Extracted

Grammar | 〜うちに

- **Meaning:** While, before / Trong khi, trước khi.
- **Trigger:** Expressing an action done while a temporary state holds, before a change occurs.
- **Contrast:** `〜うちに` emphasizes taking action before a state changes, whereas `〜あいだに` simply marks a neutral time span between start and end.
- **One-line Summary:** Perform an action while a favorable state holds or before it changes.
- **Formation:** V-ru / V-nai / V-teiru + うちに; N + の + うちに; Adj-i + うちに; Adj-na + な + うちに.
- **Semantic Field:** Time & Condition / Thời gian & Điều kiện.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜あいだに`, `〜うちに`.
- **Confusion Index:** High (frequently confused with `〜あいだに`).
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `あいだに` is incorrect when the sentence implies performing an action urgently before a state changes (e.g., while still in Japan), as `あいだに` lacks the "before conditions change" nuance.
- **Why Correct:** `うちに` is correct because it specifies taking advantage of the current window of opportunity (being in Japan) before returning home.
- **Example:** 日本にいるうちに、富士山を見たいです。 (Tôi muốn ngắm núi Phú Sĩ trong khi còn ở Nhật.)

Grammar | 〜おかげで

- **Meaning:** Thanks to, owing to / Nhờ, nhờ vào (kết quả tốt).
- **Trigger:** Attributing a desirable, successful outcome to someone's help or a favorable cause.
- **Contrast:** `〜おかげで` is used exclusively for positive outcomes, while `〜せいで` is used for negative blame.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses gratitude for a positive result caused by someone or something.
- **Formation:** V / Adj / N + の + おかげで.
- **Semantic Field:** Cause & Effect (Positive) / Nguyên nhân & Kết quả (Tích cực).
- **Related Grammar:** `〜せいで`, `〜ために`.
- **Confusion Index:** High (frequently confused with `〜せいで`).
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `せいで` is incorrect because passing an exam is a positive result, and `せいで` expresses blame for negative outcomes.
- **Why Correct:** `おかげで` is correct because passing the exam is a favorable result achieved thanks to the teacher's help.
- **Example:** 先生のおかげで合格できました。 (Nhờ thầy/cô mà tôi đã đỗ.)

Grammar | 〜せいで

- **Meaning:** Because of, due to (blame) / Vì, do (kết quả xấu, đổ lỗi).
- **Trigger:** Blaming a specific cause for an undesirable or negative result.
- **Contrast:** `〜せいで` attributes blame for bad results, whereas `〜おかげで` gives credit for good results.
- **One-line Summary:** Blames a cause for an undesirable or negative outcome.
- **Formation:** V / Adj / N + の + せいで.
- **Semantic Field:** Cause & Effect (Negative) / Nguyên nhân & Kết quả (Tiêu cực).
- **Related Grammar:** `〜おかげで`, `〜ために`.
- **Confusion Index:** High (frequently confused with `〜おかげで`).
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `おかげで` is incorrect because a match cancellation due to rain is an undesirable outcome, not something to express gratitude for.
- **Why Correct:** `せいで` is correct because it properly assigns responsibility to the rain for causing the match cancellation.
- **Example:** 雨のせいで試合が中止になった。 (Vì trời mưa nên trận đấu đã bị hủy.)

Grammar | 〜ために

- **Meaning:** Because of / In order to / Vì, do; Để.
- **Trigger:** Expressing an objective cause/reason for an event, or stating a goal/purpose.
- **Contrast:** `〜ために` indicates objective cause or goal, unlike `〜おかげで`/`〜せいで` which carry strong emotional credit or blame.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses an objective cause/reason or an intended purpose.
- **Formation:** V-ru + ために (purpose) / V-plain + ために; N + の + ために (cause/purpose).
- **Semantic Field:** Cause & Reason / Purpose / Nguyên nhân & Mục đích.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜おかげで`, `〜せいで`, `〜ように`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `ように` with a controllable volitional verb for purpose is mismatched compared to `ために` which connects directly with volitional goals or causes.
- **Why Correct:** `ために` neutrally states the objective reason (an accident) that caused the delay without emotional bias.
- **Example:** 事故のために遅れました。 (Vì tai nạn nên tôi đã đến muộn.)

Grammar | 〜ようにする

- **Meaning:** Try to, make an effort to / Cố gắng, nỗ lực để làm (hoặc không làm).
- **Trigger:** Describing a continuous personal effort or habit to achieve a target behavior.
- **Contrast:** `〜ようにする` indicates conscious personal effort/habit, while `〜ようになる` indicates an automatic or gradual change in state/ability.
- **One-line Summary:** Indicates a conscious habit or continuous effort to do something.
- **Formation:** V-ru / V-nai + ようにする (or ようにしている for ongoing habit).
- **Semantic Field:** Effort & Habit / Nỗ lực & Thói quen.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜ようになる`, `〜ことにする`.
- **Confusion Index:** High (frequently confused with `〜ようになる`).
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `ようになる` is incorrect because daily exercise requires conscious personal willpower (`ようにしている`), whereas `ようになる` denotes a natural change of state.
- **Why Correct:** `ようにしている` accurately describes the speaker's conscious daily commitment to exercise.
- **Example:** 毎日運動するようにしています。 (Tôi cố gắng để vận động mỗi ngày.)

Grammar | 〜ようになる

- **Meaning:** Come to be able to, reach a point where / Trở nên có thể, dần dần làm được.
- **Trigger:** Indicating a gradual change in ability, state, or habit over time.
- **Contrast:** `〜ようになる` denotes an acquired ability or state transition, while `〜ようにする` denotes intentional effort.
- **One-line Summary:** Describes a gradual transition into a new state or ability.
- **Formation:** V-potential / V-ru / V-nai + ようになる.
- **Semantic Field:** Change of State / Thay đổi trạng thái & Khả năng.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜ようにする`, `〜ことになる`.
- **Confusion Index:** High (frequently confused with `〜ようにする`).
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `ようにする` is incorrect when expressing acquired language capability, as acquiring fluency is a state change rather than a mere temporary action.
- **Why Correct:** `できるようになりました` properly uses `ようになる` with the potential verb form to indicate gained Japanese speaking ability.
- **Example:** 日本語が話せるようになりました。 (Tôi đã trở nên có thể nói tiếng Nhật.)

Grammar | 〜ことになる

- **Meaning:** It has been decided that / Đã được quyết định là (bởi tập thể, tổ chức, hoàn cảnh).
- **Trigger:** Decisions made by external circumstances, organizations, rules, or committees.
- **Contrast:** `〜ことになる` indicates an external or official group decision, while `〜ことにする` indicates a personal personal choice.
- **One-line Summary:** Marks an external, official, or organizational decision.
- **Formation:** V-ru / V-nai + ことになる.
- **Semantic Field:** Decision & Rule / Quyết định & Quy định.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜ことにする`, `〜ようにする`.
- **Confusion Index:** High (frequently confused with `〜ことにする`).
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `ことにした` implies an individual personal decision, which is improper for an official conference postponement decided by a board or circumstance.
- **Why Correct:** `延期することになった` properly expresses that the meeting postponement was determined as an official organizational outcome.
- **Example:** 会議は延期することになった。 (Việc họp đã được quyết định là sẽ hoãn lại.)

Grammar | 〜ことにする

- **Meaning:** Decide to / Quyết định làm (bởi cá nhân).
- **Trigger:** Expressing a subjective personal resolution or decision to adopt a behavior.
- **Contrast:** `〜ことにする` is a personal volitional decision, whereas `〜ことになる` is an external or collective decision.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses a personal decision or self-imposed resolution.
- **Formation:** V-ru / V-nai + ことにする.
- **Semantic Field:** Personal Resolution / Quyết định cá nhân.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜ことになる`, `〜ようにする`.
- **Confusion Index:** High (frequently confused with `〜ことになる`).
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `ことになった` frames quitting smoking as an external mandate imposed by others rather than a self-chosen personal resolution.
- **Why Correct:** `禁煙することにした` accurately reflects the speaker's own personal choice to quit smoking.
- **Example:** 禁煙することにした。 (Tôi đã quyết định sẽ bỏ thuốc lá.)

Grammar | 〜たところ

- **Meaning:** Just finished doing, just did / Vừa mới làm xong thi...
- **Trigger:** Immediately following the completion of an action, marking the immediate present moment.
- **Contrast:** `〜たところ` emphasizes exact temporal completion right now, while `〜ばかり` emphasizes psychological recentness (even if time has passed).
- **One-line Summary:** Indicates an action completed at the exact present moment.
- **Formation:** V-ta + ところだ / ところです.
- **Semantic Field:** Momentary Completion / Thời điểm vừa hoàn thành.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜ばかり`, `〜ところだった`.
- **Confusion Index:** High (frequently confused with `〜ばかり`).
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `ばかり` focuses on subjective perception of recentness, whereas `今帰ってきたところ` specifically grounds the arrival at the immediate present moment.
- **Why Correct:** `今帰ってきたところです` is correct because `今` anchors the completion directly at the present instant.
- **Example:** 今帰ってきたところです。 (Tôi vừa mới về đến.)

Grammar | 〜ところだった

- **Meaning:** Was about to, almost / Suýt nữa thì, vừa định...
- **Trigger:** Narrowly escaping a bad situation, or being right on the verge of doing something.
- **Contrast:** `〜ところだった` (almost happened / on the verge) vs `〜たところだ` (just completed).
- **One-line Summary:** Indicates almost happening or being on the verge of doing something.
- **Formation:** V-ru + ところだった.
- **Semantic Field:** Near-occurrence & Imminence / Suýt xảy ra & Sắp sửa.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜たところ`, `〜そうになる`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `たところだ` indicates an action already completed, whereas `出かけるところだった` indicates being on the verge of stepping out.
- **Why Correct:** `ところだった` correctly captures the imminent timing right before stepping out of the house.
- **Example:** 出かけるところだった。 (Tôi vừa định đi ra ngoài thì...)

Grammar | 〜ばかり

- **Meaning:** Just did, recently / Vừa mới (cảm giác tâm lý vừa làm xong).
- **Trigger:** Expressing that an action was completed recently according to the speaker's subjective feeling.
- **Contrast:** `〜ばかり` allows a longer elapsed objective time as long as it feels recent to the speaker, unlike `〜たところ` which requires immediate temporal closeness.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses a subjective sense that an action was completed recently.
- **Formation:** V-ta + ばかり.
- **Semantic Field:** Subjective Recentness / Thời điểm hoàn thành theo cảm nhận.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜たところ`, `〜ばかりいる`.
- **Confusion Index:** High (frequently confused with `〜たところ`).
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `たところ` requires immediate temporal completion, whereas `食べたばかり` emphasizes the speaker's feeling of fullness from having recently eaten lunch.
- **Why Correct:** `昼ご飯を食べたばかりです` correctly uses `たばかり` to convey that eating lunch feels very recent to the speaker.
- **Example:** 昼ご飯を食べたばかりです。 (Tôi vừa mới ăn trưa xong.)

Grammar | 〜ばかりいる

- **Meaning:** Do nothing but, keep doing / Chỉ toàn làm..., suốt ngày làm...
- **Trigger:** Criticizing or pointing out a repeated, exclusive action done to excess.
- **Contrast:** `〜ばかりいる` carries a negative or critical tone toward excessive repetition, unlike neutral frequency adverbs.
- **One-line Summary:** Criticizes doing an action exclusively and excessively.
- **Formation:** V-te + ばかりいる.
- **Semantic Field:** Excessive Action & Criticism / Hành động quá mức & Phê bình.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜ばかり`, `〜てばかりいる`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using plain progressive `遊んでいる` lacks the essential critical nuance of playing exclusively to excess all day long.
- **Why Correct:** `遊んでばかりいる` correctly conveys criticism that he does nothing but play all day.
- **Example:** 彼は遊んでばかりいる。 (Anh ấy chỉ suốt ngày chơi thôi.)

Grammar | 〜たびに

- **Meaning:** Every time, whenever / Mỗi khi, mỗi lần.
- **Trigger:** Recurring situations where doing action A always brings about situation B.
- **Contrast:** `〜たびに` cannot be used for routine daily natural occurrences (like waking up every morning), but rather for distinct repeated occasions.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses that every single time action A occurs, B happens.
- **Formation:** V-ru + たびに; N + の + たびに.
- **Semantic Field:** Repetition & Habitual Correlation / Sự lặp lại & Mối liên hệ chu kỳ.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜たところ`, `〜につけて`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `ときに` merely states a single time reference, whereas `たびに` highlights the repeated rule that taking photos happens on every single trip.
- **Why Correct:** `旅行するたびに` accurately connects every travel instance with photo-taking.
- **Example:** 旅行するたびに写真を撮る。 (Mỗi khi đi du lịch, tôi đều chụp ảnh.)

Grammar | 〜最中に

- **Meaning:** In the middle of, right when / Trong lúc, giữa chừng khi đang...
- **Trigger:** An unexpected interrupting event occurring right in the peak middle of an ongoing main activity.
- **Contrast:** `〜最中に` emphasizes an unexpected interruption during an active peak moment, whereas `〜あいだに` is a neutral time window.
- **One-line Summary:** Marks an unexpected interruption during the peak of an ongoing action.
- **Formation:** V-teiru + 最中に; N + の + 最中に.
- **Semantic Field:** Time & Interruption / Thời gian & Sự ngắt quãng.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜うちに`, `〜あいだに`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `あいだに` lacks the specific nuance of an abrupt phone ring interrupting the peak middle of an important meeting.
- **Why Correct:** `会議の最中に` vividly captures the phone ringing right in the middle of the meeting.
- **Example:** 会議の最中に電話が鳴った。 (Trong lúc họp, điện thoại đã reo.)

Grammar | 〜ついでに

- **Meaning:** While doing A, take the opportunity to do B / Nhân tiện, tiện thể.
- **Trigger:** Capitalizing on the primary action A to conveniently perform a secondary action B.
- **Contrast:** `〜ついでに` has a clear primary action vs secondary opportunistic action structure, whereas `〜ながら` requires two simultaneous actions.
- **One-line Summary:** Doing a secondary action opportunistically while performing a main action.
- **Formation:** V-ru / V-ta + ついでに; N + の + ついでに.
- **Semantic Field:** Secondary Opportunity / Cơ hội nhân tiện.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜がてら`, `〜ついでに`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `ながら` implies doing both actions at the exact same physical instant (e.g., walking while eating), which is impossible for shopping and going to a bank branch simultaneously.
- **Why Correct:** `買い物のついでに` correctly frames bank-visiting as an opportunistic secondary errand during a shopping trip.
- **Example:** 買い物のついでに銀行へ行った。 (Trong lúc đi mua sắm thì tôi tiện thể đi ngân hàng.)

Grammar | 〜に違い無い

- **Meaning:** Must be, bound to be / Chắc chắn là, hẳn là, nhất định là.
- **Trigger:** Strong subjective conviction or deduction based on direct evidence or observation.
- **Contrast:** `〜に違いない` is a strong conviction based on speaker's intuition/evidence, while `〜はずだ` is an expectation based on logical reasoning/schedule.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses strong subjective conviction or certainty about a fact.
- **Formation:** V-plain / Adj / N + に違いない.
- **Semantic Field:** High Certainty & Deduction / Phán đoán & Khẳng định chắc chắn.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜はずだ`, `〜に相違ない`.
- **Confusion Index:** High (frequently confused with `〜はずだ`).
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `はずだ` expresses what ought to happen logically based on a premise/plan, whereas pointing out a criminal based on evidence reflects direct strong subjective conviction (`に違いない`).
- **Why Correct:** `犯人に違いない` properly expresses the strong conviction that he must be the culprit.
- **Example:** 彼は犯人に違いない。 (Chắc chắn anh ta là thủ phạm.)

Grammar | 〜はずだ

- **Meaning:** Ought to be, expected to / Đáng lẽ phải, lẽ ra phải, chắc chắn là (theo logic/kế hoạch).
- **Trigger:** Expecting a state or event based on logical deduction, objective schedule, or prior knowledge.
- **Contrast:** `〜はずだ` relies on objective logic/schedule expectations, whereas `〜に違いない` relies on strong personal conviction.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses an expectation based on objective logic, plans, or schedules.
- **Formation:** V-plain / Adj-i / Adj-na + な / N + の + はずだ.
- **Semantic Field:** Logical Expectation / Kỳ vọng theo logic & Kế hoạch.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜に違いない`, `〜わけだ`.
- **Confusion Index:** High (frequently confused with `〜に違いない`).
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `に違いない` conveys personal subjective conviction, whereas expecting someone's arrival based on a confirmed invitation or train schedule is a logical expectation (`はずだ`).
- **Why Correct:** `来るはずだ` correctly states the logical expectation that he ought to arrive based on prior arrangement.
- **Example:** 彼は来るはずだ。 (Lẽ ra anh ấy phải đến.)

Grammar | 〜わけだ

- **Meaning:** That explains why, no wonder / Thì ra là, hóa ra là (hiểu ra lý do).
- **Trigger:** Realizing or understanding the logical reason behind a situation after hearing an explanation.
- **Contrast:** `〜わけだ` expresses logical realization of a reason, while `〜わけではない` expresses partial negation.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses a logical conclusion or realization explaining a cause.
- **Formation:** V-plain / Adj-i / Adj-na + な / N + の・である + わけだ.
- **Semantic Field:** Logical Realization / Kết luận logic & Sự vỡ lẽ.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜わけではない`, `〜はずだ`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `わけではない` would negate the statement ("it doesn't mean that"), contradicting the speaker's agreement/understanding (`なるほど`).
- **Why Correct:** `そういうわけだ` correctly confirms that the speaker now understands the logical reason behind the matter.
- **Example:** なるほど、そういうわけだ。 (Thì ra là vậy.)

Grammar | 〜わけではない

- **Meaning:** It does not mean that, not necessarily / Không hẳn là, không nhất thiết là.
- **Trigger:** Softening a statement or clarifying that a total assumption is not entirely true.
- **Contrast:** `〜わけではない` negates an absolute inference/assumption, whereas `〜ない` is a simple direct negation.
- **One-line Summary:** Softly negates a full assumption or absolute conclusion.
- **Formation:** V-plain / Adj-i / Adj-na + な / N + の・である + わけではない.
- **Semantic Field:** Partial Negation / Phủ định một phần & Giải thích làm mềm.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜わけだ`, `〜とは限らない`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using simple negation `嫌いではない` lacks the nuanced clarification that "it's not that I dislike it (though it might seem so)".
- **Why Correct:** `嫌いなわけではない` accurately clarifies that the speaker does not completely dislike it, avoiding a harsh or misunderstanding tone.
- **Example:** 嫌いなわけではない。 (Không phải là tôi không thích.)

Grammar | 〜ということだ

- **Meaning:** It means that, I heard that / Có nghĩa là, tức là; Nghe nói là.
- **Trigger:** Summarizing a conclusion or reporting hearsay information.
- **Contrast:** `〜ということだ` summarizes an equivalent meaning or restates news hearsay, whereas `〜のことだ` defines a specific word.
- **One-line Summary:** Restates a logical conclusion or conveys reported hearsay.
- **Formation:** V-plain / Sentence + ということだ.
- **Semantic Field:** Summary & Hearsay / Tóm tắt & Truyền đạt tin đồn.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜わけだ`, `〜そうだ`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `ことです` would be a simple noun definition rather than summarizing an official status/conclusion of absence.
- **Why Correct:** `欠席ということだ` correctly summarizes the conclusion that the person is absent.
- **Example:** 欠席ということだ。 (Có nghĩa là vắng mặt.)

Weak Points

1. **Nhầm lẫn Cặp Nguyên nhân - Kết quả Mang Tính Sắc thái Cảm xúc (****〜おかげで** **vs** **〜せいで****):**
    - Dễ dùng sai `せいで` trong câu có kết quả tích cực hoặc dùng `おかげで` trong câu đổ lỗi cho thiên tai/tai nạn.
2. **Nhầm lẫn Nhóm Ngữ pháp Chỉ Quyết định & Nỗ lực (****〜ようにする** **/** **〜ようになる** **/** **〜ことにする** **/** **〜ことになる****):**
    - Rất dễ nhầm giữa nỗ lực duy trì thói quen cá nhân (`ようにする`), sự chuyển biến khả năng tự nhiên (`ようになる`), quyết định cá nhân (`ことにする`), và quyết định tập thể/hoàn cảnh (`ことになる`).
3. **Nhầm lẫn Cấu trúc Chỉ Thời điểm Vừa Hoàn thành (****〜たところ** **vs** **〜ばかり****):**
    - Chưa phân biệt rõ giữa thời điểm hoàn thành ngay tại chỗ (`たところ`) và khoảng thời gian vừa mới xảy ra theo cảm nhận chủ quan (`ばかり`).
4. **Nhầm lẫn Mức độ Khẳng định Phán đoán (****〜に違いない** **vs** **〜はずだ****):**
    - Nhầm giữa phán đoán chắc chắn dựa trên chứng cứ/trực giác cá nhân (`に違いない`) và kỳ vọng đỗ/đến dựa trên kế hoạch/logic (`はずだ`).
