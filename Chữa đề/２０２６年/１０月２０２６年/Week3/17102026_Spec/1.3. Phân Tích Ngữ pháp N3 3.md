Bẫy Kiến thức & Nhầm lẫn Phổ biến ở 20 Cấu trúc Ngữ pháp N3

1. **Phân biệt Nhóm Phán đoán & Phỏng đoán Dáng vẻ (****〜みたいだ** **vs** **〜らしい** **vs** **〜そうだ（様態）** **vs** **〜そうだ（伝聞）****):**
    - `〜みたいだ`: Phỏng đoán dựa trên hình dáng bên ngoài hoặc so sánh ví von trực quan (đi trực tiếp với danh từ).
    - `〜らしい`: Phỏng đoán dựa trên chứng cứ khách quan hoặc tin đồn gián tiếp.
    - `〜そうだ（様態）`: Bỏ đuôi `i` tính từ hoặc đi với thể Bỏ `masu` của động từ để chỉ cảm nhận thị giác trực tiếp ("trông có vẻ").
    - `〜そうだ（伝聞）`: Đi với thể thông thường (Plain form) để truyền đạt lại tin tức ("nghe nói là").
2. **Phân biệt Trạng thái Kết quả Chuẩn bị & Hành động Ý chí (****〜ておく** **vs** **〜てある** **vs** **〜ている****):**
    - `〜ておく`: Hành động chuẩn bị trước cho tương lai.
    - `〜てある`: Trạng thái kết quả của một ngoại động từ được ai đó thực hiện có mục đích và còn lưu lại.
3. **Phân biệt Cho - Nhận Hành động & Mong muốn (****〜てほしい** **vs** **〜てもらう****):**
    - `〜てほしい`: Bày tỏ mong muốn đối phương thực hiện hành động.
    - `〜てもらう`: Nói về việc mình nhận được hành động giúp đỡ từ người khác (`N に 〜てもらう`).
4. **Phân biệt Phạm vi, Tư cách & Giới hạn (****〜として** **vs** **〜といっても** **vs** **〜としても** **vs** **〜にすぎない****):**
    - `〜として`: Với tư cách/vai trò.
    - `〜といっても`: Đưa ra thực tế khiêm tốn để tránh hiểu nhầm ("dù nói là... nhưng").
    - `〜としても`: Giả định nhượng bộ ("dù cho/ngay cả khi... đi nữa").
    - `〜にすぎない`: Hạ thấp mức độ ("chỉ là... mà thôi").
5. **Phân biệt Nhóm Biến đổi Tiến trình Tỷ lệ (****〜にしたがって** **vs** **〜につれて** **vs** **〜とともに****):**
    - `〜にしたがって`: Biến đổi tỷ lệ theo tiến trình kéo theo hoặc tuân theo quy tắc.
    - `〜につれて`: Biến đổi tỷ lệ tự nhiên kéo theo theo một chiều.
    - `〜とともに`: Cùng nhau hoặc hai sự việc xảy ra đồng thời.

Knowledge Extracted

Grammar | 〜みたいだ

- **Meaning:** Looks like, seems like / Có vẻ, trông như.
- **Trigger:** Subjective observation or resemblance based on visual appearance or similarity to a noun/verb.
- **Contrast:** `〜みたいだ` is casual/colloquial for resemblance and subjective judgment, whereas `〜らしい` relies on objective hearsay/evidence, and `〜そうだ` indicates immediate visual impression.
- **One-line Summary:** Colloquial expression for subjective resemblance or appearance.
- **Formation:** V-plain / Adj / N + みたいだ.
- **Semantic Field:** Conjecture & Resemblance / Phán đoán & Phỏng đoán dáng vẻ.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜らしい`, `〜そうだ（様態）`, `〜ようだ`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `〜そうだ（様態）` with a direct noun (`学生そうだ`) is ungrammatical; `〜そうだ` attaches to adjective stems or verb stems, whereas `〜みたいだ` directly attaches to nouns (`学生みたいだ`).
- **Why Correct:** `〜みたいだ` directly connects with the noun `学生` to express that he looks or acts like a student.
- **Example:** 彼は学生みたいだ。 (Anh ấy trông như là học sinh/sinh viên.)

Grammar | 〜らしい

- **Meaning:** It seems, I heard that / Có vẻ, hình như, nghe nói là.
- **Trigger:** Conjecture based on external reports, rumors, or indirect evidence.
- **Contrast:** `〜らしい` is based on objective external evidence or hearsay, whereas `〜みたいだ` is based on subjective direct perception or visual resemblance.
- **One-line Summary:** Conjecture based on objective evidence or hearsay.
- **Formation:** V-plain / Adj / N + らしい.
- **Semantic Field:** Hearsay & Objective Conjecture / Truyền đạt & Phỏng đoán khách quan.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜みたいだ`, `〜そうだ（伝聞）`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `〜みたいだ` implies a subjective visual resemblance, which is incorrect when relaying an objective health report about someone's condition.
- **Why Correct:** `〜らしい` properly indicates a judgment based on objective reports or hearsay that he is doing well.
- **Example:** 彼は元気らしい。 (Hình như anh ấy khỏe.)

Grammar | 〜そうだ（様態）

- **Meaning:** Looks like, appears to be / Trông có vẻ (diễn tả trạng thái bên ngoài).
- **Trigger:** Immediate visual impression before an action occurs or evaluating visual appearance of a state/quality.
- **Contrast:** `〜そうだ（様態）` expresses immediate visual impression (looks tasty/about to fall), whereas `〜そうだ（伝聞）` reports hearsay information ("I heard that...").
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses immediate visual impression or appearance.
- **Formation:** V-stem + そうだ; Adj-i (stem) + そうだ; Adj-na (stem) + そうだ.
- **Semantic Field:** Visual Impression & Appearance / Cảm nhận thị giác & Dáng vẻ.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜そうだ（伝聞）`, `〜みたいだ`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using plain form + `そうだ` (`おいしいそうだ`) changes the meaning to hearsay ("I heard it is delicious"), failing to express the immediate visual impression ("it looks delicious").
- **Why Correct:** `おいしそうだ` drops the `-i` ending from `おいしい` to form the conjecture of visual appearance.
- **Example:** おいしそうだ。 (Trông có vẻ ngon.)

Grammar | 〜そうだ（伝聞）

- **Meaning:** I heard that, report says / Nghe nói, hình như (diễn tả thông tin nghe được).
- **Trigger:** Relaying news, weather forecasts, or rumors received from another source.
- **Contrast:** `〜そうだ（伝聞）` connects to full plain-form sentences to relay hearsay, whereas `〜そうだ（様態）` connects to verb/adj stems to show visual appearance.
- **One-line Summary:** Relays hearsay or reported information from an external source.
- **Formation:** V-plain / Adj-plain / N + だ + そうだ.
- **Semantic Field:** Hearsay & Information Relaying / Truyền đạt thông tin.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜そうだ（様態）`, `〜によると`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using verb stem + `そうだ` (`降りそうだ`) means "it looks like it's about to rain" (visual conjecture), which misinterprets a weather forecast report ("I heard it will rain").
- **Why Correct:** `雨が降るそうだ` uses the plain non-past verb `降る` before `そうだ` to correctly relay the forecast hearsay.
- **Example:** 雨が降るそうだ。 (Nghe nói là trời sẽ mưa.)

Grammar | 〜てしまう

- **Meaning:** End up doing (unintentionally), completely finish / Lỡ..., kết thúc, làm xong (thường mang ý ngoài ý muốn).
- **Trigger:** Regret over an unintended mistake, or completion of an action with finality.
- **Contrast:** `〜てしまう` expresses regret or complete completion, whereas `〜ておく` expresses advance preparation.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses complete finish or regret over an unintended action.
- **Formation:** V-te + しまう (casual: 〜ちゃう / 〜じゃう).
- **Semantic Field:** Regret & Completion / Bất cẩn, hối tiếc & Hoàn thành.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜ておく`, `〜てある`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using simple past form (`財布をなくした`) states the fact neutrally without conveying the emotional regret and sense of irrecoverable loss.
- **Why Correct:** `財布をなくしてしまった` explicitly adds the nuance of emotional regret and unintentional error over losing the wallet.
- **Example:** 財布をなくしてしまった。 (Tôi đã làm mất ví rồi.)

Grammar | 〜てみる

- **Meaning:** Try doing, do and see what happens / Thử..., thử làm... xem sao.
- **Trigger:** Experiential or exploratory action taken to discover the outcome or taste.
- **Contrast:** `〜てみる` means testing or trying an action to see the result, whereas `〜しようとする` means attempting/trying to perform an action.
- **One-line Summary:** Performing an action as a trial to see the outcome.
- **Formation:** V-te + みる.
- **Semantic Field:** Trial & Exploration / Thử nghiệm & Khám phá.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜ようとする`, `〜ておく`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `〜ておく` means preparing food in advance, which contradicts the speaker's invitation to taste-test the food right now.
- **Why Correct:** `食べてみてください` properly invites someone to try eating the food to see how it tastes.
- **Example:** 食べてみてください。 (Hãy thử ăn xem.)

Grammar | 〜ておく

- **Meaning:** Do in advance, leave as is for later / Làm trước, chuẩn bị trước.
- **Trigger:** Taking an preparatory action in anticipation of a future need or event.
- **Contrast:** `〜ておく` indicates advance action by an agent, whereas `〜てある` describes the resulting state already prepared and visible.
- **One-line Summary:** Performing an action in advance for future convenience.
- **Formation:** V-te + おく (casual: 〜とく).
- **Semantic Field:** Advance Preparation / Chuẩn bị trước.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜てある`, `〜てしまう`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `〜てある` (`予約してある`) describes an existing state ("it is booked"), whereas making a new booking reservation in advance requires the active volitional preparation form `〜ておく`.
- **Why Correct:** `予約しておきます` actively expresses the intention to make a reservation in advance.
- **Example:** 予約しておきます。 (Tôi sẽ đặt trước.)

Grammar | 〜てある

- **Meaning:** Has been done (and remains in that state) / Đã được làm xong; ở trạng thái đã....
- **Trigger:** Describing a visible state resulting from a intentional transitive verb action done by someone.
- **Contrast:** `〜てある` uses transitive verbs to show a result left intentionally, while `〜ている` with intransitive verbs merely describes an ongoing state without emphasizing purposeful preparation.
- **One-line Summary:** Describes a resulting state left behind after a intentional action.
- **Formation:** V-transitive-te + ある.
- **Semantic Field:** Resulting State / Trạng thái kết quả do tác động.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜ている`, `〜ておく`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using intransitive `開いている` simply notes the window is open, ignoring the purposeful intent that someone opened it and left it open.
- **Why Correct:** `窓が開けてある` uses the transitive verb `開ける` with `〜てある` to show the window was intentionally opened and remains open.
- **Example:** 窓が開けてある。 (Cửa sổ đang được mở sẵn.)

Grammar | 〜てほしい

- **Meaning:** Want someone to do / Muốn ai đó..., mong ai đó....
- **Trigger:** Expressing a personal desire for another person to perform a specific action.
- **Contrast:** `〜てほしい` expresses desire for another person's action, whereas `〜たい` expresses desire for one's own action.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses a desire for someone else to perform an action.
- **Formation:** N に + V-te + ほしい.
- **Semantic Field:** Desire for Other's Action / Mong muốn hành động từ người khác.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜てもらいたい`, `〜たい`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `〜たい` (`行きたい`) expresses the speaker's own desire to go, which fails to express wanting another person to come.
- **Why Correct:** `来てほしい` directly targets another person coming to the speaker's location.
- **Example:** 来てほしい。 (Tôi muốn bạn đến.)

Grammar | 〜てもらう

- **Meaning:** Receive a favor of doing, get someone to do / Được ai đó làm giúp; nhờ ai đó....
- **Trigger:** Expressing appreciation for an action done by someone else for the speaker's benefit.
- **Contrast:** `〜てもらう` focuses on the receiver getting a favor, whereas `〜てくれる` focuses on the giver doing a favor for the speaker.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses receiving a beneficial action or favor from someone.
- **Formation:** N に + V-te + もらう.
- **Semantic Field:** Benefactive Action / Nhận hành động giúp đỡ.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜てくれる`, `〜てあげる`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `〜てあげる` implies the speaker taught the friend, reversing the direction of benefit.
- **Why Correct:** `友達に教えてもらった` properly positions the friend with particle `に` as the person who performed the teaching favor for the speaker.
- **Example:** 友達に教えてもらった。 (Tôi được bạn bè dạy cho.)

Grammar | 〜を中心に

- **Meaning:** Center around, focus on / Lấy... làm trung tâm; tập trung vào....
- **Trigger:** Specifying the core, chief entity, or main focus around which activities or events revolve.
- **Contrast:** `〜を中心に` identifies the central focus or leader of an activity, whereas `〜をはじめ` highlights a primary representative example in a list.
- **One-line Summary:** Identifies the core subject or geographic focus of an activity.
- **Formation:** N + を中心に / N + を中心とする + N.
- **Semantic Field:** Focus & Core Target / Trung tâm & Trọng tâm.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜をはじめ`, `〜を対象に`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `〜をはじめ` presents Tokyo as one example among many, missing the explicit focus that Tokyo is the main central hub of activities.
- **Why Correct:** `東京を中心に活動する` correctly indicates that Tokyo serves as the central base of operations.
- **Example:** 東京を中心に活動する。 (Hoạt động chủ yếu ở Tokyo.)

Grammar | 〜をはじめ

- **Meaning:** Including, starting with / Bao gồm; kể cả; lấy... làm đầu.
- **Trigger:** Giving a primary, most prominent example to represent a larger group or category.
- **Contrast:** `〜をはじめ` lists a prominent leading example followed by others, whereas `〜を中心に` marks the operational center or core hub.
- **One-line Summary:** Introduces a prominent leading example representing a whole class.
- **Formation:** N + をはじめ / N + をはじめとする + N.
- **Semantic Field:** Representative Example & Inclusion / Ví dụ đại diện & Liệt kê.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜を中心に`, `〜などの`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using simple `など` lacks the prominent emphasis of placing Japan at the head of the list of countries.
- **Why Correct:** `日本をはじめ多くの国` singles out Japan as the primary representative country among many.
- **Example:** 日本をはじめ多くの国。 (Bắt đầu từ Nhật Bản, rất nhiều quốc gia.)

Grammar | 〜にすぎない

- **Meaning:** Nothing more than, merely / Chỉ là... mà thôi; không hơn gì....
- **Trigger:** Minimizing or downplaying the scale, importance, or value of a fact or statement.
- **Contrast:** `〜にすぎない` downplays importance ("merely/nothing more than"), whereas `〜にちがいない` expresses strong conviction ("must be").
- **One-line Summary:** Downplays something as being merely or nothing more than a minor thing.
- **Formation:** N / V-plain + にすぎない.
- **Semantic Field:** Minimization & Limitation / Giới hạn & Mức độ thấp.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜だけだ`, `〜にほかならない`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `〜にちがいない` asserts that it must definitely be a rumor, contradicting the modest downplaying nuance ("it is merely a rumor").
- **Why Correct:** `うわさにすぎない` correctly minimizes the statement as being nothing more than an unverified rumor.
- **Example:** うわさにすぎない。 (Chỉ là tin đồn mà thôi.)

Grammar | 〜というより

- **Meaning:** Rather than, more like / Hơn là...; đúng hơn là....
- **Trigger:** Correcting or refining a description to choose a more accurate characterization over an initial one.
- **Contrast:** `〜というより` compares two labels to select the more precise one, whereas `〜に対して` contrasts two opposing entities.
- **One-line Summary:** Refines a description by stating a more accurate term.
- **Formation:** N / Adj / V-plain + というより.
- **Semantic Field:** Qualification & Exact Description / Điều chỉnh & Mô tả chính xác.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜というか`, `〜よりは`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using simple comparison `〜より` compares physical entities, whereas `〜というより` compares the suitability of two descriptive words ("friend" vs "teacher").
- **Why Correct:** `先生というより友達だ` accurately conveys that "friend" is a more fitting description than "teacher".
- **Example:** 先生というより友達だ。 (Đúng hơn là bạn bè chứ không đơn thuần là thầy giáo.)

Grammar | 〜といっても

- **Meaning:** Although it is said that, even though / Mặc dù nói là... nhưng...; dù có nói là....
- **Trigger:** Qualifying a statement to prevent overestimation or misunderstanding by the listener.
- **Contrast:** `〜といっても` softens a term by clarifying its actual modest scale, whereas `〜にもかかわらず` expresses strong obstacle/concession.
- **One-line Summary:** Qualifies a statement to clarify that the reality is less impressive than the label suggests.
- **Formation:** N / V-plain + といっても.
- **Semantic Field:** Concession & Qualification / Nhượng bộ & Làm rõ thực tế.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜とは言え`, `〜けれども`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `〜としても` means "even assuming that...", which creates a hypothetical condition rather than qualifying an actual existing status ("even though I am called a student...").
- **Why Correct:** `学生といっても社会人だ` correctly acknowledges being a student while clarifying that in reality he is also a working adult.
- **Example:** 学生といっても社会人だ。 (Dù nói là học sinh nhưng cũng đã là người đi làm.)

Grammar | 〜として

- **Meaning:** As, in the capacity of / Với tư cách là...; như....
- **Trigger:** Specifying a role, capacity, status, title, or standpoint under which an action is performed.
- **Contrast:** `〜として` marks role or official status, whereas `〜にしては` expresses an unexpected contrast based on a standard.
- **One-line Summary:** Marks the official capacity, role, or title under which an action takes place.
- **Formation:** N + として / N + としての + N.
- **Semantic Field:** Role & Capacity / Tư cách & Vai trò.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜としては`, `〜にとっても`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `〜にしては` implies an evaluation contrary to expectations ("for a teacher..."), which contradicts simply stating one's job position.
- **Why Correct:** `教師として働く` properly indicates working in the official capacity or profession of a teacher.
- **Example:** 教師として働く。 (Làm việc với tư cách là giáo viên.)

Grammar | 〜としても

- **Meaning:** Even if, even assuming that / Dù cho...; ngay cả khi... đi nữa.
- **Trigger:** Presenting a hypothetical or admitted condition that will not alter the main conclusion.
- **Contrast:** `〜としても` sets up a hypothetical concession ("even if X happens, Y remains true"), whereas `〜といっても` qualifies a real label.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses that a conclusion holds true even under a hypothetical or opposing condition.
- **Formation:** V-plain / Adj / N + だ + としても.
- **Semantic Field:** Hypothetical Concession / Nhượng bộ giả định.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜にしても`, `〜たとえ...ても`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `〜といっても` qualifies a real statement ("although it is said..."), which does not fit an opposing hypothetical stance ("even if opposed").
- **Why Correct:** `反対だとしても行く` correctly states the firm resolve to go even under the hypothetical situation of facing opposition.
- **Example:** 反対だとしても行く。 (Ngay cả khi bị phản đối thì tôi vẫn đi.)

Grammar | 〜とともに

- **Meaning:** Together with / At the same time as / Cùng với...; đồng thời....
- **Trigger:** Expressing accompanying accompaniment with a person/group, or simultaneous parallel progression of two events.
- **Contrast:** `〜とともに` indicates mutual accompaniment or simultaneous parallel change, whereas `〜にしたがって` emphasizes proportional sequential change.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses accompaniment with someone or two events occurring simultaneously.
- **Formation:** N + とともに; V-ru + とともに.
- **Semantic Field:** Accompaniment & Simultaneity / Cùng nhau & Đồng thời.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜と一緒に`, `〜に伴って`.
- **Confusion Index:** Medium.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `〜に対して` expresses opposing contrast or direction, which contradicts living peacefully alongside family.
- **Why Correct:** `家族とともに暮らす` properly expresses living in accompaniment together with one's family.
- **Example:** 家族とともに暮らす。 (Sống cùng với gia đình.)

Grammar | 〜にしたがって

- **Meaning:** As, in accordance with / Theo...; cùng với....
- **Trigger:** Proportional change where as one factor changes or advances, another factor changes correspondingly.
- **Contrast:** `〜にしたがって` indicates step-by-step proportional progression or following rules, whereas `〜として` marks role/capacity.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses that as one factor progresses, another changes proportionally.
- **Formation:** V-ru + にしたがって; N + にしたがって.
- **Semantic Field:** Proportional Change & Compliance / Biến đổi tỷ lệ & Tuân theo.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜につれて`, `〜に伴って`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `〜として` incorrectly frames aging as a role/capacity rather than a progressive continuous factor.
- **Why Correct:** `年を取るにしたがって` correctly captures the natural proportional changes that accompany growing older.
- **Example:** 年を取るにしたがって。 (Cùng với việc tuổi tác tăng lên.)

Grammar | 〜につれて

- **Meaning:** As, with / Theo...; càng... càng....
- **Trigger:** One-directional natural progression where an automatic change unfolds in tandem with another changing process.
- **Contrast:** `〜につれて` is restricted to continuous natural scale changes (weather, age, growth), whereas `〜にしたがって` can also express compliance with rules/instructions.
- **One-line Summary:** Expresses natural continuous change unfolding in tandem with another factor.
- **Formation:** V-ru + につれて; N + につれて.
- **Semantic Field:** Natural Proportional Progression / Biến đổi tự nhiên theo tiến trình.
- **Related Grammar:** `〜にしたがって`, `〜とともに`.
- **Confusion Index:** High.
- **Why Wrong:** Option using `〜において` specifies a location/domain ("in/at"), failing to express the dynamic temporal warming trend.
- **Why Correct:** `暖かくなるにつれて` properly links the gradual progression of weather becoming warmer with secondary seasonal changes.
- **Example:** 暖かくなるにつれて。 (Càng ấm lên thì...)

Weak Points

1. **Nhầm lẫn Cấu trúc Phỏng đoán & Truyền đạt (****〜みたいだ** **vs** **〜らしい** **vs** **〜そうだ（様態）** **vs** **〜そうだ（伝聞）****):**
    - Dễ chia sai dạng kết hợp (stem + そうだ vs plain + そうだ) hoặc nhầm lẫn giữa phỏng đoán so sánh trực quan (`みたいだ`) và tin đồn chứng cứ khách quan (`らしい`).
2. **Nhầm lẫn Trạng thái Ngoại động từ vs Chuẩn bị (****〜ておく** **vs** **〜てある****):**
    - Chưa phân biệt rõ giữa ngoại động từ chỉ trạng thái chuẩn bị sẵn có mục đích (`てある`) và hành động chuẩn bị trước diễn ra trong hiện tại (`ておく`).
3. **Dùng nhầm Nhóm Cấu trúc Nhượng bộ & Giả định (****〜といっても** **vs** **〜としても****):**
    - Dễ nhầm lẫn giữa việc đính chính thực tế khiêm tốn của một danh xưng (`といっても`) và một giả định nhượng bộ trái ngược ("ngay cả khi... đi nữa") (`としても`).
4. **Nhầm lẫn Nhóm Cấu trúc Biến đổi Tiến trình Tỷ lệ (****〜にしたがって** **vs** **〜につれて****):**
    - Rất dễ nhầm lẫn hai cấu trúc biến đổi tỷ lệ kéo theo, đặc biệt trong các câu diễn tả sự biến đổi tự nhiên của thời tiết hay tuổi tác.
